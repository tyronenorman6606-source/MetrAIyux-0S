VantaCore service CRM lane

VantaCore is now a 0S-owned service CRM lane, not a loose import.

The usable CRM workspace lives under FS27 with inherited 0S/Free99/FS27 auth, persisted leads, bookings, follow-ups, reviews, revenue summary, and activity routes. This 0S page explains ownership and routes operators into the actual FS27-owned workspace.

What this adds

The 0S already has CRM pieces. VantaCore adds the service-business operator pack.

Lead Firewall

Cold callers, spam, vendors, emergencies, and qualified leads are split before they hit the owner.

Booking + Follow-Up

Calendar-aware appointment handling, missed-call text-back recovery, quote nudges, and no-show recovery are mapped as the VantaCore lane.

Review + Revenue

Review requests, sentiment routing, lead value, revenue protected, and customer-memory summaries are kept in the service CRM context.

White-Label Portal

Agency/reseller and client portal posture is now owned by the 0S/FS27 gate instead of treated as standalone SaaS drift.

Already covered by 0S

This is not replacing the core system.

NEXUS already handles CRM-style records and operator ledgers. ConnectLog + Relay13 already covers relationship memory and owned messaging. SkyeMail covers mailbox proof. SkyePay and FS27 cover payment and activation authority. VantaCore is useful when the customer problem is a service-business intake operator: answer, filter, book, follow up, recover, route, review, and measure revenue leakage.

Ownership boundary

  • Dashboard and API routes inherit 0S, Free99, FS27, or SkyGate authority; VantaCore has no separate login.
  • Tenant writes fail closed unless tenant identity is resolved.
  • SkyePay is the allowed payment control lane; mock payments are local only.
  • Twilio, Resend, Google Calendar, SkyePay/Stripe, storage, NorthStar handoff, and rollback receipts are selected and dry-run proven through FS27 provider receipts; public review routing stays private-feedback-first until a real review URL is configured.
