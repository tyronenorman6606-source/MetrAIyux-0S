Launch checklist

Do this for every client before calling the app done.

Public App

Logo intro, full app, obvious download button, QR-ready URL, and working mobile scroll.

Lead Capture

Forms post into FS27 and write lead, contact, activity, campaign, source URL, and workspace slug.

Workspace Thread

Relay13 receives the request in the client's own workspace so the owner has a dedicated inbox lane.

ConnectLog Record

ConnectLog mirrors the request as a relationship event for follow-up, proof, and later reporting.

What to tell the owner

Their customers can use the app. The owner can answer or route.

Direct Route

Customer asks for owner attention. Thread is held for human/operator handling.

Local Brain Route

The default brain classifies the request, keeps cost at zero, and leaves the reply under owner control.

Paid AI Draft Route

Starter drafts 125 replies plus 31 backup messages. Response Plus drafts 425 plus 76. Both remain owner-reviewed after SkyePay activation.

Managed AI Inbox

Managed starts at 1,000 messages plus 222 backup and actually runs the inbox: triage, labels, allowlisted routine replies, timers, summaries, and escalation.

ConnectLog Route

Sales, follow-up, vendor, or client relationship threads become cards and request records.

Gate Route

SkyeGate/SkyeMerit tracks account code, billing posture, and proof lane.
