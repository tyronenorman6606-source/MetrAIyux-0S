3. Work the thread

4. Control AI cost

The local brain handles routing by default. AI response lanes unlock only after the paid SkyePay add-on is active.

AI response lanes

Three paid lanes turn on AI help without changing the lead pipeline.

Lead capture, workspace messages, and ConnectLog persistence do not require AI provider calls. Starter is $35/mo with 125 messages plus a 31-message backup bucket. Response Plus is $79/mo with 425 plus 76. Managed AI Inbox starts at $149/mo with 1,000 plus 222 and actually manages the inbox: triage, labels, allowlisted routine replies, follow-up timers, summaries, and human escalation.

Cost guardrail

No AI provider call is made unless the workspace has an active paid package. After the included and backup buckets are exhausted, Relay13 hard-stops provider calls while FS27, Relay13, and ConnectLog still capture the request for local/manual handling.
