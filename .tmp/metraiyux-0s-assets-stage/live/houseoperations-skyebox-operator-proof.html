Boundary

SkyeBox is local-first

It does not create a cloud account, sync secrets to a server, recover forgotten passwords, or replace enterprise password-manager policy.

HouseOperations is static-local here

The folder owns its local state and static endpoints. Cloud persistence requires a future Worker/D1/KV scope.

Billing is explicit

HouseOperations creates a billing intent and SkyePay offer URL. It does not mark itself paid; FS27/SkyePay owns checkout, owner approval, and policy activation.
