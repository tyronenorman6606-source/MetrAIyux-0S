Compliance Posture

Staffing requires disciplined operations, not loose promises.

This page explains how Skyes Over London Staffing thinks about documentation, worker readiness, client agreements, privacy, government claims, and quality control.

Important

Not legal advice.

This page describes operating posture. Actual compliance requirements should be reviewed with qualified professionals.

Verified Claims

Government identifiers, certifications, insurance, bonding, contract vehicles, and past performance must be verified before public use.

Candidate Data

Sensitive candidate documents should use secure collection and storage, not unsecured public forms.

Client Agreements

Staffing services should be controlled by written terms covering pricing, payment, conversions, replacements, and responsibilities.

Quality Control

Start checks, issue logs, feedback loops, and replacement workflows protect client outcomes.

Worker Readiness

Assignments should include clear start instructions, supervisor contact, dress/PPE requirements, shift expectations, and reporting rules.

Procurement Discipline

Government and prime opportunities should go through go/no-go review before proposal work begins.
