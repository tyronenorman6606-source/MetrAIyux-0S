Contract Vehicles & Buyer Paths

Explain how buyers may purchase staffing support.

This page is an educational procurement surface. It does not claim Skyes Over London Staffing holds any contract vehicle unless verified.

Buyer Education

No false vehicle claims.

Use this to discuss possible purchase paths: direct commercial agreement, subcontracting, municipal quote, agency procurement, or future vehicles.

Commercial Agreement

Private employers can engage through a reviewed staffing agreement, quote, or statement of work.

Prime Subcontract

Prime contractors can subcontract staffing support when the scope, compliance flow-downs, and pricing are defined.

Municipal Quote

Local governments may request quotes or proposals under their own procurement rules.

Agency Solicitation

Public agencies may issue RFQs, RFPs, sources-sought notices, or bid opportunities.

Set-Aside Path

Set-aside eligibility must be certified and verified. Do not claim status until real.

Future Vehicle

GSA Schedule, cooperative purchasing, and other vehicles may be future paths, not current claims unless awarded.
