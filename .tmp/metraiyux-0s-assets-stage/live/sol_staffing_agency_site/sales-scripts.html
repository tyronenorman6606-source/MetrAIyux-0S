Sales Scripts

AE outreach scripts for staffing accounts.

Use these scripts for cold calls, email outreach, LinkedIn, follow-up, government/prime inquiries, and job-order qualification.

AE Enablement

Scripts reduce drift.

Keep claims tight, gather the right information, and push every conversation toward a next action.

Cold Call Opener

“Hey, this is [Name] with Skyes Over London Staffing. We help companies cover temporary, temp-to-hire, project, and direct-hire roles. Are you currently hiring or dealing with any staffing gaps?”

Follow-Up Email

Subject: Staffing support for [Company]

Hi [Name], following up on staffing support for [role/department]. If you send headcount, shift, pay range, start date, and requirements, we can confirm the best staffing model and next steps.

Prime Contractor Opener

“We support primes with staffing, administrative support, contact support, and project workforce coordination. Is there a current subcontracting lane or upcoming solicitation where local labor coverage is needed?”

Job Order Close

“To move this from interest to action, I need role title, headcount, shift, location, pay range, start date, requirements, decision maker, and onboarding steps. Can we capture that now?”
