# Government Contracting Readiness Checklist
Skyes Over London Staffing

This checklist is internal/operator-facing. Do not paste it into a public page unless you remove notes that are not client-facing.

## Verified public claims only

Do not claim any of the following until real:
- Active SAM.gov registration
- UEI
- CAGE code
- SBA certification
- 8(a), SDVOSB, WOSB, HUBZone, DBE, MBE, or other set-aside status
- GSA Schedule / MAS holder status
- Past federal performance
- Bonding capacity
- Insurance coverage levels
- Security clearance or facility clearance

## Registration package

- Exact legal entity name
- DBA / public brand name
- Business address
- Taxpayer information
- Bank information
- Entity administrator email
- Business POC
- Government POC
- Electronic business POC
- NAICS candidates
- PSC candidates if needed
- Capability statement
- Business website
- Company email
- Insurance documentation
- Payroll / worker classification process
- Subcontractor and vendor agreements

## Contract pursuit workflow

1. Capture solicitation or prime opportunity.
2. Confirm scope, NAICS, due date, period of performance, location, and role types.
3. Check whether the business can actually perform the work.
4. Identify staffing source plan.
5. Confirm pricing model, payroll exposure, insurance, compliance obligations, and reporting.
6. Draft quote/proposal.
7. Owner reviews before submission.
8. Submit only after claims and attachments are verified.
9. Log submission and follow-up date.
10. If awarded, create delivery checklist and quality-control log.
