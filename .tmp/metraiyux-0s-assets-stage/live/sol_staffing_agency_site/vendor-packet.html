Vendor / Subcontractor Packet

Control partner and subcontractor onboarding before work begins.

Use this page when another vendor, subcontractor, or partner is involved in staffing delivery, recruiting support, payroll support, field operations, or government/primes.

Risk Control

Document the relationship.

Do not let vendors operate without written scope, responsibility boundaries, compliance review, and approval.

Partner Identity

  • Legal entity
  • DBA
  • Primary contact
  • Address
  • W-9 if needed
  • Insurance if required

Scope

  • What they provide
  • What they cannot provide
  • Service territory
  • Staffing category
  • Start/end dates
  • Reporting process

Controls

  • Agreement status
  • Confidentiality
  • Worker classification
  • Data handling
  • Payment terms
  • Quality escalation
