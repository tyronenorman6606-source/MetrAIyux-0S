Internal Sales Surface

AE Command Center for staffing accounts.

Use this page to train AEs, pitch employers, qualify job orders, and keep the staffing offer consistent. This page should be internal or password-protected before serious production.

AE Objective

Open employer accounts.

Find businesses with hiring pain, qualify their need, gather the job order, submit the lead, then follow up until placement or disqualification.

Call Script

Simple employer opener.

Opening: “Hey, this is [AE Name] with Skyes Over London Staffing. We help companies fill temporary, temp-to-hire, project, and direct-hire roles without relying only on job posts. Are you currently hiring or dealing with staffing gaps?”

If yes: “What roles are hardest to fill right now, and how soon do you need people in place?”

If not now: “Understood. Who handles hiring when you do need extra staff? I can send over our staffing overview and stay available when demand spikes.”

Job Order Questions

  • Role title and duties
  • Headcount needed
  • Location and schedule
  • Pay range or bill-rate target
  • Start date and urgency
  • Required skills, tools, licenses, or experience
  • Temporary, temp-to-hire, direct-hire, or project

Decision Questions

  • Who approves staffing vendors?
  • What vendor paperwork is required?
  • What caused the current hiring need?
  • What happens if this role stays unfilled?
  • What would make a candidate an immediate yes?
  • What disqualifies a candidate?

Government / Prime Pursuit

AE rules for government contracting leads.

Do not promise certifications, contract vehicles, set-aside status, insurance levels, clearance, or past federal performance unless ownership has verified it.

Ask for the contract facts

  • Agency, prime, or buyer name
  • Solicitation number or opportunity link
  • NAICS and scope description
  • Due date and question deadline
  • Role types, headcount, location, schedule, and period of performance
  • Required insurance, background checks, wage determinations, and reporting

Use safe positioning

  • “We can review the scope and confirm fit.”
  • “We can support staffing and workforce coordination lanes.”
  • “We will verify registration, certifications, and compliance items before proposal submission.”
  • “Send the solicitation or subcontracting package so we can respond accurately.”
