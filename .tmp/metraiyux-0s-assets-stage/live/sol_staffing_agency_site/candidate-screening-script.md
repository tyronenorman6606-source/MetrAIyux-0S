# Candidate Screening Script

## Opening
Thanks for applying with Skyes Over London Staffing. I’m going to confirm your availability, work history, location fit, and role preferences so we can understand what assignments may fit.

## Questions
- What city/area are you in?
- How far can you commute?
- What shifts can you work?
- When can you start?
- What pay range are you looking for?
- What roles have you done before?
- What tools, software, or equipment have you used?
- Do you have certifications or licenses?
- Are there physical requirements you cannot do?
- Is there anything that would stop you from accepting quickly?

## Close
We cannot guarantee placement, but clear information helps us match you faster when a fitting role is available.
