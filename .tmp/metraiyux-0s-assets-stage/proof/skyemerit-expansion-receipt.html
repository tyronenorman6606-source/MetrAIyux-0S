Commercial Boundary

Free99 means no charge to receive the pack.

It does not mean anonymous access, uncapped coupons, cash payout, or a way around owner-approved activation. SkyeMerit lowers only eligible spend bands, records metadata for SkyePay, and keeps the gate in control.
