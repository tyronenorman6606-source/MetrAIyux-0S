Boundary

Do not over-sell the scan.

This receipt proves local folder inventory, route wiring, and smoke-ready proof paths. It does not prove production provider execution, ad-platform spend, Stripe checkout, customer tenancy, external storage, or live publishing for this suite. Those require separate owner approval, secrets configured in the right environment, and a fresh browser/API proof receipt.
