Overlap review

Why keep this if the 0S already has related systems?

Keep VantaCore only as the service-business operator lane: lead firewall, missed-call recovery, booking/follow-up, review routing, revenue intelligence, and white-label service CRM packaging. Do not use it to duplicate ConnectLog + Relay13 messaging, NEXUS records, SkyeMail mailbox, SkyePay billing, RouteX/HouseOps dispatch, or FS27 auth. Those remain the parent systems.

Activation rule

Customer-impacting provider writes stay explicit: the selected stack is configured and dry-run proven through FS27 receipts, while public review routing remains private-feedback-first until a real Google/Skye Reviews destination URL is configured.
