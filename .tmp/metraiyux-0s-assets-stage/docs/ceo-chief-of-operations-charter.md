# Chief Executive Officer / Chief of Operations — 13-Cabinet Office Charter

This demonstrative charter establishes the Chief Executive Officer / Chief of Operations as the central executive office responsible for strategic execution, cabinet coordination, operational discipline, revenue alignment, client success, staffing oversight, financial review, technology readiness, compliance routing, quality assurance, vendor coordination, and expansion planning.

The office operates subject to the authority of the founder, owner, managing member, board of directors, board of managers, executive committee, or other governing body. It does not replace legal, tax, accounting, insurance, financial, or other licensed professional review.

## Purpose

The purpose of the office is to provide unified leadership over the 13-cabinet structure and prevent operational fragmentation. The office turns approved strategic direction into measurable execution.

## 13 Cabinets

1. Executive Command Cabinet — vision, ownership direction, strategic doctrine, executive authority.
2. Operations Cabinet — SOPs, daily execution, project management, internal discipline.
3. Sales & AE Cabinet — account executives, pipeline, proposals, close support.
4. Client Success Cabinet — onboarding, retention, escalation, relationship management.
5. Finance & Accounting Cabinet — budgets, billing, payroll coordination, margin visibility.
6. Legal & Compliance Cabinet — contract routing, policy files, risk registers, compliance workflows.
7. HR & Staffing Cabinet — recruiting, onboarding, job orders, workforce planning.
8. Technology & Systems Cabinet — websites, dashboards, automation, AI tools, data workflows.
9. Marketing & Brand Cabinet — messaging, campaigns, SEO, sales collateral, capability statements.
10. Government & Enterprise Contracting Cabinet — registrations, procurement readiness, enterprise support.
11. Partnerships & Vendor Relations Cabinet — vendors, referral partners, subcontractors, alliances.
12. Quality Assurance & Performance Cabinet — audits, KPIs, proof systems, corrective action.
13. Expansion & Innovation Cabinet — new markets, AI strategy, pilots, growth experiments.

## Operating Standard

Each cabinet should maintain a defined scope, assigned owner, active priorities, measurable outputs, documented workflows, and escalation procedures.

## Filing Safety

For actual incorporation or official records, use only real names, real appointments, accurate authority descriptions, and reviewed legal language.
