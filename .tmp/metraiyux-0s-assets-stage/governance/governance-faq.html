Governance FAQ

What is this for?

This governance center gives the company a formal operating vocabulary before incorporation or restructuring. It helps explain who owns which lane and what must be documented.

Can these names be filed?

Only when the people, consent, titles, and authority are verified. Planning roles belong in planning material.

Does this replace an attorney?

No. It is a structured draft package for review, not legal advice.
