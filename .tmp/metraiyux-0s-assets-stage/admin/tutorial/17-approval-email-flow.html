17. Approval Email Flow From Command to Decision

How the Main Automation Brain turns risky commands into Resend approval emails and operator decisions.

Lesson steps

Command intake

Admin submits a command in the Main Automation Brain chat or a portal intake form.

Risk classification

The brain classifies the command as safe local work, approval-gated work, or blocked/escalated work.

Cabinet routing

The primary brain drafts the action and the secondary reviewer checks risk, proof, and public-facing claims.

Email creation

The Worker creates an approval record, builds the Resend email, and sends it to ADMIN_APPROVAL_EMAIL.

Decision capture

The admin opens the approval inbox, approves or rejects the action, and the Worker logs the decision.

Operator checklist

  • ADMIN_APPROVAL_EMAIL set
  • RESEND_API_KEY saved as Worker secret
  • PUBLIC_ADMIN_URL points to the deployed admin URL
  • Approval inbox tested with a sample social draft

Proof rule

Do not mark this lesson complete until the related page, endpoint, token, or connector has been opened and tested. Local receipts are acceptable for demo mode; live claims require Worker/D1/provider proof.
