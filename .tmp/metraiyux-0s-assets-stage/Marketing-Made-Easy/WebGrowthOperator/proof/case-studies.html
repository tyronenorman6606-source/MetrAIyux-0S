Case studies

Case studies with proof standards.

The Skyes Over London LC case study format for documenting problems, work shipped, evidence, measurable outcomes, and next-step recommendations without unsupported claims.

1. Client problem: What was broken, missing, slow, confusing, or leaking leads.
2. Work shipped: Pages, systems, assets, campaigns, workflows, and reporting completed.
3. Evidence: Screenshots, form tests, analytics, call records, review activity, campaign notes, or client approval.
4. Result: What changed and how it was measured. Unknowns stay labeled unknown.
5. Next move: The logical upsell or operational fix after the first result.
No fake case studies: the page is structured so approved case studies can be added later without rewriting the site. Until a result is documented, the site explains the proof standard instead of inventing wins.
