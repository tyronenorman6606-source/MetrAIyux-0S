Sales scripts
for every service lane.

Use these scripts as starting points. Keep the delivery natural. The strongest opener is always one specific observation about the business, followed by one clean diagnostic question.

Script

Local business cold call opener

Use when the business has a visible local presence and likely revenue leaks.

Hi, this is [NAME] with Skyes Over London LC. I am not calling to sell you a random website. I was looking at how local customers would find and contact your business, and I noticed a few places where leads could be leaking before they ever reach you. The quick question is: are you already tracking how many calls, form requests, reviews, and follow-ups your website and Google profile are actually producing each month?
Script

Website build opener

Use when the site is weak, outdated, missing, or hard to use on mobile.

I looked at your online presence from the customer side. The business may be solid, but the website does not make the offer as clear as it could. A visitor should know what you do, why to trust you, how to contact you, and what to do next within the first few seconds. Skyes Over London builds that front door, hosts it, and keeps it current so the business does not look abandoned after launch. Would it be worth a quick intake to see what the build would actually need?
Script

Growth ops opener

Use when the business already has a site but lacks a system.

Most businesses do not only have a website problem. They have a follow-up problem, a review problem, a profile problem, a content problem, or a missed-call problem. What we do is connect the website to the growth systems around it: reviews, Google Business Profile updates, paid traffic landing pages, missed-call recovery, CRM follow-up, and reporting. If I showed you the exact leaks we would fix first, would you be open to reviewing that?
Script

Paid traffic opener

Use only when they run ads or show intent to run ads.

If you are already paying for ads, the issue is usually not just the campaign. It is whether the offer, landing page, tracking, calls, forms, and follow-up are working together. Skyes Over London manages the campaign lane around the full lead path so ad spend is not disconnected from the website and follow-up process. Are you currently separating ad spend from management and tracking which leads are actually turning into booked opportunities?
Script

Review engine opener

Use when reviews are low, old, or inconsistent.

A lot of good businesses have customers who would leave reviews, but nobody asks at the right time or gives them an easy path. We install a clean review request flow, QR assets, testimonial capture, and private feedback routing so happy customers are easier to turn into public trust. Are you currently asking customers for reviews in a consistent way, or is it random?
Script

Missed-call recovery opener

Use for service businesses, retail/service hybrids, clinics, contractors, salons, shops.

The fastest revenue leak for a local business is a call that comes in while the team is busy. If that person does not get a fast response, they usually call the next option. We set up missed-call text-back, booking links, callback alerts, and simple lead recovery so fewer opportunities die unanswered. Do you know how many business calls are missed in a normal week?
Script

Walk-in / drop-by opener

Use in person when approaching a physical location.

I will keep this quick. I help businesses clean up the online side: website, Google profile, reviews, follow-up, and lead recovery. I noticed a couple of things that could make it easier for people to find you, trust you, and contact you. Who normally handles the website, Google profile, or marketing decisions here?
Script

DM opener

Use for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or direct business messages.

Hey [NAME], I came across [BUSINESS] and noticed a few simple online growth leaks that could be cleaned up: website clarity, Google profile trust, review flow, and follow-up path. I run Skyes Over London LC. We build and operate the web presence around those systems, not just a one-time page. Want me to send a quick breakdown of what I would fix first?
Script

Voicemail

Keep voicemail under 25 seconds.

Hi [NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] with Skyes Over London LC. I had a quick note about [BUSINESS] and a few online areas that may be costing calls or quote requests before they reach you. I will send a short message as well. If this is worth reviewing, you can reach me at (986) 210-7040 or B2B@solenterprises.org.
Script

Audit handoff

Use after reviewing their current presence.

I did not want to send a generic pitch, so I put the opportunity into a simple order: first, the digital front door; second, the trust layer; third, the lead recovery system; fourth, the follow-up/reporting layer. The first move I would recommend is [PACKAGE] because [REASON]. After that, the natural next layer is [UPSELL] because [REASON].
