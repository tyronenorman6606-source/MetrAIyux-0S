Missed-Call + Lead Recovery

Missed calls and quote requests get routed into a faster response path with text-back, booking links, callback alerts, and a simple recovery process.

Start IntakeView Pricing

Who this is for

  • Businesses that miss calls during work hours or after hours.
  • Service companies buying ads or relying on phone leads.
  • Owners who need lead rescue without hiring a full front desk.

What the client receives

  • Missed-call text-back flow
  • Booking or quote link
  • Lead alert routing
  • Callback script
  • Tested sample flow
  • Monthly missed-lead report where data is available

What must be clear

  • No cold texting, spam campaigns, or sensitive regulated workflows without custom scope.
  • SMS use must follow opt-in, consent, and provider rules. Tool and messaging costs are separate unless bundled.
