Missed-Call + Lead Recovery
fulfillment playbook.

Missed calls and quote requests get routed into a faster response path with text-back, booking links, callback alerts, and a simple recovery process.

Public service pageIntake formPricing

Deliverables owed

  • Missed-call text-back flow
  • Booking or quote link
  • Lead alert routing
  • Callback script
  • Tested sample flow
  • Monthly missed-lead report where data is available

Access / inputs to collect

  • Phone/provider access or approved tracking number path.
  • Business hours, callback rules, staff contacts, and service area.
  • Booking link, quote form fields, or calendar preference.

Scope controls

  • No cold texting, spam campaigns, or sensitive regulated workflows without custom scope.
  • SMS use must follow opt-in, consent, and provider rules. Tool and messaging costs are separate unless bundled.

Step-by-step SOP

Map the missed-call scenario.
Choose the tool/provider path.
Configure auto-response and alerts.
Connect quote or booking destination.
Test the missed-call flow.
Train owner/staff and report monthly recovery opportunities.

QA before marking complete

  • Confirm each purchased deliverable is either shipped, blocked by client input, or custom-quoted.
  • Check the relevant live path: links, forms, profile changes, ad settings, lead routes, CRM steps, or report data.
  • Document screenshots, links, notes, or test outcomes in the client record.
  • Send the completion or monthly status note with next recommended action.

Report template

  • Missed calls where trackable
  • Auto-responses triggered
  • Replies/bookings where trackable
  • Open lead leaks
  • Script improvements
