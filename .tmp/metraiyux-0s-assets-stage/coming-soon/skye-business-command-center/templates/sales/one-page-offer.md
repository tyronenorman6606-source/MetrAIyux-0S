# Skye Business Command Center

A branded business operations portal for companies that need cleaner follow-up, faster quoting, better customer tracking, and fewer missed messages.

## Includes
- CRM and lead pipeline.
- Shared support inbox and tickets.
- Quote/intake/onboarding forms.
- Estimate and invoice setup.
- Branded dashboard.
- Hosting and monthly support options.

## Pricing
Starter: $497 setup + $99/month.
Business: $997 setup + $199/month.
Ops Pro: $1,997+ setup + $399+/month.

Contractors, service companies, consultants, agencies, mobile services, staffing teams, real estate vendors, logistics companies, med spas, cleaning companies, and appointment-based businesses.
