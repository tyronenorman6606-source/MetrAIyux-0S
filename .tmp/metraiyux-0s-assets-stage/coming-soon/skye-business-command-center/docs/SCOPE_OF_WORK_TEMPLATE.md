# Scope of Work Template

## Project
Skye Business Command Center deployment for [Client Name].

## Included
- Branded hub configuration.
- CRM setup with starter pipeline.
- Shared inbox/support desk setup.
- Intake/quote/onboarding form setup.
- Invoice/estimate configuration.
- DNS and HTTPS setup guidance.
- Admin account setup.
- Basic staff walkthrough.
- Backup script configuration.
- Launch smoke and acceptance checks.
- Client handoff document.

## Not included unless written separately
- Legal advice.
- Tax/accounting advice.
- Payment processing or merchant services.
- Banking, money movement, or PayFac services.
- HIPAA/PCI/SOC2 certification.
- Guaranteed search rankings.
- Custom software development outside the agreed scope.
- Data entry or migration beyond the agreed number of records.

## Monthly support
Monthly support covers reasonable maintenance, uptime checks, backups review, minor configuration support, and issue triage. Major new features, migrations, custom integrations, or emergency recovery outside normal support may be billed separately.
