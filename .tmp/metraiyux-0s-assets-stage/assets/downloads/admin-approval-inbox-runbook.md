# Admin Approval Inbox Runbook

1. Open `/admin/approval-inbox.html`.
2. Confirm Worker endpoint and ADMIN_TOKEN are set in the browser session.
3. Load the Worker ledger.
4. Review the command, primary brain, secondary brain, and risk lane.
5. Approve, reject, or mark needs revision.
6. Keep export receipts for proof.

Approval emails are alerts. They do not approve the action by themselves.
