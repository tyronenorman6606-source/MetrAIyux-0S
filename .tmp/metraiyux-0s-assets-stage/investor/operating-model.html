How the cabinet model works

The cabinet model divides the company into clear functional lanes while keeping executive command centralized. The Chief of Operations coordinates daily movement, each cabinet owns a business function, and the proof layer checks whether claims match actual execution. This turns the company into an organized operating system rather than a collection of disconnected services.
