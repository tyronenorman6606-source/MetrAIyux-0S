# Saleable Product Positioning

## Best price point

This is most believable as a $2.99/month personal utility for an individual doctor, resident, clinic owner, or medical office operator who wants a private workflow helper outside of a heavy enterprise platform.

## The sellable angle

"A private local workflow vault for the doctor who wants a cleaner way to track follow-ups, packets, notes, referrals, labs, meds, and day-to-day office work without sending data to another cloud service."

## What makes it worth referring

- It is simple enough to understand in minutes.
- It has 13 useful workflow surfaces.
- It makes local storage and backups visible instead of vague.
- It exports portable JSON/CSV.
- It has receipts and restore points.
- It avoids the trust-killer of pretending to be an enterprise EHR.

## What not to claim

Do not claim:

- HIPAA certified.
- EHR replacement.
- Billing system replacement.
- Cloud-secure by default.
- Multi-user enterprise clinic suite.
- Medical decision support.

## Stronger public copy

"Doctor Ops Personal Vault helps doctors organize the small operational loops that get lost between visits: lab follow-ups, prior-auth notes, referral routing, medication reconciliation, visit prep, discharge packets, consent tracking, care gaps, chronic care touchpoints, and handoffs. Your data stays local by default, and backups/exports stay under your control."
