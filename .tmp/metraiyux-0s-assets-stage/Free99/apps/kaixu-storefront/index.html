The attached bundles do not behave like random experiments. They read like a connected operating stack: AI infrastructure, sovereign storage, repo control, storefront delivery, multi-rail fulfillment, and an economy layer designed to sit on its own dedicated gateway.

The point of a founder editorial mini site is not to mumble vague buzzwords and hope the lights are flattering. The point is to show what each system does, why it exists, and how the pieces compound when they are operated together. That is what lives here.

The attached product set

kAIxU Brain with Skye Errors, SkyeDrivePro, SkyeGit, kAIxU Storefront Suite, the Commercial Closeout Bundle, and the Skye Economy Command Layer all solve different operational problems, but they solve them in the same language: Cloudflare-native control, branded delivery, admin visibility, and systems that behave like infrastructure rather than one-off pages.

Why these systems matter together

The real value is not just that each product does a job. It is that they can reinforce one another without turning into platform spaghetti in a cheap trench coat.

  • AI and admin visibility

    kAIxU Brain is not just model routing. It carries smoke coverage, admin routes, and structured error reporting so runtime problems become visible instead of mysterious.

  • Storage and editing sovereignty

    SkyeDrivePro keeps files, metadata, sync, and local persistence under your control, then bridges editable files into a richer native document surface.

  • Codebase control

    SkyeGit pushes toward a repo host that owns policy, indexing, usage, and org controls instead of surrendering that authority to somebody else’s platform.

  • Commercial delivery

    The storefront and closeout bundle take real ZIP products from seeded assets to checkout, licensing, claim URLs, and fulfilled delivery.

  • Economy architecture

    The economy layer separates commerce, currency, treasury, and gateway responsibilities into dedicated brains so growth does not collapse into one bloated worker.
