Some bundles are just folders in a trench coat. This one has a clearer job: shorten the distance between a working storefront and the assets that storefront is meant to sell.

In This Release OverviewWhat Is IncludedWhy It MattersWho It Fits

Overview

The Commercial Closeout Bundle is the packaging layer that turns the storefront from a sales surface into a cleaner release artifact.

The bundle includes the storefront repo plus four sellable product ZIPs: the Gateway-Only Pack, B2B SaaS Core Pack, Enterprise Readiness Pack, and Finance Control Pack. That means the operator does not just receive a commerce engine. The operator receives the seeded commercial payloads that engine is already built to deliver.

What is included

Storefront repo

The storefront bundle already includes the public-facing product pages, checkout routes, portal, admin surface, claim handling, and worker-based fulfillment logic.

Gateway-Only pack

A sellable seeded pack positioned for buyers who want the gateway lane without the larger surrounding stack.

B2B SaaS Core pack

A seeded product pack aimed at buyers who need a more developed commercial software foundation.

Enterprise + Finance packs

Two more seeded product lanes extend the storefront into readiness, control, and stronger commercial positioning instead of a single-note offer.

The release notes make the operating expectation clear: the product ZIPs are included in the bundle and should be uploaded into the bound R2 bucket so delivery can happen through the storefront’s claim and fulfillment surfaces.

Why it matters

Packaging matters more than people admit. A storefront with no seeded product assets is half-dressed. A pile of product files with no sales and fulfillment surface is equally goofy. This bundle solves that mismatch by shipping both layers together.

Release gets stronger the moment the buyer-facing surface and the delivered payload stop pretending they are separate conversations.
Commercial Closeout Bundle Positioning

Commercial value

Bundled release packaging reduces handoff friction, shortens go-live work, and makes the product story easier to explain to buyers. That is not glamorous, but it is exactly the kind of unsexy leverage that makes a commercial stack easier to operate.

Who it fits

This bundle fits operators who want to move faster from “we have code” to “we have a release-ready commercial package.” It is especially useful for bundle-based digital product sales, closeout releases, and structured handoff deals where the storefront and the product payload need to arrive together.

Need a tighter release package for digital products?

The Commercial Closeout Bundle is built for that moment when the storefront and the shipped payload need to land as one coherent product story.
