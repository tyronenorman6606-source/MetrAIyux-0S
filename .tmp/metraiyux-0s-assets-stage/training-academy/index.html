Teaching sequence

I made the system teach by moving, showing, doing, and recording.

Open the right room

Explain the room

Each step tells the user why the room exists, what risk it controls, and what outcome it supports.

Highlight the control

Practice the workflow

Export proof

Training ends with a saved local record or export, so handoff has evidence instead of verbal memory.
