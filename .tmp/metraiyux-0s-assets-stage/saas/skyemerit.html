SkyeMerit Wallet

Merits lower eligible SkyePay spend without opening the gate.

SkyeMerit is a Free99 customer feature: no charge to receive the pack, no anonymous access, and no escape from the gate. First-time users get a $6 premium kAIxu model spend credit plus protected discount codes that only apply inside the eligible spend band.

First-time pack

$6 premium kAIxu credit

Every first-time signup or onboarding record receives a SkyeMerit pack. The kAIxu credit is usage credit, not a cash payout.

Protected coupons

23%, 28%, and 31%

The first-time code auto-selects the best eligible rule and caps the discounted spend, so large tickets do not discount the whole enterprise scope.

Gate policy

Auth stays on

Free99 and SkyeMerit do not remove FS27, SkyGate, client login, or local admin gate requirements.
