Tenant Provisioning

Production provisioning path.

This is the operator-facing checklist for turning a signup into a real customer workspace. Tenant provisioning, white-label resale, branded portals, custom domains, and buyer-owned platform mirroring are paid Enterprise / Managed Gate or owner-approved quote scope, not Free99.

  1. Customer submits signup and selects a plan; Free99 preview users do not receive tenant/resale provisioning.
  2. Billing intent is created. In production, checkout, invoice, or written Enterprise / Managed Gate approval must be confirmed before tenant provisioning.
  3. Cloudflare Worker creates customer and workspace records in D1.
  4. Workspace services are inserted into workspace_services table.
  5. Provisioning task enters Queue for async setup.
  6. Resend sends approval/setup email to owner/admin.
  7. Customer dashboard becomes available behind upstream auth.
  8. Site Operator Brain routes first command to the correct cabinet brain.

Provisioning boundary

Free99 can open gated app surfaces, but it cannot provision a buyer's resale SaaS. Custom tenant policies, AI caps, hosted custody, external provider routes, and branded workspace cloning must be written into the paid plan or quote before this checklist runs.
