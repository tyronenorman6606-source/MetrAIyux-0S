Access boundary

Free99 opens the door; paid entitlements cover cost-bearing work.

The overall 0S can let people see and use useful surfaces without leaving them out. The line is cost: anything that burns AI, provider APIs, hosted custody, messaging, identity, payments, route providers, or tenant resale capacity must be tied to a paid plan, top-up, or written owner-approved quote.
