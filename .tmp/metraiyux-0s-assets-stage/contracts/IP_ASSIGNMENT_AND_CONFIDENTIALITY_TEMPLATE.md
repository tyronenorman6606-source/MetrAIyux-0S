# IP Assignment And Confidentiality Template

Counsel-review draft. Do not use without review and signer-specific facts.

## 1. Parties

- Company: `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`
- Contributor: `[NAME / ENTITY]`
- Effective date: `[DATE]`

## 2. Covered Work

Covered Work includes code, designs, UI, brand assets, documentation, prompts, workflows, automations, connector logic, deployment scripts, data schemas, templates, training materials, media, product names, and derivative work created for or delivered to the Company.

## 3. Assignment

Contributor assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in Covered Work to the fullest extent permitted by law, including copyrights, trade secrets, moral-right waivers where permitted, derivative rights, and rights to register, enforce, and license the work.

## 4. Pre-Existing Materials

Contributor must identify any pre-existing materials, open-source components, third-party assets, AI-generated assets, stock assets, fonts, model outputs, or licensed tools included in the work.

## 5. Confidentiality

Contributor must protect non-public source code, credentials, customer data, strategy, prompts, workflows, pricing, business plans, security details, product roadmaps, legal templates, and buyer information.

## 6. No Credential Retention

Contributor must return or destroy credentials, secrets, API keys, tokens, customer data, and internal records at project end or on request. Contributor may not reuse credentials or keep hidden access.

## 7. Warranty Requests For Counsel

Counsel should decide what representations are appropriate regarding originality, non-infringement, authority to assign, open-source compliance, AI-generated materials, and third-party permissions.

## 8. Signature Evidence

Store:

- Signed assignment.
- Contributor legal name and contact.
- Scope of work or invoice reference.
- Repository or deliverable list.
- Third-party asset disclosure.
- Date and signer authority.
