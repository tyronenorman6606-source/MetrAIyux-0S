# Operating Agreement Counsel Brief

This is a structure brief for counsel. It is not an operating agreement and should not be signed as-is.

## Purpose

MetrAIyux needs a formal operating agreement or equivalent governance document before serious licensing deals. The agreement should show who owns the company or platform IP, who can sign deals, how money moves, and how founder authority is preserved.

## Clauses Counsel Should Draft Or Review

- Entity name, formation state, principal office, and registered/statutory agent.
- Member and manager names using verified legal identities.
- Ownership percentages or membership units.
- Capital contributions and future contribution rules.
- Manager/member authority and limitations.
- Who may sign platform licenses, reseller agreements, enterprise contracts, provider agreements, and IP assignments.
- IP ownership and assignment to the entity or holding company.
- Confidentiality and trade-secret obligations.
- Restrictions on transfers, competing use, and unauthorized sublicensing.
- Profit/loss allocations and distributions.
- Tax matters and tax-professional coordination.
- Books, records, bank accounts, audits, and approval thresholds.
- Dispute resolution, deadlock handling, buyout, resignation, dissolution, and winding up.
- Founder reserve rights, if desired.

## Evidence To Store

- Final signed operating agreement.
- Written signer authority.
- Member/manager consents or resolutions.
- Amendments.
- Counsel memo on authority to license MetrAIyux IP.

## MetrAIyux-Specific Questions

- Should MetrAIyux IP be owned by the operating entity or by a holding entity that licenses it to operating companies?
- Should Skyes Over London LC own the brand or only publish the public legal hub?
- Should each white-label deployment use a separate order form under one master license?
- What audit rights are needed to verify usage, sublicense restrictions, and revenue share?
