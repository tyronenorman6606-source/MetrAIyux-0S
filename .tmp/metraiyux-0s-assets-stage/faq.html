Executive Cabinet FAQ

Is this a staffing agency?

It can support staffing, but the structure is broader. The 13-cabinet office presents staffing as one controlled service lane inside a larger operations, technology, client success, finance, compliance, sales, and quality assurance model.

Are the cabinet executives real people?

The roster is demonstrative unless real people are legally appointed. Use real names and verified resumes for incorporation, filings, official officer records, payroll, or regulated representations.

What is the local brain?

The local brain is a lightweight browser-side knowledge assistant. It is not a heavy AI model. It retrieves answers from included site content, resumes, service pages, blogs, and cabinet docs. Optional model endpoints can be connected later.

Can this be used for government contracting?

It can be used as a readiness presentation framework. Actual government submissions require accurate registrations, verified company details, compliance review, and real capability evidence.

What makes the model sellable?

The company is positioned around accountable execution, clear cabinet ownership, public-facing service pages, AE sales assets, client intake, readiness documentation, and proof-centered operating standards.
