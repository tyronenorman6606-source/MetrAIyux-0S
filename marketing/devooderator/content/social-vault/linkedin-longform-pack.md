That is the world MetrAIyux 0S is being built inside.

The 0S is not just a collection of apps. It is a gated operating surface with shared auth, worker routing, proof receipts, SkyeVault custody, MCP tooling, and agent lanes that can actually touch the work. The proof layer matters too. I want receipts. I want smoke checks. I want deploy records. I want plain-language boundaries when browser proof was owner-handled instead of pretending a test passed.

That is founder discipline to me.

Not perfect-tech promises.

## 5. Shared auth is not glamorous. It is the reason the 0S can stay sane.

One of the most important rules in the 0S is also one of the least flashy:

Mounted apps do not get their own little founder passwords.

That sounds like a small implementation detail until you have a system with many surfaces, owner routes, client rooms, proof endpoints, admin tools, and agent-operated pages.

If every mounted app invents its own admin lane, the platform becomes a pile of keys. It gets harder to reason about. Harder to rotate. Harder to audit. Easier to leak. Easier to misconfigure. Easier to forget.

So the 0S uses the shared FS27/SkyGate/Free99 auth lane owned by the main Worker.

Owner surfaces forward the shared gate credential through accepted headers and session helpers. Mounted app API routes are expected to rely on the shared gate/operator auth helpers, not a private password hiding in an app folder.

That is not just security hygiene.

It is product architecture.

The Worker is default-deny. Public entrypoints stay tiny. New 0S surfaces have to pass through the gate before assets or proxied APIs render. Unauthenticated requests should redirect to the owner login lane. Authenticated requests should render normally.

That is how a platform grows without turning into a maze.

I care about this because AI agents move fast. Founder energy moves fast. Marketing wants new pages. Product wants new apps. Operators want quick access. Clients want clean handoff.

The gate is what keeps speed from becoming chaos.

I do not need another secret. I need one shared owner trust lane that the platform understands.

That is a boring sentence.

It is also the kind of boring that keeps the company alive.

Which claims are forbidden?

For the DevodeRator Social Vault update, the ownership lane is narrow on purpose.

Keep the existing vault content.

Do not edit social.html.

Do not edit style.css.

That is how I want AI inside the 0S.
