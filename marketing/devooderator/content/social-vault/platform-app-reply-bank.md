### What is this?

Because small businesses do not all need the same door. The point of the 0S is to keep shared auth, proof, recovery, and operator control under one system while each app gets a clear job.

## Objection Replies

### This sounds too big.
That is why I am posting it app by app. You do not need the whole system to understand one useful lane.

### Why not just use existing tools?
Use existing tools when they solve the job. I am building the 0S for the gaps between tools: shared auth, recovery posture, proof trails, client handoff, and operator control.

### Is this for enterprise only?
No. The architecture can grow, but the first-dollar lane matters most: documents, pages, vaults, content, local apps, and practical workflows for real operators.

### Is it secure?
I do not sell perfect-security slogans. The posture is shared gate, default-deny routing where mounted, public-safe proof, and no app-specific admin password sprawl.

### What should I start with?
Start with the lane closest to your pain: SkyeVault for recovery, SovereignDocs/SkyeDocxMax for docs, SkyeWebCreatorMax/WebGrowthOperator for web/growth, SkyeMusicNexus for artists, or Client App Factory for local apps.
