1. Devil's advocate haircut: what I actually discounted.

  • Zero customer revenue: not a technicality. The Stripe account has charges_enabled: true and details_submitted: true (confirmed in skyenet-live-production-stress-latest.json), but I saw no receipt of a real external customer completing a cs_live checkout. The billing plumbing is live; the customers are not. A market buyer discounts hard for this.
  • Provider-gated surfaces: SkyeNet Functions is "controlled preview" per the valuation brief — arbitrary isolated execution is the enterprise unlock, not the current product. SkyeMail's Zoho integration proved at the API/Worker level but full customer workspace proof has a stated boundary. Any surface requiring a customer's connected Resend/Twilio/Zoho account is a boundary, not a feature, until proven otherwise.
  • Scope breadth without ICP clarity: 41 priced surfaces across the registry, 875+ HTML pages, 17 operating brains. A diligence buyer will ask "which 3–5 of these does your ICP actually buy first?" The answer is probably clear to the founder; it needs to be clear in 5 minutes to an investor.
  • Founder dependency: every architecture decision, proof convention, naming scheme, and product direction flows through one operator. The IP is in the repo; the operator context is not yet institutionalized. That is a real risk premium a buyer applies.
  • No external validation: no customers, no press coverage, no pilot agreements, no advisory board. The proof discipline is internal and genuine — it is not the same as market validation.

6. Risks and diligence gaps.

  • No ARR proof: the biggest single gap. Every other number is derived, not observed from customers. A buyer's first request will be "show me the last three months of subscription revenue."
  • SkyeNet Functions execution boundary: the "controlled preview / Netlify-compatible function intake" language in the brief is honest but is a gap. The isolated runtime for arbitrary customer code is the enterprise unlock, not the current product.
  • SkyeMail send-path proof: I confirmed the Worker routes are up. I did not confirm that outbound emails from a real customer address reach a real inbox through Zoho in the current session's proof receipts. The Zoho smoke was listed in the artifact directory but I did not confirm its specific contents.
  • 17-brain command mesh: the mesh is documented and the 725 KB chunks are referenced. A technical buyer will want to evaluate whether the brain routing is deterministic and auditable at scale, not just functional in smoke tests.
  • Contractor payout execution: the brief states that real-money payout execution stays approval-gated. That is intentional and correct, but it means the "contractor payment readiness" lane is an approval UI, not a live payout flow. Worth flagging in diligence.
  • Proof receipt freshness: the receipts I confirmed were generated on 2026-05-23 and 2026-05-24. A buyer will run these scripts live before signing.

7. Plain-English conclusion.

MetrAIyux 0S is a live, deployed, proof-disciplined prerevenue platform. That is a real category and it has a real value. That does not mean the higher numbers are wrong. It means they require external customer validation to defend in a serious diligence conversation.
