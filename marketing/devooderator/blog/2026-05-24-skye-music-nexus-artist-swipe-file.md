## 7-Day Launch Sprint

```text
Day 1: Post the release story and send 20 direct DMs.
Day 2: Post a hook clip and ask fans to comment their favorite line.
Day 3: Send the blog/podcast/playlist pitch to 25 targets.
Day 4: Drop a behind-the-scenes clip or lyric breakdown.
Day 5: Offer a supporter bundle or paid download.
Day 6: Ask 10 fans for screenshot proof, reactions, or testimonials.
Day 7: Recap the week, thank supporters, and tease the next drop.
```
