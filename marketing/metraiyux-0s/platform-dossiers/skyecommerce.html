Competitor stack

How it stacks up without lying.

The comparison is category-level on purpose. Specialist tools are not bad; they are often excellent at the narrow job. The 0S argument is ownership, proof, shared auth, payment activation, and cross-lane context.

Shopify The commerce heavyweight. SkyeCommerce is stronger when commerce must live inside the 0S with docs, apps, proof, and owner-controlled payments.
WooCommerce Flexible plugin commerce. SkyeCommerce reduces plugin sprawl by making ops lanes first-party.
Medusa / Saleor Developer commerce frameworks. SkyeCommerce is a working 0S lane, not just a backend starter.
