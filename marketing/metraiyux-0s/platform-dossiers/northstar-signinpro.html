Competitor stack

How it stacks up without lying.

The comparison is category-level on purpose. Specialist tools are not bad; they are often excellent at the narrow job. The 0S argument is ownership, proof, shared auth, payment activation, and cross-lane context.

Auth0 / Clerk Excellent auth platforms. NorthStar is the 0S-owned workflow gate, not a general auth vendor.
Firebase Auth Fast app auth. NorthStar focuses on shared platform sessions.
App-local passwords Simple at first. NorthStar avoids long-term credential sprawl.
