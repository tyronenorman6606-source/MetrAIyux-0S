Competitor stack

How it stacks up without lying.

The comparison is category-level on purpose. Specialist tools are not bad; they are often excellent at the narrow job. The 0S argument is ownership, proof, shared auth, payment activation, and cross-lane context.

Stripe Checkout The core payment rail. SkyePay adds 0S offer mapping, approval gates, and internal product truth.
Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy Simple sales surfaces. SkyePay is stronger when checkout must activate a private platform workspace.
Shopify payments Great commerce checkout. SkyePay is built for 0S platform products, not only carts.

What backs the page.

Live store

SkyePay is the payment and activation lane for 0S platform offers. The live store connects checkout to offer IDs, owner approval, limits, and follow-up activation instead of treating payment as a disconnected receipt.

SkyeNet paid ladder

The SkyeNet offer mapping includes starter hosting, growth hosting, managed functions, and sovereign runtime reserve lanes. That lets a free Bob-style pilot become a natural upgrade path without changing the story after the owner says yes.

Bob free pilot to paid activation

Bob's app proves the free value first: live app, workspace handoff, QR flyer, and Relay13 chat. Paid activation can add custom domain support, more capacity, managed edits, backup, AI response add-ons, and stronger owner reporting.
