# Legal Action Items for Skyes Over London / SOLEnterprises

This package is drop-ready, but legal protection also requires operational setup.

## High-priority counsel review
- Confirm exact legal entity name, DBA names, state of formation, and governing law.
- Add official business mailing address to commercial email footers before running commercial email campaigns.
- Register or confirm registered agent and service-of-process handling.
- Review arbitration/class-action waiver enforceability for target states and customer types.
- Review privacy obligations for every jurisdiction where users or customers operate.
- Confirm whether CCPA/CPRA, GDPR/UK GDPR, COPPA, GLBA/Safeguards Rule, HIPAA, employment, credit, insurance, logistics, marketplace, or tax laws apply to specific product lanes.
- Designate a DMCA agent with the U.S. Copyright Office if hosting user-generated content at scale and seeking DMCA safe harbor.
- Build consent and opt-out ledgers for SMS, calls, email, cookies, and marketing.
- Build signed order forms / statements of work for enterprise and client-service deals.
- Build contractor IP assignment and confidentiality signature flow.
- Build creator/artist payout and tax onboarding before media monetization.

## Product controls to wire
- Consent capture logs: timestamp, URL, IP/user agent where appropriate, form text version, phone/email, source.
- STOP/HELP processing and suppression list.
- Refund/cancellation ledger.
- AI disclosure banner for chatbots and generated content.
- Human review gates for high-risk AI outputs.
- Abuse reporting inbox and takedown workflow.
- DMCA notice/counter-notice workflow.
- Security incident log and key rotation procedure.
- Data export/delete request workflow.
- Vendor/subprocessor register.
- Commercial email footer injection with physical mailing address.
