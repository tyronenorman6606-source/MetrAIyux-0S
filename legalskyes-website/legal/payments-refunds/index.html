Payment, Refund, and Chargeback Policy

Payment obligations, no-refund defaults, subscriptions, chargebacks, setup fees, and usage-based charges.

Effective date: May 3, 2026. Governing law baseline: Arizona, United States. Contact: legal-skyes@solenterprises.org.

Payment obligation

Fees are due according to the checkout, invoice, order form, subscription plan, statement of work, or signed agreement. Access, delivery, setup, licensing, and implementation may be conditioned on payment.

No-refund baseline

Unless a signed agreement or required law says otherwise, payments for setup, implementation, custom work, digital delivery, consulting, audits, AI credits, telecom usage, design work, deployment, and completed services are non-refundable.

Committee refund review

Customers should contact support before initiating a chargeback. Where an unhappy-customer dispute is accepted into the in-house committee lane and no signed agreement or mandatory law controls a different remedy, discretionary refund proposals are capped at up to 67% of the purchase amount after documented overhead. The case record should show purchase amount, overhead, eligible refund base, cap, proposed amount, reason, decision date, and committee reviewers.

See In-House Arbitration and Dispute Committee.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions renew until cancelled. Cancellation stops future renewal but does not automatically refund past charges, current billing periods, used credits, telecom fees, AI usage, or completed work.

Usage fees

Usage-based charges may include AI tokens, calls, texts, email volume, storage, bandwidth, compute, provider fees, verification, data processing, logs, route data, or overages. Usage fees are generally non-refundable once incurred.

Chargebacks

Unauthorized chargebacks, payment reversals, or payment disputes may result in suspension, collections, loss of access, recovery fees, legal fees, and termination. Contact support before initiating a chargeback.

Taxes

Customer is responsible for taxes, duties, levies, and government charges except taxes based on our net income.

Late payment

Late amounts may result in suspension, interest where allowed, collections, and recovery of reasonable costs of collection, including attorney fees where permitted.

Contact and legal notices

Questions, legal notices, privacy requests, intellectual-property notices, and support requests may be sent to legal-skyes@solenterprises.org. Include the applicable service, account, order, URL, content, and facts needed to investigate.
