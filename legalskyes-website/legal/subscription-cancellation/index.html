Renewal

Subscriptions automatically renew at the interval selected unless cancelled before renewal through the available account controls or by written support request where account controls are unavailable.

Cancellation timing

Cancellation prevents future renewal. It does not erase unpaid invoices, usage fees, existing obligations, or charges already incurred.

Downgrades

Downgrades may reduce features, seats, limits, storage, automations, integrations, support, and available history. Customer is responsible for exporting data before downgrading if features may be lost.

AI and communication credits

Credits may expire, be plan-specific, be non-transferable, and be usable only for eligible services. Credits have no cash value unless required by law.

Account closure

After termination or cancellation, we may retain, archive, or delete account data according to legal, backup, security, dispute, and operational retention needs.

Enterprise plans

Enterprise, implementation, agency, white-label, or custom plans may be governed by separate signed terms and may have minimum commitments.

Contact and legal notices

Questions, legal notices, privacy requests, intellectual-property notices, and support requests may be sent to grayskyes@solenterprises.org. Include the applicable service, account, order, URL, content, and facts needed to investigate.
