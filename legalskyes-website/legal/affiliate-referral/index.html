Affiliate, Referral, and Revenue Share Terms

Referral payouts, revenue shares, tracking, disclosures, clawbacks, and marketing compliance.

Effective date: May 3, 2026. Governing law baseline: Arizona, United States. Contact: grayskyes@solenterprises.org.

Program scope

Affiliate, referral, partner, contractor, creator, reseller, or revenue-share programs must be accepted by us in writing or through an approved platform workflow.

Disclosure

Participants must clearly disclose material connections, paid relationships, affiliate links, sponsorships, referral compensation, free products, or incentives in marketing and endorsements.

No deceptive claims

Participants may not make false, misleading, unsubstantiated, income-guarantee, performance-guarantee, legal, medical, financial, or product claims.

Payout eligibility

Payouts require valid tracking, qualified revenue, no refund, no chargeback, no fraud, no self-referral abuse, account verification, tax information, and compliance with program rules.

Clawbacks

We may reverse, offset, hold, or claw back payouts for refunds, chargebacks, fraud, policy violations, cancelled accounts, duplicate referrals, or invalid traffic.

No agency

Participants are independent contractors and may not bind the Company, sign contracts for us, collect payments outside approved channels, or represent themselves as employees unless expressly authorized.

Contact and legal notices

Questions, legal notices, privacy requests, intellectual-property notices, and support requests may be sent to grayskyes@solenterprises.org. Include the applicable service, account, order, URL, content, and facts needed to investigate.
