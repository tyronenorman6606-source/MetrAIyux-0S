Accessibility Statement

Accessibility commitment, reporting pathway, and reasonable accommodation language.

Effective date: May 3, 2026. Governing law baseline: Arizona, United States. Contact: grayskyes@solenterprises.org.

Commitment

We aim to make public-facing digital experiences reasonably accessible and usable for visitors, customers, and users.

Continuous improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Some beta, experimental, third-party, legacy, or embedded content may not fully conform to every guideline at all times.

Feedback

Accessibility barriers can be reported to grayskyes@solenterprises.org. Include the page, issue, assistive technology used if relevant, and preferred contact method.

Alternatives

Where practical, we will provide reasonable alternative access to information or support if a feature is not accessible.

Contact and legal notices

Questions, legal notices, privacy requests, intellectual-property notices, and support requests may be sent to grayskyes@solenterprises.org. Include the applicable service, account, order, URL, content, and facts needed to investigate.
