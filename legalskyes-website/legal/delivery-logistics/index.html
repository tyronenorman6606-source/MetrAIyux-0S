Delivery, Dispatch, and Logistics Terms

Terms for Routex-style dispatch, delivery coordination, proof of delivery, offline navigation, and routing workflows.

Coordination role

Delivery, dispatch, routing, offline navigation, proof-of-delivery, and logistics tools may help coordinate activity but do not replace licensed carriers, driver judgment, safety obligations, insurance, permits, or legal compliance.

Driver and merchant responsibility

Drivers, contractors, merchants, and logistics partners are responsible for vehicle safety, licensing, insurance, road laws, cargo legality, package handling, delivery confirmation, customer communication, and safe operation.

Route data

Routes, maps, ETAs, geofences, sensor data, and delivery instructions may be inaccurate, outdated, incomplete, blocked, or unavailable. Users must obey road signs, laws, closures, and safety conditions over app instructions.

Proof of delivery

Photos, signatures, timestamps, location data, notes, scans, and confirmations may be used for operational proof but may not conclusively resolve all disputes. Fraud, technical errors, poor connectivity, or user error may affect records.

No emergency use

Logistics tools are not emergency dispatch, public safety, medical transport, law-enforcement, aviation, maritime, or life-critical systems.

Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, we are not responsible for accidents, injuries, traffic violations, cargo loss, theft, vehicle damage, missed windows, unsafe stops, third-party carrier errors, or user failure to follow safety requirements.

Questions, legal notices, privacy requests, intellectual-property notices, and support requests may be sent to grayskyes@solenterprises.org. Include the applicable service, account, order, URL, content, and facts needed to investigate.
