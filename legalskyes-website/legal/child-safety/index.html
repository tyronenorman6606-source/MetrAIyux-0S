Children and Minor Safety Policy

COPPA-aware restrictions, under-13 prohibition, guardian requirements, and content safety.

Effective date: May 3, 2026. Governing law baseline: Arizona, United States. Contact: grayskyes@solenterprises.org.

Not directed to children

Services are not directed to children under 13 and should not be used by children under 13 without a specific signed compliance arrangement and verifiable parental consent where required.

Teen users

Users under the age of majority must use the services only with parent or guardian permission and only where the service permits minor access.

No collection from children

Do not use our services to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 unless expressly authorized through a signed agreement and compliant workflow.

Child safety content

Content involving child exploitation, sexualization of minors, grooming, trafficking, abuse, or endangerment is prohibited and may be reported to authorities.

Parent requests

Parents or guardians may contact grayskyes@solenterprises.org to request review or deletion of information submitted by a minor, subject to verification and legal requirements.

Contact and legal notices

Questions, legal notices, privacy requests, intellectual-property notices, and support requests may be sent to grayskyes@solenterprises.org. Include the applicable service, account, order, URL, content, and facts needed to investigate.
