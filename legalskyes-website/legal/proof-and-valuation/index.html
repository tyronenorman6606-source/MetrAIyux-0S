Current valuation bands

The current Codex full-repo engineering valuation treats MetrAIyux 0S as a multi-SaaS operating system, not as a single app. The current published bands are:

Proof basis

The source valuation pass was based on repo inventory, route inventory, proof receipts, smoke/stress/e2e command results, Worker configuration, and platform evidence. Key proof markers included 102,469 workspace files scanned outside major caches, 91,854 tracked repo files, 11,087,013 tracked text lines, 23,282 local HTML pages crawled, 2,617 regenerated SkyWay route entries, 173 root npm scripts, and a large JSON proof corpus under test artifacts.

Primary internal evidence paths include metraiyux_0s_site/data/valuation-source-of-truth.json, test-artifacts/codex-valuation-audit/full-repo-inventory.json, test-artifacts/codex-valuation-audit/proof-receipt-index.json, test-artifacts/codex-valuation-audit/command-runs.json, test-artifacts/codex-valuation-audit/skye-crawler-static-report.json, test-artifacts/codex-valuation-audit/skyeway-route-atlas.json, metraiyux_0s_site/wrangler.toml, and metraiyux_0s_site/cloudflare/worker.js.

Live stress and documentation posture

The 0S is not being opened as SaaS on vibes alone. The system is stressed, smoke-tested, route-mapped, and documented before broader SaaS release. The proof story includes SkyeMusicNexus workflow stress, SkyeRouteX runtime/smoke/stress/e2e passes, SkyeNet deployment/function proof, Free99 mounted-app proof, SkyeMerit math proof, Marketing Made Easy smoke, static crawling, and route-atlas regeneration.

Those receipts do not mean nothing can break. They mean the platform has a documented operating record, a repeatable proof discipline, and a visible maintenance trail for issues found during testing.

No 100% success guarantee

Skyes Over London LC cannot guarantee 100% success for every result, automation, launch, customer workflow, AI output, payment route, provider connection, deploy, proof run, or business outcome. Nobody honestly could for a system this layered. The 0S has millions of moving parts, several layers of intelligence, several layers inside the 0S itself, and the FS27/SkyGate/Free99 gate that controls the lane before customer surfaces open.

Proof artifacts, valuation ranges, smoke tests, stress tests, live routes, screenshots, logs, and deployment receipts are operational evidence. They are not guarantees of legal compliance, investment outcome, approval by third parties, uninterrupted service, revenue, rankings, deliverability, platform acceptance, customer conversion, or perfect model output.
