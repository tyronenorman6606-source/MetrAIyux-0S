Vendor and Independent Contractor Terms

Terms for collaborators, contractors, media reps, implementation partners, drivers, developers, and vendors.

Effective date: May 3, 2026. Governing law baseline: Arizona, United States. Contact: grayskyes@solenterprises.org.

Independent contractor status

Vendors and contractors are independent contractors and are not employees, partners, franchisees, agents, or joint venturers unless a signed agreement says otherwise.

Work quality

Contractors must perform work professionally, lawfully, safely, and according to the applicable scope, deadlines, confidentiality requirements, brand standards, and technical requirements.

Confidentiality

Contractors must protect non-public business, technical, customer, pricing, security, credential, source code, AI, product, and strategy information.

IP assignment

Unless a signed agreement says otherwise, contractor work made for the Company is assigned to the Company upon payment, including all rights needed to use, modify, commercialize, license, and enforce it.

No unauthorized commitments

Contractors may not make warranties, promises, discounts, legal statements, employment offers, payment commitments, public statements, or platform commitments on behalf of the Company without written authority.

Compliance

Contractors are responsible for taxes, licenses, insurance, equipment, background requirements where applicable, safety, data protection, and compliance with applicable laws.

Contact and legal notices

Questions, legal notices, privacy requests, intellectual-property notices, and support requests may be sent to grayskyes@solenterprises.org. Include the applicable service, account, order, URL, content, and facts needed to investigate.
