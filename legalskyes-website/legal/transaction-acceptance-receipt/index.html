Stored fields

A transaction acceptance receipt should store legal pack version, acceptance timestamp, acceptance surface, source URL, customer email, order ID, offer ID, amount, currency, selected product, no-outcome-guarantee acknowledgement, privacy acknowledgement, truthful-review/non-waiver acknowledgement, and payment/refund policy acknowledgement.

Boundaries

Acceptance records document the terms shown or linked at checkout. They do not erase non-waivable rights, truthful lawful reviews, mandatory consumer protections, payment-network rights, regulator reports, law-enforcement reports, or any required neutral external arbitration process.
