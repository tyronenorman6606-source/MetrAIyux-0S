Creator content

Creators, artists, media representatives, and users retain ownership of eligible content they upload. They grant limited service licenses needed to operate, display, preview, package, sell, support, and promote the content through the services, unless a signed agreement says something different.

Music ownership boundary

SkyeMusicNexus, Media Over London, Skyes Over London, and SOLEnterprises do not own an artist's music, masters, compositions, royalties, distribution rights, publishing rights, licensing rights, or uploaded media by default. We are not a music distributor, label, publisher, royalty collector, or licensing administrator unless a separate signed agreement and provider/legal lane expressly creates that role.

Artist media may be retained or transformed only as needed for hosting, preview playback, proof, fulfillment, support, takedown review, marketing of the artist/platform surface, or other agreed service purposes. We do not keep or reuse artist media for unrelated company profit outside the agreed service lane unless the artist grants that right in writing.

Music Nexus readiness

The Music Nexus Artist Readiness page explains how artist intake can be used for generated artist websites, press kits, storefronts, release drops, artist-brain memory, rights and split notes, payout paperwork, takedown holds, shared 0S gate access, and SkyeNet publishing review.

Artists should use Music Nexus signup to begin onboarding, artist storefronts to review public pages, and the rights page before public playback, publishing, or payout release. This readiness tooling is not formal legal advice.

Rights required

You must own or have all rights, releases, samples, publishing rights, performance rights, synchronization rights, artwork rights, likeness rights, trademark rights, and permissions needed for content you upload or monetize.

Revenue shares

Revenue shares, platform fees, referral payouts, payout eligibility, reserves, chargebacks, taxes, processor fees, royalties, and distribution income are governed by the applicable creator agreement, management agreement, checkout terms, payout policy, or product disclosure. No royalty share applies unless a signed agreement says it applies.

Payout eligibility

Payouts may require account verification, tax forms, fraud screening, minimum thresholds, completion of onboarding, active account status, and absence of unresolved disputes or infringement claims.

Content moderation

We may remove, restrict, demonetize, delist, or disable content that appears illegal, infringing, abusive, deceptive, unsafe, low-quality, fraudulent, or inconsistent with platform standards.

No promotion guarantee

We do not guarantee streams, downloads, sales, playlist placement, rankings, audience growth, sponsorship, press coverage, or income.

Name and likeness

You grant us permission to use public-facing names, usernames, logos, artwork, previews, descriptions, snippets, and likenesses you provide to operate and promote the platform unless a signed agreement says otherwise.

Contact and legal notices

Questions, legal notices, privacy requests, intellectual-property notices, and support requests may be sent to legal-skyes@solenterprises.org. Include the applicable service, account, order, URL, content, and facts needed to investigate.
