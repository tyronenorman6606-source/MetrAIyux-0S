Important

Legal protection is a system, not a magic sentence.

These pages reduce exposure by publishing contract terms, user rules, disclaimers, consent language, privacy rights, DMCA/IP handling, communications controls, AI limitations, dispute routing, and product-specific risk allocation. They do not make any company impossible to sue, and they should be reviewed by counsel before high-volume commerce, telecom, finance, healthcare, employment, child-directed, or international expansion.
