Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed by Skyes Over London LC through websites, apps, forms, SMS, email, AI tools, SaaS products, client services, creator programs, commerce workflows, logistics workflows, developer tools, support, and business operations.

We may collect identifiers, contact information, account details, authentication information, billing and transaction information, support messages, communication preferences, device and log data, usage data, form submissions, files, media, prompts, outputs, project data, route or delivery information, creator profile data, business records, and information you authorize through integrations.

We do not claim ownership of customer content solely because it is processed by our AI features. Depending on your location and relationship with us, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, restrict, object, opt out of certain sharing or targeted advertising, withdraw consent, or appeal a privacy decision. Requests can be sent to the contact email listed on this site.

Retention
