Order documents

Enterprise services are governed by a signed order form, statement of work, proposal, invoice, purchase order accepted by us, or master services agreement.

Priority

Signed enterprise documents control over website terms for the specific enterprise engagement if there is a conflict.

Confidentiality

Each party may receive confidential information and must use reasonable care to protect it, use it only for the business relationship, and restrict disclosure to personnel and providers with a need to know.

Implementation dependencies

Customer must provide timely access, credentials, data, DNS, provider accounts, technical contacts, approvals, legal reviews, and business decisions needed for implementation.

Change control

Changes to scope, timelines, deliverables, integrations, provider costs, infrastructure, compliance posture, or user volume may require a change order and additional fees.

Risk allocation

Enterprise pricing reflects the risk allocation in these terms. Customer-specific indemnities, unlimited liability, regulated commitments, security addenda, or custom warranties require signed approval and adjusted pricing.

Audit and proof artifacts

Proof artifacts, smoke tests, logs, reports, valuations, or status documents are operational evidence, not guarantees of legal compliance, revenue, investor outcome, platform approval, or uninterrupted service.

Contact and legal notices

Questions, legal notices, privacy requests, intellectual-property notices, and support requests may be sent to grayskyes@solenterprises.org. Include the applicable service, account, order, URL, content, and facts needed to investigate.
