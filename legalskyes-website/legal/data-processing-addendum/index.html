Data Processing Addendum

Processor/controller terms for enterprise and client data processing.

Effective date: May 3, 2026. Governing law baseline: Arizona, United States. Contact: grayskyes@solenterprises.org.

Roles

Where applicable, customer is the controller or business for customer personal data, and the Company acts as processor or service provider when processing personal data on customer’s documented instructions.

Processing instructions

We process customer personal data to provide, secure, support, analyze, debug, improve, and maintain the services, and as otherwise instructed by customer or required by law.

Confidentiality

Personnel and service providers with access to customer personal data are subject to confidentiality obligations or equivalent restrictions.

Subprocessors

We may use subprocessors for hosting, infrastructure, AI models, communications, payments, analytics, support, security, and operations. We remain responsible for subprocessors as required by applicable agreements.

Security measures

We maintain reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of services, which may include access controls, logging, encryption in transit where practical, backups, monitoring, segregation, and incident response processes.

Assistance

Taking into account the nature of processing and available information, we will provide reasonable assistance for data subject requests, security incidents, and compliance obligations where required and commercially practical.

Return or deletion

Upon termination, customer may request return or deletion of customer personal data subject to legal retention, backups, dispute records, security records, and operational constraints.

International transfers

Where required, international transfers will be handled through appropriate safeguards or contractual measures.

Contact and legal notices

Questions, legal notices, privacy requests, intellectual-property notices, and support requests may be sent to grayskyes@solenterprises.org. Include the applicable service, account, order, URL, content, and facts needed to investigate.
