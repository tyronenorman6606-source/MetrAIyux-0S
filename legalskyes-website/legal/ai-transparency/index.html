AI Transparency Notice

Public-facing AI disclosure language for chatbots, generated content, and automated workflows.

Effective date: May 3, 2026. Governing law baseline: Arizona, United States. Contact: legal-skyes@solenterprises.org.

AI interaction notice

Some website, support, routing, content, booking, analysis, and operational features may be powered by AI or automation. Users may interact with an automated system before human review.

Generated content

Content, recommendations, summaries, replies, media, code, routes, business insights, and classifications may be AI-generated or AI-assisted. AI content should be verified before reliance.

Human escalation

Users may request human review where available. Some automated workflows may be limited to routing, intake, support, scheduling, or informational assistance.

Sensitive decisions

We do not intend general AI features to make final decisions about employment, housing, credit, education, healthcare, insurance, legal rights, public benefits, or safety-critical matters without separate authorized compliance controls.

Disclosure and labeling

Where required, AI-generated content, synthetic media, chatbots, and automated interactions should be labeled or disclosed to users in a clear, visible, and context-appropriate way.

Contact and legal notices

Questions, legal notices, privacy requests, intellectual-property notices, and support requests may be sent to legal-skyes@solenterprises.org. Include the applicable service, account, order, URL, content, and facts needed to investigate.
