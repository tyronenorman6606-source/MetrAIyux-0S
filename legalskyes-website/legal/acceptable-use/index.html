Prohibited conduct

You may not use the services for unlawful, harmful, abusive, deceptive, infringing, exploitative, or dangerous activity.

Security abuse

No malware, botnets, credential stuffing, unauthorized scanning, denial-of-service, exploit delivery, phishing, keylogging, spyware, account takeover, token theft, or bypassing access controls.

Spam and deceptive communications

No spam, spoofing, misleading headers, deceptive sender identity, purchased SMS lists, false consent claims, fake reviews, fake testimonials, dark patterns, or unlawful telemarketing.

Regulated goods and services

No illegal drugs, weapons, explosives, human trafficking, counterfeit goods, stolen goods, unlawful gambling, unlawful financial services, unlicensed medical claims, or regulated activity without documented authorization.

AI misuse

No use of AI features to impersonate people without consent, generate fraudulent documents, manipulate voters, create deceptive deepfakes, evade safety systems, produce illegal instructions, or automate unlawful decisions.

Children

Services are not directed to children under 13. Users may not collect data from children through our systems unless a signed agreement and verified compliance workflow authorizes it.

Enforcement

We may remove content, restrict access, suspend services, preserve evidence, report abuse, notify affected parties, or terminate accounts when conduct creates legal, safety, security, carrier, platform, payment, or operational risk.

Contact and legal notices

Questions, legal notices, privacy requests, intellectual-property notices, and support requests may be sent to legal-skyes@solenterprises.org. Include the applicable service, account, order, URL, content, and facts needed to investigate.
