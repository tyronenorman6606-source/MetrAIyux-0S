# Over3arth Research Backbone

Over3arth should feel supernatural in presentation, but its behavior layer should stay grounded in psychology and behavior design.

## Product thesis

Users do not need another passive mood-board app. They need an interface that converts intention into repeated proof. Over3arth frames life domains as realms so the user can emotionally buy into the journey, but every major mechanic pushes toward practical behavior.

## Design principles

### 1. Values-based affirmation

Affirmations work best when they connect to self-integrity, values, and identity. Over3arth should avoid vague hype and instead help the user speak from what they value, what they are practicing, and what action they will take next.

Product implementation:

- Affirmation Forge
- Archetype mantra
- Prime intention
- Saved affirmation vault

### 2. Mental contrasting

Positive visualization alone can become fantasy. Over3arth should ask users to imagine the desired future and name the obstacle that may block it.

Product implementation:

- Desired outcome field
- Main obstacle field
- Ritual reflection prompt

### 3. Implementation intentions

Goal intentions become stronger when users define what they will do in a predictable situation. The app should make “If ___ happens, then I will ___” a core field.

Product implementation:

- If–then battle plan field
- Quest generator
- Daily ritual next action

### 4. Self-determination

People stay more engaged when the system supports autonomy, competence, and relatedness.

Product implementation:

- Autonomy: user names their world, chooses archetype, chooses realms
- Competence: XP, level, completed proof ledger
- Relatedness future upgrade: circles, covenants, private accountability groups

### 5. Expressive writing

Writing can help users process emotion, structure experience, and extract meaning. Over3arth should support notes without turning the product into a clinical tool.

Product implementation:

- Notes page
- Reality Ledger
- Daily ritual release/gratitude fields

### 6. Flow and gameful feedback

Users need a sense of progress and immediate feedback without manipulative dark patterns. Over3arth should make progress visible through XP, streaks, reality charge, and completed quests.

Product implementation:

- Reality charge meter
- XP and level
- Quest completion
- Realm map

## Claim boundary

Safe claim:

“Over3arth helps users shape their lived reality through intention, reflection, planning, and consistent action.”

Avoid:

- “This app lets users literally control external reality.”
- “This app cures anxiety, depression, trauma, or ADHD.”
- “This app guarantees manifestation or wealth.”

Use:

- “Forge your intended reality.”
- “Turn intention into action.”
- “Make proof visible.”
- “Command your attention.”
