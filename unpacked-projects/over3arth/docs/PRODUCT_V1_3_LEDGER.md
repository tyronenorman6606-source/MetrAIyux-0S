# Over3arth Product v1.3 Ledger

## Scope

This pass moves Over3arth from a strong local-first productivity PWA into a more serious retention and launch-readiness product.

## Added

✅ Reality Contracts with vow, daily proof, evidence standard, boundary, reward, and review date.
✅ Contract templates for 7-day, 13-day, and 30-day arcs.
✅ Contract-generated proof quests.
✅ Contract completion and ledger logging.
✅ Recovery Rite detection for missed ritual rhythm.
✅ Recovery Rite quest generation.
✅ Ascension Card generation as a text artifact.
✅ Ascension Card download and clipboard copy attempt.
✅ Local monetization lane cards: Forge, Ascendant, Sovereign.
✅ Local plan interest signals without fake payment collection.
✅ Schema v4 state normalization for contracts, recovery rites, share cards, and launch signals.
✅ README, command center, manifest, analytics, service worker, and proof updates.

## Truth boundaries

✅ The app stores data locally.
✅ The app exports/imports local JSON.
✅ The app can request browser notification permission.
✅ The app stores reminder preference.
✅ The app does not claim closed-app scheduled push reminders.
✅ The app does not collect payment.
✅ The plan cards are product/launch signals only until a real billing backend is connected.
✅ The app does not promise supernatural outcomes, medical treatment, or guaranteed external results.
