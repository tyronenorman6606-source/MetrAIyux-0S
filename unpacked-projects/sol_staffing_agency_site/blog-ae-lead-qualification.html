AE Lead Qualification: How to Separate Real Staffing Opportunities from Noise

A sales enablement article for staffing Account Executives qualifying employer demand.

Start a RequestBack to Insights

Every lead is not a job order.

A real staffing opportunity has a role, timeline, decision maker, budget or pay range, location, and urgency.

Pain reveals priority.

Ask what happens if the role stays open. If there is no consequence, the opportunity may not move.

Budget matters.

If pay is far below market, the AE needs to surface that early instead of letting recruiters struggle silently.

Decision process matters.

A qualified opportunity has someone who can approve terms, review candidates, and commit to next steps.

Capture disqualifiers.

Good lead qualification includes what the employer does not want, not just what they hope to find.

Submit complete notes.

Recruiters need context. AEs protect the desk by submitting complete, accurate job-order notes.

Build the workflow.

Use the site’s intake, proposal, onboarding, and operations pages to turn this guidance into repeatable execution.
