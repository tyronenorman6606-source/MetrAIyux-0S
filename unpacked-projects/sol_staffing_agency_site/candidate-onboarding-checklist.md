# Candidate Onboarding Checklist

Do not collect sensitive documents through unsecured forms.

## Before start
- Name and contact confirmed
- Role confirmed
- Start date confirmed
- Location confirmed
- Supervisor/check-in contact confirmed
- Shift confirmed
- Dress code/PPE confirmed
- Pay expectations confirmed
- Transportation/commute confirmed
- Attendance expectations explained
- Issue-reporting contact shared

## Documents
- Resume/work history
- Required certifications
- Identity/work authorization via secure process
- Background-check authorization if applicable
- Client-specific onboarding packet if applicable
