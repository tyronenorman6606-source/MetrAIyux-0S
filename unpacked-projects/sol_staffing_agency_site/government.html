Contract Readiness Checklist

What needs to be real before serious government contracting.

Registration & Identity

  • Legal entity name confirmed
  • Business address and POC information ready
  • SAM.gov entity registration or UEI path started
  • CAGE code entered when issued
  • NAICS codes verified against actual services
  • Banking and tax information controlled by the owner

Performance & Compliance

  • Capability statement completed
  • Past performance entries documented honestly
  • Quality-control process written
  • Candidate screening and onboarding process documented
  • Insurance, bonding, payroll, and worker classification reviewed
  • Proposal approval and contract review workflow defined
