Engagement Models

Clear staffing models for different hiring needs.

This page explains how employers can engage the agency. Exact pricing depends on role type, market, pay rate, urgency, compliance requirements, volume, risk, and contract terms.

No Fake Quotes

Quote after intake.

The site explains the model without hardcoding inaccurate rates. Final terms should be issued through a reviewed client agreement or proposal.

Temporary Staffing

Best for urgent coverage, seasonal volume, short-term demand, call-outs, event support, or operational backlogs.

  • Usually billed hourly
  • Markup depends on role/risk
  • Client approves timesheets
  • Replacement process should be defined

Temp-to-Hire

Best when the employer wants to evaluate performance before conversion.

  • Hourly bill rate during assignment
  • Conversion rules defined upfront
  • Evaluation window recommended
  • Good for attendance and fit validation

Direct Placement

Best for permanent roles where the employer wants recruiting support.

  • Placement fee model
  • Fee tied to annual compensation or flat agreement
  • Guarantee/replacement terms may apply
  • Interview process must be responsive

Project Crew

Best for defined scopes with multiple workers, dates, sites, or deliverables.

  • Scope-based or hourly structure
  • Headcount and supervisor path defined
  • Schedule and safety requirements documented
  • Useful for field, facilities, event, and logistics support

Government / Prime Support

Best for agencies, municipalities, and primes that need staffing support tied to a scope or solicitation.

  • Quote follows solicitation terms
  • Compliance requirements reviewed
  • NAICS and scope checked
  • No unverified certification claims

AE Partnership

Best for account development, employer prospecting, job-order gathering, and territory growth.

  • Commission terms must be written
  • Lead ownership rules defined
  • Qualified job-order standard required
  • Internal dashboard recommended
