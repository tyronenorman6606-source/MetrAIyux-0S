Launch Checklist

What to do before serious production.

Public Content

  • Replace legal entity placeholders
  • Add real phone number
  • Add service area details
  • Remove unverified jobs
  • Enter verified case studies only
  • Replace YOUR-DOMAIN-HERE in sitemap and robots

Government Fields

  • Enter UEI only when issued
  • Enter CAGE only when issued
  • Verify NAICS
  • Do not claim set-aside status unless certified
  • Do not claim GSA Schedule unless awarded
  • Review procurement packet before sending

Forms

  • Enable Netlify Forms or backend
  • Test staffing-request
  • Test candidate-application
  • Test government-inquiry
  • Test quote-request
  • Route notifications to owner/CRM

Security

  • Lock AE Command
  • Lock Employer Portal preview
  • Lock Government Opportunities
  • Lock Deployment Command Center
  • Do not collect sensitive docs through static forms
  • Add secure upload backend later

Smoke Tests

Run these before sharing the site.

Navigation: Click Home, Services, Employers, Candidates, Jobs, Government, Insights, Contact.

Forms: Submit test entries after deployment and verify they reach Netlify Forms or your connected backend.

SEO: Open sitemap.xml, robots.txt, and llms.txt. Replace the placeholder domain.

Internal routes: Confirm AE Command, Government Opportunities, Employer Portal, and Deployment Command are not publicly exposed.

Truth check: Confirm there are no fake jobs, fake testimonials, fake government registrations, fake certifications, or fake past performance.
