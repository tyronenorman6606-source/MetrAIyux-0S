Yes. This app is intended for adults 21 and older because it references tobacco, vape, hookah, and smoke-shop products.

The listed categories are glass, vapes, cigars, pipes, electronic devices, CBD products, tobacco, hookah, and exotic snacks.

Bob's Smoke Shop is listed as open daily from 8:00 AM to 12:00 AM. Call the shop to confirm holiday hours or special schedule changes.

Where is the shop located?

Bob's Smoke Shop is located at 5115 N Dysart Rd, Unit 214, Litchfield Park, AZ 85340.

Can I order online?

This app does not offer online ordering, delivery, pickup, or shipping. Call or visit the shop for current options.

Are prices listed online?

Prices, specials, and product availability can change. Call or visit the shop for current details.

Are CBD products medical products?

No medical claims are made in this app. Ask staff for product information and review product labels before purchase.
