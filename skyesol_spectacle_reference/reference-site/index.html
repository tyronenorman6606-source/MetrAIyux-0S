Pattern Library

Core spectacle patterns

These are distilled from the SkyeSol public site, especially the home, leadership, service, bundle, and kAIxU pages. Treat them as reusable recipes, not a one-page clone.

01

Founder/operator hero

Lead with person plus company signal. Use a large serif headline, tight uppercase system label, and a portrait floating in a glow ring.

  • Portrait or emblem is a first-viewport object.
  • Gold text gradient marks the founder or company name.
  • CTAs stay compact, uppercase, and command-like.
02

Liquid dark field

Layer a near-black base with aurora-like color movement, light grain, and faint scan texture for high-contrast depth.

  • Keep the background moving slowly.
  • Use cyan and violet as energy, not the whole palette.
  • Let gold carry authority and hierarchy.
03

Glass gradient cards

Cards sit on transparent dark glass with hairline borders, small radius, top-line glow, and hover lift.

  • Use 4-8px radius for SkyeSol core pages.
  • Prefer thin luminous borders to heavy shadows.
  • Accent each card with a small label or number.
04

Editorial/service sections

Alternate compact service grids, proof metrics, and editorial notes so the page feels like both portfolio and operating memo.

  • Use section labels before large serif titles.
  • Keep body copy muted and operational.
  • Separate bands with gold hairline dividers.

Motion Grammar

Movement should feel deliberate

SkyeSol motion works best when it suggests a living system: reveals, counter runs, slow float, shimmer, and glow pulses. Avoid frantic loops.

Use a pause/reduced-motion escape hatch on reference pages and any long-lived tool surface.

Reuse Notes

How to apply the look later

Use these rules as the handoff note for future SkyeSol-adjacent static pages and prototypes.

Palette

Start with #05050b and #07070f. Add gold for authority, violet/cyan for energy, and small fire accents for urgency.

Typography

Pair a dramatic serif for headlines with a clean sans for body and a mono/condensed uppercase treatment for labels.

Layout

Use full-width dark bands, constrained inner content, hairline dividers, and dense grids. Keep cards shallow and purposeful.

Voice

Write like an operator: proof, governance, field reality, execution, systems, command, credibility, and next action.
