The newsletter

An exciting week for the Skye platform stack

From Gray London Skyes and the SOLEnterprises / Skye platform team

This was one of those weeks where the work stopped feeling like a collection of separate builds and started behaving like an actual ecosystem.

MetrAIyux 0S is live as the command layer. SkyeGateFS27 is live as the auth and proof gate. SkyeVault-Drop is live for secure client file intake. CitadelDB is live as a proof-backed database command center. The Skye Ecosystem Portal is live as the front door. The SOL staffing platform is live as a practical operating example. The supporting worker fleet now answers cleanly with live health responses.

That means we are opening a limited beta for companies that want to test the stack in real business conditions.

Beta companies get two weeks for absolutely free. Usage will be limited by fair-use caps because we are not trying to turn a beta test into a surprise infrastructure bill. The point is simple: let your team try the platforms, run real workflows, test the fit, and tell us what does or does not hold up for your business.

If you find something that is not ready for your company’s actual usage, we want to know. Your feedback will go into the platform roadmap, and beta testers who help us sharpen the system will be eligible for a discounted rate for the first six months after the beta window.

This is for teams that want more than a brochure site. If your company needs operations, secure intake, proof receipts, admin command, staffing workflows, database clarity, or a sharper way to manage client work, this beta is built for you.

Beta terms

Free does not mean unlimited. It means useful, controlled, and honest.

Fair-use limits

Usage caps apply to files, AI/provider calls, heavy automation, storage, and high-volume requests. The beta is for evaluation, not unlimited production load.
