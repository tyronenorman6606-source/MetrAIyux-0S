No-surprise access model

Every screen has a job and an audience.

This map keeps the product from feeling like a bundle of unrelated apps. Customers see branded platform surfaces. Operators use the attached tools privately.

Public

Product pages

Homepage, pricing, demo, and sales explanation. No credentials, no raw app links.

Customer

Portal pages

Customer portal, support request, intake, billing, and handoff. One branded customer experience.

Operator

Command pages

Dashboard, setup, proof, maintenance, command center, brain, and admin tools.

Internal

Attached apps

Support desk, CRM, billing admin, and form builder. Self-hosted, but not customer signup destinations.
