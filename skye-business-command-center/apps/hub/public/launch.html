Launch promise

What “done” means for a paying client.

The client has a working branded hub, working admin accounts, working support inbox, working CRM pipeline, working invoice/estimate lane, working intake forms, backup plan, handoff instructions, and a support path. Anything less is “installed,” not “delivered.”

Client can accessHub, CRM, support desk, billing, and forms.
Client can operateReceive requests, track leads, issue estimates, and follow up.
Operator can supportBackups, logs, updates, rollback, health report, and renewal reminders.
