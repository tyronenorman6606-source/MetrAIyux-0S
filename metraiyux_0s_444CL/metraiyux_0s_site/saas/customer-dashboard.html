Customer command room.

Customers can talk to their workspace operator brain here. In production this command is persisted in D1, routed by the Site Operator Brain, and can generate approval tasks.



What becomes live after deployment
Commands can write to D1, queue work in Cloudflare Queues, trigger approval email through Resend, and dispatch approved work to connected providers.
