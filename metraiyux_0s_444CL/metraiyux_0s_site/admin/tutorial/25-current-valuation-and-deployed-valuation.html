Current Valuation and Deployed Valuation

The platform now has a live public overview asset, so valuation should treat it as deployed product infrastructure and public sales collateral, not only a packaged ZIP.

Operator valuation bands

  • Current deployed asset: $85K-$220K.
  • Deployed operating platform with public overview, full system route, D1, auth, Resend, isolation, and smoke receipts: $275K-$750K.
  • Revenue-backed SaaS with customer workspaces: $750K-$3.5M+ depending on MRR, churn, retention, connector depth, and usage proof.

What changed

The public spectacle overview is now a deployed Cloudflare Pages asset with sitemap, robots, fit check, guided tour, public brief export, and route to the larger full website. That improves sales-readiness, diligence-readiness, and replacement-cost value.

What still blocks higher value

Revenue beats screenshots. The next jump needs customer usage, protected billing/auth, retained workspaces, connector logs, approval records, and MRR/ARR.

Directional valuation only. Live production value depends on deployed proof, customer usage, retained revenue, protected secrets, connector policy, and verified approval controls.
