20. Autonomous Company Rhythm

The daily operating rhythm for using the admin brain as the company command screen.

Lesson steps

Morning digest

Ask the Main Automation Brain to summarize leads, open tasks, approvals, content drafts, candidate movement, and client issues.

Revenue block

Route lead follow-ups to Celeste Monroe and proposal work to the Proposal Center.

Staffing block

Route candidate/job-order activity to Sienna Brooks with QA follow-up where needed.

Client block

Route onboarding, retention, escalation, and renewal items to Adrian Cross.

Proof block

Route all public claims, launch statements, and completion claims to Victor Saint before publishing.

Operator checklist

  • Daily digest exported
  • Approval queue reviewed
  • At least one revenue action created
  • At least one content/proof action created
  • Ledger exported at end of day
