Lesson 23: Tenant Isolation and Security Gate Brain

This lesson explains the exact separation between customer SaaS workspaces and your owner/admin orchestrator brain.

Step 1 — Keep two control planes

Customer SaaS is not the same thing as the owner company OS. Customers get workspace tools. You get the Main Automation Brain, owner connectors, proof vault, and production authority.

Step 2 — Put Security Gate Brain in the middle

Every customer command is screened by Security Gate Brain before it can create an admin review task. Safe customer-scoped work stays inside the customer workspace.

Step 3 — Isolate credentials

Owner Resend keys, social OAuth, production publishing tokens, ADMIN_TOKEN, D1 global ledger access, and deployment secrets must not be present in public/customer code.

Step 4 — Approval-gate external action

Publishing, client outbound, public claims, contracts, hiring/firing, payments, pricing, and filings remain approval-gated.

Smoke check

Try a customer command that asks to post from the founder social account. The correct result is quarantine or approval-required, not automatic dispatch.

Owner/admin layer and customer SaaS layer are intentionally separated. Live enforcement requires deployed auth, Worker gates, D1/KV bindings, and connector policies.
