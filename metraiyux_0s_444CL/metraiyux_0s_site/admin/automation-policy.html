Autonomy boundaries

Admin Automation Policy

The autonomous business layer is allowed to operate aggressively inside safe bounds: draft, route, queue, summarize, assign, export, remind, prepare packets, and collect proof. External action needs configured systems and approval rules.

Allowed automatically

Internal drafts, internal task creation, cabinet routing, local receipts, proof checklists, CRM record drafts, candidate/client/vendor intake classification, and admin summaries.

Allowed after admin approval

Public social posts, email sends, proposal delivery, price quotes, client promises, vendor outreach, published claims, and website content changes.

Always restricted

Legal advice, tax advice, actual incorporation filings, contract execution, payments, hiring/firing decisions, regulated claims, and anything requiring a licensed professional.

Default approval gate

By default, the Worker treats these words as approval-required: publish, post, send, contract, payment, hire, fire, legal, tax, file, incorporate, price, refund, public claim, signature, client promise.

Demonstrative business OS. Live automation requires admin auth, provider credentials, approval policy, and deployment verification.
