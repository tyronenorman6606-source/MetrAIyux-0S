SaaS and white-label

Worker-level tenant isolation means the platform scales to clients without exposing owner infrastructure.

Each customer gets an isolated D1 workspace. 0meg4kAI verifies at runtime that no customer command reaches owner-scoped brains. White-label licensing on the Autonomous Office tier means the full platform deploys under a client's brand, to their Cloudflare account, in 10 days — and generates recurring revenue without additional infrastructure cost per client.

Strong fit signals

The system earns its keep when the operational complexity already has a cost.

If work is getting lost, approvals are inconsistent, customers enter through founder-owned channels, and enterprise buyers are asking for structure you don't have documented — this is what MetrAIyux 0S was built to fix.

High-fit indicators

  • Recurring intake from clients, candidates, vendors, or buyers that currently arrives through unmanaged channels.
  • Approval decisions on contracts, payments, hiring, or public claims that need a traceable record for enterprise or government diligence.
  • A public presence that needs to explain the operation clearly enough for serious buyers — before they ask for access.
  • Customer-facing workspaces that need Worker-level separation from the owner command layer.
  • Operations complex enough to justify 16 routing lanes — where a single undifferentiated AI assistant creates more confusion than clarity.
  • Plans to license the platform to clients, bill recurring, and run a SaaS business on top of deployed infrastructure.
