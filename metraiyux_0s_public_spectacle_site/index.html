What it is

A complete operating layer — not a project management tool or a chatbot wrapper.

MetrAIyux 0S routes every company command through a named functional brain, scans it with 0meg4kAI in two layers, and either executes with a logged receipt or holds it in the approval inbox. No autonomous contracts. No autonomous payments. No autonomous hiring. The system knows what it is.

16-brain model

Each brain owns a domain. Work routes to the right function with a logged receipt.

The 16 brains are deterministic keyword classifiers — not LLM inference. Same command in, same routing outcome, every time. That's what makes the approval gates and proof receipts meaningful. The brains know their domain, route to the right function, and log receipts. They do not hallucinate.
