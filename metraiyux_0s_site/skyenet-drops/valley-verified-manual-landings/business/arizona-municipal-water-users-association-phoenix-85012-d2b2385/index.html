Arizona Municipal Water Users Association

A regional association helping Valley municipalities coordinate water policy, stewardship, conservation education, and sustainable-supply planning for desert communities.

What the Public Sources Confirm

Member cities

Phoenix, Peoria, Scottsdale, Glendale, Avondale, Goodyear, Tempe, Mesa, Gilbert, and Chandler are named in the research.

Public reach

The official history says member municipalities serve roughly 3.7 million people.

Mission

AMWUA focuses on assured, safe, and sustainable water supplies for member communities.

Resources

Public resources include conservation information, tools, assistance, meetings, and issue tracking.

For

Municipal staff, elected officials, water agencies, residents, businesses, and policymakers working on Arizona water issues.
