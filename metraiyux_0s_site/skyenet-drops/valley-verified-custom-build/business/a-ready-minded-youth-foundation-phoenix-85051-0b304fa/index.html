What They Do

The foundation serves youth and young adults through a 10-week in-person Phoenix campus model with home economics, job preparation, automotive basics, wood shop, leather works, parenting, heat press and DTF transfer, and self-discipline sessions. Its group program serves ages 13-17 and 18-22, while the vocational foundation program serves ages 14-22.
