## What Valley Verified Is Supposed To Be

Valley Verified is a Phoenix-area business trust and conversion network. Its job is to make each listed company look real, useful, local, and ready to contact. A visitor should land on a business page and immediately understand what the company does, where it serves, why it is credible, and what action to take next.

The business is the product. The page must lead with the company, not platform language, not AI commentary, not generic verification talk, and not a SkyEmail sales pitch. Platform services can support the business after the page proves the business.

## Core Rules

1. Every company gets its own folder, assets, facts, and build notes before any new SkyeNet page is made.
2. Every public landing must be hand-built business by business from the available facts and real assets in that company's folder.
3. No generated template pages, no mass page generator, no generic filler sections, and no reusable copy that makes every business sound the same.
4. A page should say the business is verified/accepted where appropriate. It should not ask the business to verify itself as the main action.
5. SkyEmail is only a post-claim utility: give the accepted owner sign-in instructions after claim, allow 24 hours for activation, and keep it secondary to the business.
6. Internal provisioning must notify the owner/team to provision the workspace, track remaining seats, and warn when the last two seats remain. Seats are bought in groups of five.
7. No creative IP or paid assets are downloadable by the public unless the viewer is the artist/owner or has paid through SkyePay for that asset.
8. No text-logo placeholders. Missing-logo businesses get a clean non-text symbol mark as a working asset until a real brand logo is made or provided.
9. Real assets stay in the business folder that owns them. Business facts stay in structured files that can be reviewed before design.
10. SkyeNet hosting should happen only after the rebuilt content package is ready for true custom pages and proofed against live Citadel-backed infrastructure.

## What A Business Folder Must Contain

Each business folder in this package contains:

- business.json: structured facts already present in the Valley Verified dataset.
- README.md: human-readable business brief for the designer/operator.
- assets/: real copied assets where they exist, plus a non-text working logo mark when no real logo was found.
- assets/asset-inventory.json: exact asset list and source paths.

## Landing Page Standard For The Later Rebuild

Each future landing should be designed around the business category, tone, and customer intent. It should include the business name, category, location, service area, offer, credibility signals, contact paths, visual assets, and a clear action. It should not include a page-length explanation of Valley Verified, 0S, SkyeNet, or SkyEmail unless the specific business workflow requires it.

Pages should be visually distinct. A smoke shop, dentist, property manager, electronics recycler, film production shop, barber, and pickleball venue should not share the same skeleton. Reuse data and platform wiring only after the design direction is custom.
