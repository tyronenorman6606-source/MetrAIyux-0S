# Lucid Private Offices Research

Status: moderate

## Source-Backed Notes
- Official Lucid page confirms the Camelback/Biltmore location at 2325 East Camelback Road, Suite 400, Phoenix, AZ 85016.
- Services include private offices, team suites, part-time offices, coworking, meeting rooms, and virtual office/business address plans.
- Official pages describe all-inclusive workspace with furnished or unfurnished options, high-speed internet, IT support, complimentary printing, receptionist services, meeting-room access, and secure 24/7 access for private-office members.
- Chamber listing confirms phone (602) 847-9835 and workspace/office leasing categories.
- Official pricing visible at access time included offices from $675/month, part-time offices at $375/month, and virtual office plans from $75/month.
