# Arizona Municipal Water Users Association

Researched: 2026-05-28
Status: rich

## Notes

- Local business.json lists Arizona Municipal Water Users Association at 3003 N. Central Ave., Ste. 1550, Phoenix, AZ 85012 with phone (602) 248-8482.
- Official contact page confirms the same address and phone plus info@amwua.org and visitor parking validation.
- Official pages describe AMWUA as a municipal water association focused on collaboration, advocacy, conservation education, and sustainable water supplies.
- Official history says AMWUA was incorporated in 1969 and now represents ten member municipalities serving roughly 3.7 million people.
- Member municipalities shown on the official site include Phoenix, Peoria, Scottsdale, Glendale, Avondale, Goodyear, Tempe, Mesa, Gilbert, and Chandler.
- Public office hours were not found.
