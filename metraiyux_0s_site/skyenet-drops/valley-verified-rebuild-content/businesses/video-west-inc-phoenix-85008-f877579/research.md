## Confirmed Facts
- Official ON Services Phoenix page lists Video West powered by ON Services at 1050 N. 52nd Street, Phoenix, AZ 85008; phone 480.222.3180.
- Official transition page says Video West joined ON Services in March 2025 and officially uses the ON Services brand starting January 1, 2026.
- Official Phoenix page describes live event AV production in Phoenix, including staging, lighting, video production, concerts, corporate events, and large-scale productions.
- Official Phoenix page lists event-production capabilities including AV technology, digital/interactive technology, projection mapping, lighting, content and speaker session management, scenic/custom fabrication, computer rental/support, and venue AV.
- Third-party directory lists hours Monday-Friday 8:00AM-5:00PM and weekends closed; official page did not confirm hours.
- AZ Big Media reports the 1050 N. 52nd St. headquarters/warehouse was a 40,000-square-foot facility near Loop 202 and Sky Harbor.

## Landing Page Use
