# National Technical Institute Research

Status: source_backed
Researched: 2026-05-28T06:24:12Z

## Confirmed Facts
- Official Phoenix campus: 3660 E Wier Ave, Phoenix, AZ 85040; phone (480) 591-4000.
- Official site lists HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and Phoenix-only commercial refrigeration training.
- Official page describes a 10,000-square-foot training space and 6,000-square-foot lab with 3-phase power, electrical training units, and hands-on equipment.
- AZ Job Connection confirms the same address and phone, lists NTI as a private for-profit/proprietary school, and shows approval status Approved.
- NTI Arizona page says the Phoenix campus opened in 2019 and offers HVAC, electrical, and plumbing courses.
