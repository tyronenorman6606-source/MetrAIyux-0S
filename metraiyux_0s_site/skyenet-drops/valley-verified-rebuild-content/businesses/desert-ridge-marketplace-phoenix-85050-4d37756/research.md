# Desert Ridge Marketplace Research

Status: complete
Researched: 2026-05-28T06:23:37Z

## Source-Backed Summary
- Official site positions Desert Ridge Marketplace as a North Valley destination for shopping, chef-driven restaurants, health/fitness options, entertainment, upgraded amenities, and more than 100 brands.
- Official footer lists 21001 N. Tatum Blvd., Phoenix, AZ 85050 and Management Office: (480) 513-7586.
- Official events page says the marketplace hosts hundreds of free events and happenings each year, including signature events, art festivals, live music, and fitness classes.
- Greater Phoenix Chamber confirms categories: Shopping Malls, Bars and Nightclubs, Entertainment, Restaurants, and Retail.

## Landing Page Guidance
- Write for visitors first: shop, eat, entertainment, events, map/contact.
- Add a secondary path for leasing, sponsorship, and media inquiries.
- Tenant names and event dates are dynamic; refresh from the official directory/events page before publishing.
