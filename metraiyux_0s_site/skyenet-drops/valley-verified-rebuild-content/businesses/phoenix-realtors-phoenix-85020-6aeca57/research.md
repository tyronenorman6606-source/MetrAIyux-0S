# Phoenix REALTORS Research

Status: source_backed
Researched: 2026-05-28T06:24:12Z

## Confirmed Facts
- Official office page confirms 7878 N 16th St, Suite 150, Phoenix, AZ 85020; phone 602.246.1012; email info@phoenixrealtors.com.
- Official office hours are Monday-Friday, 8:00AM to 4:00PM.
- Official member benefits include free CE classes, MarketStats tools, listing media services, local discounts, REALTOR store deliveries, office space reservations, member store pricing, and member cafe.
- Official "How It Works" page says local membership also includes state and national association membership with Arizona Association of REALTORS and National Association of REALTORS.
- Official Market Stats page says Phoenix REALTORS provides reports based on ARMLS data for primarily the Phoenix-metro area and some surrounding Arizona communities.
