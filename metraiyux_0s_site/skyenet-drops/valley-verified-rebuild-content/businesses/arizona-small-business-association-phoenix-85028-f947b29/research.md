# Arizona Small Business Association

Researched: 2026-05-28
Status: rich

## Notes

- Local business.json lists Arizona Small Business Association at 11811 N. Tatum Blvd., Ste. P195, Phoenix, AZ 85028 with phone (602) 306-4000.
- Official site confirms the same address, phone, and info@asba.com.
- Official about page says ASBA was founded in 1973 and is a nonprofit 501(c)(6), membership-based trade association and nonpartisan advocate/resource hub for Arizona small businesses.
- Official home page lists 2,500+ members, 30,000+ social reach, 53 years in business, and members in all 15 Arizona counties.
- Official benefits page confirms networking, advocacy, education, events, community calendar, member directory, SCALE, Small Business Incubator, ElevateIT, ForgeAhead, Locate48, ribbon cuttings, and digital marketing benefits.
- Exact membership prices were not visible in reviewed text; official membership page says packages are tiered and points questions to Robin Duncan.
