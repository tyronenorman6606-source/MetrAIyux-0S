# Entity Formation Readiness

This is an owner/counsel preparation sheet, not legal or tax advice. The company registration and EIN are treated as established based on founder confirmation on 2026-05-17.

## Private Evidence To Attach

- Exact legal entity name.
- Jurisdiction of formation.
- Entity type.
- Member-managed or manager-managed structure.
- Founder/member ownership.
- Authorized signer and manager authority.
- Registered/statutory agent.
- Principal office and mailing address.
- EIN confirmation letter or tax-professional confirmation.
- Tax classification to discuss with a tax professional.
- Whether MetrAIyux is owned by a new entity, Skyes Over London LC, or another holding/licensing structure.

## Arizona Reference If Arizona Is The Jurisdiction

The repo's Legal Skyes materials currently use Arizona as the governing-law baseline. If Arizona remains the chosen jurisdiction, the Arizona Corporation Commission LLC forms page lists Articles of Organization for creating a limited liability company and Statutory Agent Acceptance materials.

Official source: https://www.azcc.gov/corporations/forms/llc-forms

## Formation Evidence To Store Privately

- Articles of Organization receipt.
- Approved entity record.
- Statutory agent acceptance.
- Operating agreement execution copy.
- EIN confirmation letter.
- Banking/account opening evidence.
- Authority resolution naming who can sign contracts, vendor terms, payment processor agreements, and platform/commercial agreements.

Do not publish the full EIN in public pages, source comments, screenshots, or buyer-facing marketing. Share it only through tax, banking, counsel, and diligence channels that need it.

## Questions For Counsel

- Should MetrAIyux be a separate operating LLC, a DBA/product line of Skyes Over London LC, or a platform asset held by another entity?
- Which entity should own MetrAIyux platform assets?
- Which entity should sign platform/commercial deals?
- Which entity should hold customer data, receive payments, employ contractors, and own platform IP?
- What governing law, venue, dispute path, and limitation-of-liability structure should be used in commercial deals?
