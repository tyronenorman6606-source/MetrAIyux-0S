# Legal and Copyright Notes

This local app is for research, ideation, and original content transformation.

Do:

- Use source posts to identify topics, market shifts, framing angles, and educational opportunities.
- Write original Skyes Over London content from those ideas.
- Attribute source inspiration when useful.
- Verify factual claims, dates, product names, and legal/regulatory statements before publishing.

Do not:

- Copy full article text into public output.
- Reuse publisher images without rights.
- Present source claims as your own original research unless independently verified.
- Claim endorsement, partnership, certification, or affiliation with the source publisher.

The app prompts the AI to transform, not copy. Final publishing judgment is still yours.
