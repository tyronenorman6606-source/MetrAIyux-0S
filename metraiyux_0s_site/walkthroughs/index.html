What happens after start

The guide takes over the route.

  1. It stores the selected path in this browser.
  2. It loads the first 0S page in that path.
  3. It scrolls to the relevant control.
  4. It highlights the target room or input.
  5. Next moves to the next control or next page.

Expected completion

A trained user can operate without a live explanation.

They should know where to ask the Local Brain, where to show proof receipts, where to route buyers, where to onboard clients, where approval gates protect admin automation, and where to export training or launch evidence.

Completion checklist

  • Can identify the right room for a task.
  • Can ask the Local Brain for routing and proof help.
  • Can use at least one browser-local tool.
  • Can explain approval gates before live actions.
  • Can export a training or proof record.
