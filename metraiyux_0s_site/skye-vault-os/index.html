SkyeVaultOS

SkySecure Vault Console sits under FS27 and SkyeVault so ignored secrets, delete-candidate folders, developer handoffs, restore points, grants, revokes, diffs, reloads, bundles, drive handoffs, Reape0r mutable current repo mirrors, and audit receipts stay visible without pushing plaintext into Git. The current evidence is live system proof: filesystem work ran through the CLI/app proof lane, and FS27 plus 0S publish the deployed proof, command, inventory, search, restore-point, audit, health, and console routes.
