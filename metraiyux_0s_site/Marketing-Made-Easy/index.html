NorthStar rule

This platform now follows the same ownership pattern.

Public marketing rooms, build rooms, document rooms, launch rooms, and intelligence rooms all route through one platform entry. The modules can still keep their own UI, but they do not get their own rogue auth lane, root API lane, or public control-plane mutation lane.

The mounted worker owns /api/marketing-made-easy/*, the shell owns /Marketing-Made-Easy/index.html?workspace=<slug>, and the workspace room is Free99 while still gate-owned and rate-limited.
