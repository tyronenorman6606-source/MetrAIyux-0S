AE rules
for honest selling.

The company can be aggressive, premium, and direct without promising things it cannot control. These rules protect the brand, the client, and the sale.

Do say

  • We manage the system around the website.
  • We may coordinate vetted software, vendors, ad platforms, and fulfillment partners when needed.
  • Ad spend is separate from management fees.
  • We report what shipped, what changed, and what should happen next.
  • We can improve the lead path, trust layer, follow-up, and reporting.
  • We need access, client inputs, and approval before certain work can start.

Do not say

  • Do not guarantee leads, rankings, revenue, ROAS, or review volume.
  • Do not claim official partner status unless it is actually true.
  • Do not pretend ad platforms, software tools, or subcontractors are owned by Skyes Over London.
  • Do not hide ad spend or blur it into management fees.
  • Do not promise fake reviews, incentivized positive reviews, or review removal.
  • Do not sell unlimited work inside a fixed package.
Lockdown rule: this hub contains internal selling scripts and should remain behind Netlify Basic Auth, Netlify Password Protection, Netlify team login, Identity, or another real access layer before public deployment.
