AE Contractor Onboarding
paperwork, tax file, payout profile, and agreement acceptance.

Use this packet before an AE starts selling. It creates a consistent file for taxes, payout setup, commission rules, confidentiality, and independent-contractor classification support. Have counsel review final legal language before use.

Legal/tax guardrail: contractor status depends on the real working relationship, not the label on the form. The IRS looks at behavioral control, financial control, and the type of relationship. Arizona also follows IRS worker-status determinations for withholding purposes. Do not control contractor schedules like employees, do not promise employee benefits, and do not use this packet as a substitute for legal advice.

SKYES OVER LONDON LC INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE AGREEMENT TEMPLATE This template is operational paperwork, not legal advice. Skyes Over London LC should have a licensed attorney review the final version before relying on it. 1. Relationship. Contractor is engaged as an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, joint venturer, or agent authorized to bind Skyes Over London LC. Contractor controls the manner, method, schedule, and place of work subject to the contracted deliverables and compliance rules. 2. Services. Contractor may prospect, introduce, qualify, and help close clients for approved Skyes Over London LC services. Contractor may not promise unapproved services, guarantee rankings, guarantee ad returns, guarantee lead volume, bind the company to custom scope, modify pricing, collect cash personally, or represent themselves as an employee. 3. Compensation. Contractor is paid only according to the written commission plan or signed addendum then in effect. No commission is earned until client payment clears. No commission is paid on ad spend, domains, third-party software, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, unpaid invoices, or pass-through vendor costs. Recurring commissions, if offered, are subject to retention, active account status, clawback rules, and the term stated in the commission plan. 4. Taxes. Contractor is responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes, estimated tax payments, self-employment taxes, licensing, expenses, insurance, and filings related to contractor earnings. Contractor must provide a completed Form W-9 or applicable tax documentation before payment. 5. Compliance. Contractor must follow company sales rules, advertising compliance rules, platform policies, review policy rules, privacy rules, and no-guarantee language. Contractor must not create fake reviews, misrepresent agency status, claim official partnerships unless approved, or make unsupported performance claims. 6. Confidentiality. Contractor may receive private scripts, pricing, fulfillment methods, customer information, client data, internal systems, and business plans. Contractor must keep that information confidential and use it only for approved Skyes Over London LC work. 7. Client Protection. Contractor may not bypass Skyes Over London LC, redirect prospects to a competing service, solicit active clients away from the company, or use company-provided leads for personal deals. State law can affect enforceability, so this section should be reviewed by counsel. 8. Ownership. Scripts, sales materials, site assets, playbooks, reports, pricing, dashboards, and internal systems remain Skyes Over London LC property unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. 9. Termination. Either party may end the contractor relationship under the terms of the final signed agreement. Termination does not remove confidentiality, client-protection, payment reconciliation, or return-of-property obligations. 10. Signature. Contractor acknowledges they read the agreement, understand they are an independent contractor, and accept the commission plan, compliance rules, confidentiality duties, and payment-profile requirements. SKYES OVER LONDON LC ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE COMMISSION PLAN ADDENDUM TEMPLATE Default plan unless changed in writing: - 15% to 20% on eligible one-time build and setup fees after client payment clears. - 10% recurring commission on eligible monthly retainers for up to 12 months while the account remains active and paid. - 0% commission on ad spend, domains, software pass-through, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, unpaid invoices, or vendor costs. - 60-day clawback may apply if a client refunds, chargebacks, cancels, or fails onboarding. - Recurring commission stops if the client account becomes unpaid, cancelled, refunded, charged back, or transferred under a different written arrangement. - Contractor must log prospect source, package sold, client payment confirmation, and accepted scope before payout approval. This plan is not a wage promise and does not guarantee income. Contractor earnings depend on closed, paid, eligible revenue. SKYES OVER LONDON LC CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTERNAL SYSTEMS ACKNOWLEDGMENT TEMPLATE Contractor may access private pricing, scripts, service packaging, client lists, prospect notes, fulfillment systems, internal strategy, templates, and operating methods. Contractor agrees to keep those materials confidential, not publish them, not sell them, not use them for unrelated ventures, and not disclose them to unauthorized parties. Contractor must protect client data, tax documents, payment profiles, account access, business information, and private reports. Contractor must immediately report suspected unauthorized access, lost devices, misdirected files, or accidental disclosure. SKYES OVER LONDON LC CONTRACTOR PAYMENT PROFILE AUTHORIZATION TEMPLATE Contractor authorizes Skyes Over London LC to use the submitted payout profile to issue contractor payments through approved methods, which may include bank transfer, Stripe, PayPal, Cash App, check, or another approved payout method. Contractor is responsible for keeping payout details current and accurate. Payments are subject to cleared client payments, commission approval, payout schedule, clawback rules, tax documentation, and account status. Skyes Over London LC does not pay commission on pass-through costs, ad spend, unpaid invoices, refunds, chargebacks, or disqualified deals.

4. Tax and document uploads

Request a current Form W-9 from U.S. contractors before issuing reportable payments. Do not put full SSNs/TINs into plain text fields; upload the official W-9 file into the restricted Drive folder.

Use the official IRS W-9. Keep it restricted because it can contain TIN/SSN data.

5. Payment profile

Select the preferred payout method. Bank and payout details are encrypted before they are uploaded to Google Drive by the backend function.

Security rule: restrict the destination Google Drive folder to ownership/admin only. Do not share raw W-9, bank, or identity files with AEs.

6. Submit packet

Submission saves the onboarding summary, encrypted payout profile, agreement acceptance, and uploaded documents into the configured Google Drive folder.

Backend requirements: Netlify deploy with GOOGLE_DRIVE_FOLDER_ID, GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_EMAIL, GOOGLE_PRIVATE_KEY, and CONTRACTOR_PACKET_ENCRYPTION_KEY_BASE64. Without these, the form will show a configuration error instead of pretending to save.

Allowed uploads: PDF, PNG, JPG, or DOCX. Max 8MB per file and 30MB total. W-9 is required. Admin notification can be sent when ADMIN_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL and RESEND_API_KEY are configured.
Setup required: contractor packet uploads need Google Drive and Netlify environment variables before live use. Open the Drive + Env Setup Guide for the full walkthrough.
