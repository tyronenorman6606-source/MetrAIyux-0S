Client portal

Client portal scaffold.

Client portal scaffold for reports, shipped deliverables, invoices, requested edits, open blockers, and next recommended growth moves.

Integration note: this portal scaffold is ready to connect to your existing authentication system and data layer. It does not expose client records publicly. Keep this folder behind login before live use.
Reports

Monthly report cards for traffic, forms, calls, reviews, ads, shipped work, blockers, and next recommendations.

Deliverables

Pages shipped, content published, GBP updates, review assets, automations, and campaign work.

Invoices + payment links

Invoice status, payment links, retainer status, and renewal notes.

Edit requests

Client change requests, priority, requested date, files, status, and completion notes.

Open blockers

Access needed, pending approvals, missing assets, platform issues, and client-side dependencies.

Next moves

Recommended upsells, fixes, campaigns, content, tracking, and operations priorities.
