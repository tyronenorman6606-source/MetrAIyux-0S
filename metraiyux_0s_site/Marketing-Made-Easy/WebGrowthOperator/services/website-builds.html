Website Builds

A premium, mobile-first website that explains the offer clearly, earns trust quickly, and gives visitors a simple path to call, book, request a quote, or walk in.

Start IntakeView Pricing

Who this is for

  • Local businesses that need a legitimate website without dragging the project for months.
  • Service brands that need clear service pages, call/quote flows, and local trust signals.
  • Founder-led companies replacing weak, outdated, or scattered web presence.
  • Operators who want the website to become the base layer for hosting, content, ads, reviews, and lead recovery.

What the client receives

  • Page map and conversion path
  • Homepage and selected internal pages
  • Service/offer copy written in clear client language
  • Responsive SVS-branded interface
  • Contact form or intake path
  • On-page SEO basics
  • Mobile and link QA
  • Deployment and launch handoff

What must be clear

  • Complex ecommerce, dashboards, portals, membership systems, and heavy backend features are custom quoted.
  • Build packages do not include unlimited revisions, custom photography, paid software subscriptions, ad spend, or third-party platform costs.
  • Work begins after scope and billing are approved. Source handoff occurs after the build invoice is completed.

Client inputs needed

  • Business name, offer, service area, phone, email, logo, and brand preferences.
  • Domain/DNS access or a decision-maker who can complete requested DNS changes.
  • Current photos, testimonials, menus, pricing, service lists, or proof assets if available.
  • Examples of sites or styles the client likes and dislikes.

Delivery workflow

Confirm site type and page count.
Collect business facts, offers, assets, and access.
Build the structure, copy, visual direction, and responsive layout.
Connect forms, CTA paths, metadata, and deployment settings.
Run mobile, form, image, link, and navigation checks.
Launch, document what shipped, and move the client into hosting/care or growth operations.
