Paid Traffic Management

Campaigns are connected to offers, landing pages, tracking paths, and reporting instead of random boosted posts or unmanaged ad spend.

Start IntakeView Pricing

Who this is for

  • Businesses ready to buy visibility or leads with a clear offer.
  • Owners who have tried ads without landing pages, tracking, or follow-up.
  • Service companies that need calls, quote requests, appointments, or form leads.

What the client receives

  • Campaign intake and offer selection
  • Keyword/audience direction
  • Ad copy direction
  • Landing page or campaign section alignment
  • Conversion tracking plan where available
  • Optimization notes
  • Monthly spend and performance summary

What must be clear

  • No guaranteed leads, sales, ROAS, or ranking promises.
  • Ad spend is separate from management unless stated in writing.
  • Skyes Over London manages the work; it does not present itself as Google, Meta, or any platform unless a verified status exists.

Client inputs needed

  • Ad account access or approval to create/manage campaigns.
  • Monthly ad budget and approval rules.
  • Target services, service area, margin priorities, and blocked terms/audiences.
  • Landing page approval and conversion goals.

Delivery workflow

Confirm the offer and budget.
Check landing page readiness and tracking path.
Build campaign structure and ad copy direction.
Launch with spend controls and conversion goals.
Review search terms, audiences, creative, and waste.
Report spend, lead activity where trackable, changes made, and next tests.

Report after work ships

  • Clicks and traffic quality
  • Calls/forms/bookings where trackable
  • Changes made
  • Wasted-spend notes
