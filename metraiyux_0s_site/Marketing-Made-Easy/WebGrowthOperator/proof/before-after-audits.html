Before / after audits

Before and after audit patterns.

Common before-and-after audit patterns for Phoenix-area business websites, including weak CTAs, missing service pages, poor mobile layout, weak reviews, and broken follow-up.

Before: brochure-only homepage

No clear offer, weak CTA, no proof, no local relevance, and no next step beyond a generic contact form.

After: conversion path

Clear headline, service menu, proof blocks, city/service relevance, form CTA, phone CTA, and follow-up path.

Before: paid traffic to weak page

Ads send visitors to a generic homepage that does not match the offer or capture the lead clearly.

After: campaign landing page

Ad offer, proof, form, call path, service area, tracking plan, and follow-up workflow are aligned.

Before: reviews are random

Happy customers are not asked at the right moment and unhappy customers are not routed into recovery.

After: review engine

QR links, staff scripts, post-service request timing, testimonial capture, and private recovery route.
