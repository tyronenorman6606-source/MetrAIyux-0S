Google Business Profile Ops
fulfillment playbook.

The profile becomes more accurate, current, and useful with cleaned-up services, posts, photos, links, Q&A, and local trust updates.

Public service pageIntake formPricing

Deliverables owed

  • Profile audit
  • Services/category recommendations
  • Description and link refresh
  • Posts, offers, photos, and Q&A updates
  • Local trust recommendations
  • Monthly activity summary

Access / inputs to collect

  • Business Profile owner access or manager invitation.
  • Correct business name, phone, address/service area, hours, website, and service list.
  • Approved photos, offers, announcements, and public-facing details.

Scope controls

  • No fake locations, no keyword-stuffed business names, no false categories, and no guaranteed map placement.
  • The business must control its own profile. Skyes Over London should be added as a manager, not as the true owner.

Step-by-step SOP

Confirm ownership/access.
Audit profile accuracy and missing trust signals.
Update services, descriptions, links, photos, and posts as approved.
Add Q&A and offer updates where appropriate.
Monitor obvious profile issues and suggested changes.
Report what changed and what should be improved next.

QA before marking complete

  • Confirm each purchased deliverable is either shipped, blocked by client input, or custom-quoted.
  • Check the relevant live path: links, forms, profile changes, ad settings, lead routes, CRM steps, or report data.
  • Document screenshots, links, notes, or test outcomes in the client record.
  • Send the completion or monthly status note with next recommended action.

Report template

  • Posts/photos/offers added
  • Services or links updated
  • Profile risks
  • Review and content recommendations
