Managed Hosting + Care
fulfillment playbook.

The website stays live, secure, editable, and supported without the client needing to understand deployments, SSL, forms, or small technical updates.

Public service pageIntake formPricing

Deliverables owed

  • Managed deployment path
  • SSL and CDN delivery
  • Form routing check
  • Monthly edit allowance
  • Versioned update process
  • Quarterly tune-up where included
  • Status note after work is shipped

Access / inputs to collect

  • Domain/DNS access or client cooperation for DNS changes.
  • Clear monthly edit requests submitted through email or intake.
  • Approval for public-facing changes before they go live when needed.

Scope controls

  • Care does not mean unlimited redesign, unlimited pages, emergency app support, or complex platform administration.
  • Domains, email hosting, paid plugins, outside software, and premium integrations are separate unless included in writing.

Step-by-step SOP

Confirm hosting plan and site location.
Connect deployment, domain, SSL, and form route.
Document the support path for edit requests.
Process monthly edits inside the plan allowance.
Check navigation, forms, and mobile after edits.
Send a concise status note and recommend the next improvement.

QA before marking complete

  • Confirm each purchased deliverable is either shipped, blocked by client input, or custom-quoted.
  • Check the relevant live path: links, forms, profile changes, ad settings, lead routes, CRM steps, or report data.
  • Document screenshots, links, notes, or test outcomes in the client record.
  • Send the completion or monthly status note with next recommended action.

Report template

  • Edits completed
  • Site/form status
  • Open client requests
  • Recommended next improvement
