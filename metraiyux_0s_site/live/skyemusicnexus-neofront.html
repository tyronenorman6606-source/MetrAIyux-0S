Honest boundary

It sells the room without overclaiming the rails.

SkyeMusicNexus is a gated creation, release-ops, and proof workspace. It does not claim hosted DAW distribution, live DSP submission, distributor ingestion, public streaming licensing, royalty settlement, legal rights clearance, or real payout movement until those provider integrations are separately approved and proven.

  • Free99 means no charge for Lite, not anonymous access.
  • Paid drops are work packets, not automatic distribution authority.
  • Rights and royalty views are proof-safe operating notes unless a verified provider scope is connected.
  • Every lane keeps the gate session requirement intact.
