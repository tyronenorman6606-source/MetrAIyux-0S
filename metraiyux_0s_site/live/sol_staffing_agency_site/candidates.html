Candidate Readiness

What makes a candidate easier to place.

Clear Availability

Know your start date, shift preference, transportation limits, location range, and schedule restrictions.

Proof of Experience

List employers, duties, tools, certifications, software, equipment, customer-facing experience, and measurable results.

Fast Communication

Respond quickly, keep your phone active, check email, and confirm interviews or onboarding steps immediately.

Document Readiness

Have identification, work authorization, resume, references, certifications, and requested onboarding documents available.

Role Fit

Be honest about what you can do, what you cannot do, pay expectations, commute limits, and physical requirements.

Reliability

Attendance, communication, professional attitude, and follow-through often matter as much as technical skill.
