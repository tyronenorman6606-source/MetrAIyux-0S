# Bill Rate Calculator Notes

This is not final pricing. A real staffing bill rate must account for:
- Worker pay
- Employer payroll taxes
- Workers compensation
- General liability / E&O if applicable
- Benefits if applicable
- Payroll provider fees
- Recruiting cost
- Overhead
- Bad debt risk
- Overtime rules
- Client payment terms
- Contract compliance
- Market scarcity
- Margin target

Formula used in the static calculator:
Loaded Cost = Pay Rate × (1 + Burden %)
Bill Rate = Loaded Cost ÷ (1 - Gross Margin %)

Review with finance/payroll/legal before quoting.
