# Client Agreement Notes

Internal starter notes only. Have qualified counsel draft/review actual staffing agreements.

## Agreement topics

- Legal entity and client entity
- Services provided
- Temporary staffing, temp-to-hire, direct placement, or project model
- Bill rates / fees
- Payment terms
- Timesheet approval
- Conversion fees
- Replacement policy
- Background check responsibility
- Safety and site requirements
- Insurance
- Confidentiality
- Non-solicitation if applicable
- Termination
- Dispute handling
- Governing law

Do not place workers without appropriate agreements, payroll/insurance setup, and compliance review.
