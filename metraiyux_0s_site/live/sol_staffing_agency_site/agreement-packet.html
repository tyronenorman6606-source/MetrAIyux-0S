Agreement Packet

Organize agreement documents before staffing begins.

This internal page defines which agreement pieces need review before placing workers or pursuing a staffing scope. This is not legal advice.

Counsel Review

Do not improvise contracts.

Final agreements should be drafted or reviewed by qualified counsel.

Master Service Agreement

Defines the staffing relationship, services, payment, responsibilities, confidentiality, termination, and dispute process.

Job Order / SOW

Defines role, location, headcount, schedule, rate, start date, requirements, and client-specific terms.

Direct-Hire Terms

Defines placement fee, guarantee if any, replacement policy, payment trigger, and candidate ownership period.

Temp-to-Hire Conversion

Defines conversion timing, fee, approval, and restrictions.

Timesheet Approval

Defines how hours are approved, disputed, corrected, and billed.

Government Flow-Downs

For prime/government work, required clauses and obligations must be reviewed before acceptance.
