How it works

From intake to tracked paperwork.

SovereignDocs turns paperwork into a guided workflow: choose a lane, answer questions, generate a draft or prep worksheet, edit in SkyeDocxMax, route for review when needed, and track the full case.

1

Choose a lane

Start with formation, contracts, compliance, partner review, signatures, estate worksheets, trademark prep, or a custom case.

2

Complete intake

Answer guided prompts so the system can assemble the right documents, risk gates, and next steps.

3

Edit in SkyeDocxMax

Open serious drafts in the dedicated editor layer while keeping the case, fields, and document metadata connected.

4

Route or export

Export eligible documents, build packets, or send high-risk work to an official-source or partner-review lane.

5

Track the case

Follow status, notes, reminders, review returns, artifacts, and client-facing progress from the dashboard.

Designed for clean boundaries.

Self-help document automation stays separate from official filing systems and professional legal review. That keeps the product useful without making unsafe promises.
