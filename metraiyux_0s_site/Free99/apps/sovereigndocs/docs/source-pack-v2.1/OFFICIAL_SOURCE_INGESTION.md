# Official Source Ingestion Guide

This library intentionally avoids copying proprietary private template libraries.

Use official/public sources to expand filing-related workflows:

## Federal courts

The U.S. Courts site provides national federal court forms and states that national court forms can be used in all federal courts. Local courts may maintain separate local forms.

Recommended SovereignDocs treatment: link to official forms, provide preparation worksheets, and avoid pretending worksheets are official court filings.

## IRS

The IRS publishes forms, instructions, and publications through its official forms portal.

Recommended SovereignDocs treatment: build trackers, request letters, intake flows, and checklists; link users to official IRS forms when a government form is required.

## USPTO

The USPTO is the official federal agency for patents and trademarks.

Recommended SovereignDocs treatment: create trademark/IP assignment outlines, brand-license agreements, evidence organizers, and filing checklists. Link to official USPTO filing resources when an official filing is required.

## State and county

For state and county court, business, DMV, real estate, and licensing forms:

1. Store a source record in `source-catalog.json`.
2. Mark whether it is official, public-domain, licensed, or attorney-created.
3. Do not copy private competitor text.
4. Keep official forms as linked resources unless you have permission or clear public-domain status.
