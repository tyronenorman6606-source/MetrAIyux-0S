# Official Source Strategy

SovereignDocs should not treat every document as the same kind of asset.

## Private original templates

Examples: NDA, independent contractor agreement, service agreement, board consent, demand letter, proposal acceptance, lease worksheet.

These are original templates assembled from SovereignDocs variables, clauses, questionnaires, review metadata, and jurisdiction overlays.

## Official-source workflows

Examples: IRS W-9, IRS SS-4, U.S. Courts national forms, USPTO Trademark Center forms, patent forms, state business filings, DMV forms, county court forms.

These should be prep packets and official-source routes unless a lawful reusable official fillable form process is verified.

## Why this matters

A private template can be drafted and reviewed. An official agency/court/tax form should stay tied to the current official source because the agency may change versions, instructions, filing rules, login requirements, fees, and submission pathways.
