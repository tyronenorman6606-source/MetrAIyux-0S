# SovereignDocs Disclaimers

## Platform disclaimer

SovereignDocs provides document automation, guided paperwork, templates, editing, signing, storage, and organization tools. SovereignDocs is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

## Template disclaimer

Templates are starting points. Laws, filing requirements, court rules, agency rules, local ordinances, consumer rules, employment rules, privacy rules, tax rules, and execution requirements can vary by jurisdiction and change over time.

## Professional review

Users should consult a licensed attorney, tax professional, financial professional, or authorized agency when a document affects legal rights, taxes, regulated industries, court filings, immigration, employment, housing, healthcare, estate planning, consumer rights, debt, privacy, or official filings.
