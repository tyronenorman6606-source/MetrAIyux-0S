# Partner Review Boundaries

The partner-review lane exists because users may want review from real legal teams. The platform must still avoid overclaiming.

Required acknowledgments:

- Not legal advice.
- Partner review terms.
- No guarantee of acceptance or outcome.
- SovereignDocs is not responsible for partner outcome.
- User is responsible for the accuracy of facts and final use.

High-risk records are still blocked from completed public export by default. Partner-review submission generates a packet or prep worksheet for review, not a completed public legal document.
