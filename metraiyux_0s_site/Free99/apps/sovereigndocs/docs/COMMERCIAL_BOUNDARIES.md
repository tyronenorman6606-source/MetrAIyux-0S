# Commercial Boundaries

SovereignDocs can collect facts, generate draft/prep packets, organize document packages, create official-source routes, record status events, and route users to configured legal/service partners.

SovereignDocs must not claim that it is performing legal services, guaranteeing approval, filing with agencies, serving as a registered agent, validating legal sufficiency, or accepting responsibility for partner work unless a separate live integration and contract proves that scope.

Public pages should use: prep, packet, worksheet, route, partner review, official-source, tracker, monitor, intake, dashboard, vault.

Public pages should avoid: guaranteed approval, attorney-reviewed unless actually reviewed by a partner and scoped, official filing by SovereignDocs, legal advice, valid in all states, state-compliant, court-ready.
