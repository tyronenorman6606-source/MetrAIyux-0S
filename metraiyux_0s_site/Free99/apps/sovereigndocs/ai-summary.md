# SovereignDocs AI Summary

SovereignDocs is a self-help document automation platform. It is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Current v6 build uses the v2.1 confidence-review library as source of truth:

- 10,200 generated template records
- 15 categories
- 51 jurisdictions
- 37 official-source prep workflows
- elevated-review records guarded before completed export

Runtime files:

- `template-library/manifest.json`
- `audit/publish-gates.json`
- `official-source-library/official-workflows.json`
- `review-workflow/review-queue-high-risk.json`
- `template-library/state-overlays-v2/US-AZ.json`

Do not summarize the platform as attorney-reviewed, state-compliant, court-ready, or an official filing engine.


V9 adds a legal partner review submission and routing lane with strict no-guarantee, no-legal-advice, and upstream-auth role boundaries.


V10 adds business formation prep, compliance tracking, registered-agent referral, trademark/IP prep, estate/life-planning worksheets, partner legal access, e-sign/vault, and order tracking. SovereignDocs remains document automation and partner routing only, not a law firm and not legal advice.
