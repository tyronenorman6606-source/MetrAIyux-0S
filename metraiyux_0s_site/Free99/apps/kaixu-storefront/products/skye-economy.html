Overview

The Skye Economy Command Layer is not just a checkout upgrade. It is a more complete operating model for how commerce, fulfillment, currency, founder credit, and member experiences should be separated and coordinated.

The root commerce worker handles storefront, order creation, checkout launches, webhook verification, ZIP seeding, signed downloads, and delivery email handling. On top of that, the attached release adds refund and dispute automation, tax and VAT quoting, versioned entitlements, analytics, provider smoke harnesses, and tests.

Commerce layer

Multi-rail payment automation

The attached worker supports Stripe, PayPal, Square, Lemon Squeezy, Coinbase Commerce, Gumroad, and direct admin-issued licenses instead of depending on one rail like a startled intern.

Automated fulfillment

Payment webhooks mark orders paid, ensure ZIPs exist in R2, mint licenses, create claim routes, and queue or send delivery email without the founder doing manual cleanup.

Hardening controls

Refund and dispute access policy, tax tables, entitlement manifests, smoke harnesses, and CI tests move the release out of hobby-commerce territory.

Seed hydration

Bundled ZIP assets can be hydrated into R2 lazily from internal seed paths, which keeps delivery closer to the worker and reduces brittle manual staging.

  • Signed worker downloads

    Buyers download through a signed worker route instead of a naked public bucket URL, which keeps delivery branded and access-controlled.

  • Provider manifest

    Admin can inspect a provider manifest endpoint to copy live webhook URLs and mapping keys into payment dashboards.

  • Retry-aware email queue

    Delivery email send and retry logic helps the commerce brain finish the handoff instead of quitting after payment.

  • Analytics and risk review

    The release explicitly calls out analytics and risk surfaces, which matters when a storefront is becoming a business asset instead of a demo.

Economy workers

This architecture locks the economy into separate Cloudflare brains. The Commerce Worker handles checkout, orders, and fulfillment. The SkyeCoin Worker manages wallet balances, mint, spend, freeze, packages, and circulation snapshots. The SkyeToken Worker manages founder treasury pools, grants, redemptions, freezes, and liability tracking.

Why separation matters

Separating commerce, currency, and treasury into dedicated workers is how a system grows without turning into an exhausted monolith. Each lane can be reasoned about, secured, and evolved without making the whole platform feel like a cursed pile of side effects.

Dedicated SkyeVerse gateway

The attached build also includes a dedicated SkyeVerse member gateway. That matters because SkyeVerse is not supposed to ride on Gateway13. It is meant to run on its own lane while reusing useful patterns from the sovereign control plane and kAIxU brain docs.

In the attached notes, the SkyeVerse gateway expands into banking, rewards, luxury-arcade engagement, service redemptions, and named arcade rooms. The important product signal is not the novelty of a room list. The important signal is the separation of member engagement into its own gateway surface rather than collapsing it into the commerce worker.

A serious economy stops pretending one worker should do everything forever.

This is a fit for founders and operators building branded digital economies, member systems, software sales, reward programs, or service-redemption flows who want the payments, currency, treasury, and member gateway surfaces to feel like an intentional architecture instead of a patch job.
