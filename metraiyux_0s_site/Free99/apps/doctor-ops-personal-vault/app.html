Doctor Ops Personal Vault · v6.0

A sellable personal-use doctor workflow vault that keeps data local by design.

This is positioned as a lightweight monthly utility, not an enterprise EHR replacement. It gives a doctor a private local command layer for office workflows: reminders, packets, follow-ups, notes, receipts, exports, restore points, and file-backed backups. No external database, telemetry, or third-party cloud sync is configured by default.

Open workflow floor
Designed for local-first personal workflow use. The optional Node runtime writes to data/platform-store.json and local data/backups/. This package does not claim HIPAA certification, medical advice, billing advice, or legal compliance.
