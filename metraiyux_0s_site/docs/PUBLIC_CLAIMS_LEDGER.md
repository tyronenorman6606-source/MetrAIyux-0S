## Claims The Site Must Not Make

- Do not claim fictional or sample cabinet members are real appointed officers unless those people are real, consented, and appointed.
- Do not claim government contract awards, SAM/UEI/CAGE/SBA/GSA status, insurance, bonding, or certifications without actual verification.
- Do not claim legal advice, tax advice, regulated professional services, employment-law compliance, or procurement advice.
- Do not claim fully autonomous legal, hiring, payment, tax, contracting, public-publishing, or external-send authority without human approval and professional review where required.
- Do not claim Free99 means anonymous access, no auth, no gate, or no owner control.
- Do not expose admin tokens, API keys, database URLs, mailbox proof internals, provider secrets, mirror secrets, or private customer/operator notes.
- Do not claim live provider integrations, production databases, payment movement, mailbox hosting, music distribution, cloud storage, GPS/navigation, or customer tenancy unless the specific route has current proof.
- Do not publish private review details, customer records, candidate records, uploaded files, or proof notes without consent and QA approval.
- Do not convert valuation ranges, model costs, internal calculator output, or financing examples into fixed public prices.
