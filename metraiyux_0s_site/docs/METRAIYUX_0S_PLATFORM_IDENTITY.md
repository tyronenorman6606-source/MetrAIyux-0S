# MetrAIyux 0S Platform Identity

## Official name

**MetrAIyux 0S**

## Positioning

MetrAIyux 0S is a protected autonomous business operating platform for founder-led companies. It combines owner-admin command, customer SaaS workspaces, tenant isolation, 13-cabinet workflows, approval-gated automation, Cloudflare deployment kits, Resend approval emails, and a 16-brain local/worker-ready operating model.

## Core tagline

Protected autonomous command for modern business.

## Product boundaries

- The owner/admin OS is for the platform owner only.
- Customer SaaS workspaces are tenant-scoped and cannot access owner credentials, production social connectors, or the Main Automation Brain directly.
- 0meg4kAI acts as security, QA, tenant boundary review, and system-audit assistant.
- The Main Automation Brain remains the owner-side orchestrator.
- External posting, outbound email, contract actions, pricing changes, hiring/firing, payments, legal/tax/filing-sensitive actions, and unsupported public claims require approval gates unless the operator intentionally changes the policy.
