# Default Support Mailbox Workflow

Mailbox: support@clientdomain.com

## Queues

- New Requests
- Waiting on Client
- Waiting on Internal Team
- Billing Question
- Urgent Issue
- Closed

## Auto-reply

Thanks for contacting us. We received your request and a team member will respond as soon as possible during business hours.

## Assignment rules

- Billing questions go to billing/admin.
- New service requests go to sales/operations.
- Existing customer issues go to support.
- Urgent outage/safety/customer-blocking issues get priority.

## Internal note standard

Every ticket should include the customer issue, next action, owner, and follow-up date before it is left open.
