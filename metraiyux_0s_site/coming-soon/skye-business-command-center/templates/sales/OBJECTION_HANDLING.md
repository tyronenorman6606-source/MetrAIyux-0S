# Objection Handling

## “Why not just use SaaS?”
Most SaaS tools charge separately for CRM, support, forms, invoices, and scheduling. This package gives the business a configured operations layer, one setup partner, and monthly support.

## “Is this custom software?”
It is a configured business operations deployment using proven open-source components plus a branded hub, setup, workflow design, hosting support, and maintenance.

## “Can you make it fully ours?”
The client gets their branding, domains, workflows, users, templates, and support process. Upstream open-source licenses remain respected.

## “Is it secure?”
It can be deployed with HTTPS, firewall controls, strong secrets, isolated client instances, backups, and access controls. It is not sold as a compliance-certified platform unless a separate compliance engagement is completed.
