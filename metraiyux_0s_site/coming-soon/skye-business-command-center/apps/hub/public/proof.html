Acceptance standard

Do not call it complete until these flows work.

This page is the operator-facing proof ledger. It protects the sale, the client, and the brand by separating “containers are up” from “business can use it.”

System proof

Infrastructure checks

  • Docker containers healthy
  • Domain and HTTPS active
  • SMTP verified
  • Backups run and archive exists
  • Health report saved to proof folder
Workflow proof

Business checks

  • Support email creates a ticket
  • Ticket can be assigned and replied to
  • Lead exists in CRM and can move stages
  • Estimate/invoice can be created
  • Intake form submission is visible

bash scripts/acceptance.sh
bash scripts/health-report.sh
