Connection exchange engine

Two QR paths, one serious field workflow.

Use the ConnectLog QR when someone should save your card directly into their ConnectLog. Use the phone-contact QR when they do not have the app and just need your contact card saved into their device.

ConnectLog QR

Opens the app or browser version and imports your exchange profile into the scanner’s local ConnectLog vault.

Phone Contact QR

Creates a standard vCard contact through the phone camera path, with no app required.

Scan or Paste Intake

Scan QR where supported, or paste a ConnectLog link, compact payload, or vCard when camera access is unavailable.

What ConnectLog does

A contact list is not enough. ConnectLog remembers the mission.

◇

Exchange profile onboarding

Save your name, company, role, email, phone, website, socials, location, note, and tags once, then use it everywhere.

⌗

QR sharing and intake

Generate ConnectLog import QR codes, phone-contact vCards, downloadable QR SVGs, and shareable profile cards.

✦

Relationship intelligence

Prioritize stale relationships, upcoming follow-ups, high-priority contacts, action queues, and daily mission briefs.

↗

Follow-up discipline

Track next follow-up dates, snooze people, mark contacted, create calendar reminders, and export a daily agenda.

◈

Vault portability

Export JSON, CSV, warm lists, all-contact vCards, and profile backups so your network is not trapped.

✶

Seed pack ingestion

Bulk-load curated contact packs and merge them into your local vault when your deployment is refreshed.

●

CSV business intake

Import scraped or curated business lists with common headers for name, company, role, email, phone, website, location, tags, and notes.

◆

Duplicate resolver

Find and merge matching records by email, phone, and name/company fingerprints without losing notes or timeline history.

Private by default

No account wall. No forced cloud vault.

ConnectLog stores its app data locally in the browser through IndexedDB. It is designed for private deployments, device-level app use, and exportable network memory.
