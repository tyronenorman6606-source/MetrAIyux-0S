# NEXUS Operator Runbook

Daily use:

1. Open the NEXUS Business Inbox.
2. Paste incoming business signals.
3. Route through the Site Operator Brain.
4. Review primary brain and secondary reviewer.
5. Assign a human owner.
6. Export the local ledger or persist through the Cloudflare Worker.
7. Use proof receipts when a public claim, client promise, or launch status changes.

Autonomy boundary: NEXUS routes and records. It does not spend money, sign contracts, approve legal claims, or publish regulated statements.
