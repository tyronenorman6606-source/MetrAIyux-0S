Commercial terms

Pricing, limits, and what each plan actually includes.

MetrAIyux 0S is sold as a managed company operating system, not as unlimited AI. Every paid lane includes a workspace, controlled activation after confirmed Stripe payment, AI/gateway metering, device controls, proof receipts, HouseOperations command lanes, SkyeBox local authenticator-vault handoff, sovereign stack options, SkyeMerit protected merit packs, relationship/messaging expansion, the paid SkyeRouteX workforce command lane, Free99 SkyeProfitConsole, SkyeMediaCenter, and Skye Content Forge access, and SkyeMusicNexus Lite as the Free99 music lane. SkyeMusicNexus Studio, Label Command, Managed Music Ops, drop add-ons, gated audio vault storage, content requests, and community campaign add-ons are paid buyable offers. Free99 means no charge only where a card says Free99; it never means anonymous access. Card checkout runs through SkyePay and Stripe; the webhook records payment status and activation follows the plan policy. Live provider actions still require configured production secrets.

How MetrAIyux is structured

The subscription is the operating-system fee. The setup fee pays for configuration and handoff. AI runs through FS27 at billable model rates, and the plan cap blocks runaway usage before it becomes a problem.

ai lane

AI is metered, not free

Every kAIxu call is counted against your plan's monthly credit pool. When the pool is exhausted FS27 hard-stops the call — no surprise overages, no runaway spend.

cap lane

Caps protect the offer

Starter, Growth, and Autonomous plans stop at their AI spend cap unless the customer buys a top-up, upgrades, or signs custom terms.

stack lane

Sovereign add-ons expand revenue

CitadelDB, SkyeVault, SkyeMail, HouseOperations, SkyeBox, ConnectLog, Relay13, SkyeRouteX, SkyeMusicNexus, and Skye Content Forge create owned lanes for database, files/repos/docs, business email, house command, authenticator custody handoff, relationship capture, messaging infrastructure, workforce route command, music operations, and content publishing instead of handing every dollar to outside platforms.

kAIxu 6.7 AI rate card

All AI inference runs through kAIxu 6.7 — a sovereign model owned and operated by Skyes Over London LC. FS27 meters credits per call. 1 credit = $0.01 billable compute. Your plan's monthly credit pool is the cap; FS27 hard-stops at the limit. Customers never interact directly with the underlying provider infrastructure.
