Commercial terms

Pricing, limits, and what each plan actually includes.

MetrAIyux 0S is sold as a managed company operating system, not as unlimited AI. Every paid lane includes a workspace, automatic activation after confirmed Stripe payment, AI/gateway metering, device controls, proof receipts, sovereign stack options, and a defined monthly usage policy. Card checkout runs through SkyePay and Stripe; the webhook unlocks the workspace when the transaction is confirmed.

How MetrAIyux is structured

The subscription is the operating-system fee. The setup fee pays for configuration and handoff. AI runs through FS27 at billable model rates, and the plan cap blocks runaway usage before it becomes a problem.

ai lane

AI is metered, not free

Every kAIxu call is counted against your plan's monthly credit pool. When the pool is exhausted FS27 hard-stops the call — no surprise overages, no runaway spend.

cap lane

Caps protect the offer

Starter, Growth, and Autonomous plans stop at their AI spend cap unless the customer buys a top-up, upgrades, or signs custom terms.

stack lane

Sovereign add-ons expand revenue

CitadelDB, SkyeVault, and SkyeMail create owned lanes for database, files/repos/docs, and business email instead of handing every dollar to outside platforms.

kAIxu 6.7 AI rate card

All AI inference runs through kAIxu 6.7 — a sovereign model owned and operated by Skyes Over London LC. FS27 meters credits per call. 1 credit = $0.01 billable compute. Your plan's monthly credit pool is the cap; FS27 hard-stops at the limit. Customers never interact directly with the underlying provider infrastructure.

Sovereign stack choices

The 0S can run as a true owner-controlled stack. Neon, Google Drive, Gmail, and GitHub-style storage can stay available as outside lanes, but the sovereign path is CitadelDB, SkyeVault, SkyeMail, SkyePay, and FS27.

database

CitadelDB

Owner can choose the sovereign database lane instead of Neon. FS27 tracks database lane selection, migration, verification, and cutover events.

vault

SkyeVault

Files, proof exports, customer docs, repo packages, and handoff packs can live in the Vault with plan-scoped storage and file limits.

email

SkyeMail

Workspace inboxes, mailbox keys, approval-sensitive sends, and business email events run through the sovereign mail lane.

gate

FS27 mirror

The 0S logs locally and mirrors action events to FS27 so customer activity, billable actions, privileged actions, and AI usage stay visible.
