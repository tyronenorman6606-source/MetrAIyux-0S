Billing

Billing routes through SkyePay.

SkyePay is our house payment gateway for setup, monthly access, and preview closeout. Stripe handles Checkout, SkyeMerit lowers only eligible spend bands when applied, then FS27 records paid status and holds the workspace for owner-approved activation. SkyeProfitConsole and Skye Split Engine are Free99, meaning no charge, so they stay outside paid checkout while still requiring a gate session.
