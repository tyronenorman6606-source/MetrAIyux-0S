Tenant Provisioning

Production provisioning path.

This is the operator-facing checklist for turning a signup into a real customer workspace. Tenant provisioning, white-label resale, branded portals, custom domains, and buyer-owned platform mirroring are paid Enterprise / Managed Gate or owner-approved quote scope, not Free99.

  1. Customer submits signup and selects a plan; Free99 preview users do not receive tenant/resale provisioning.
  2. A SkyePay handoff is recorded. Production activation waits for paid status, invoice approval, or written Enterprise / Managed Gate approval.
  3. 0S SaaS Worker creates customer and workspace records in the configured 0S storage lane.
  4. Selected services, plan limits, and shared-gate workspace identity are saved with the customer record.
  5. Command Bridge receives the customer-visible workspace and action receipts.
  6. SkyeMail is requested only when a real mailbox identity is supplied or selected.
  7. Customer dashboard becomes available behind the shared FS27/SkyGate/Free99 session.
  8. Site Operator Brain routes first command to the correct cabinet brain.

Provisioning boundary

Free99 can open gated app surfaces, but it cannot provision a buyer's resale SaaS. Custom tenant policies, AI caps, hosted custody, external service routes, and branded workspace cloning must be written into the paid plan or quote before this checklist runs.
