AI usage is priced through FS27

AI usage runs through kAIxu 6.7 — a sovereign AI model owned and operated by Skyes Over London LC. FS27 meters every call against your plan's AI credit allowance. 1 credit = $0.01 billable compute. Your plan includes a monthly credit pool; FS27 hard-stops at the limit.

What happens after payment

1. Stripe Checkout

SkyePay creates the Checkout Session and stores the order, offer, customer email, line items, and policy snapshot.

2. Automatic unlock

When Stripe confirms the SkyePay transaction, FS27 marks the workspace unlocked without a manual owner step.

3. Gate policy

The confirmed plan writes monthly cap, RPM, RPD, device, model, workspace, file, and storage rules onto the customer record.

4. Enforcement

FS27 checks those rules before AI, embedding, stream, job, vault, and protected command usage.
