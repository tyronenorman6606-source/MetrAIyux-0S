What is public knowledge now

Payments route through our own gateway.

Client continuation

After a private preview, clients continue through a named offer with visible setup, monthly price, included usage, request limits, and activation terms.

Owner approval gate

Confirmed Stripe payment records the managed workspace as paid pending owner approval. FS27 writes the order, plan policy, and approval state onto the customer record.

Stripe backed

Card handling remains inside Stripe Checkout. SkyePay owns the route, offer metadata, order status, and workspace handoff state.

Callable API

Apps can call offers, checkout, and status routes directly through the SkyePay API contract and browser SDK; customer access still routes through FS27 limits.

Free99 boundary

SkyeProfitConsole is no charge and does not create a paid SkyePay product. It still requires a 0S or FS27 gate session before app boot and runtime actions.

AI billing truth

SkyePay unlocks the workspace; FS27 enforces the spend.

AI usage runs through kAIxu 6.7 — a sovereign AI model owned and operated by Skyes Over London LC. The AI credit pool on each plan is a metered FS27 billable cap. When the pool is exhausted, FS27 hard-stops the call. Rate card is disclosed on the pricing page.

Rate card

kAIxu 6.7 variants (nano, mini, 6.7, pro, max) are priced per 1M input/output tokens through FS27. Full rate card on the pricing page.

Cap lock

Monthly AI caps, RPM, RPD, device policy, and model allowlists are written after confirmed payment.

Top-up path

Usage above caps requires a paid top-up, plan upgrade, or enterprise scope.

Event mirror

Checkout, unlock, workspace, command, and action events mirror into FS27 when configured.

Sovereign stack choices attach to the paid workspace: CitadelDB can replace Neon, SkyeVault can replace Google Drive/GitHub-style storage, and SkyeMail can replace Gmail-only business email.
