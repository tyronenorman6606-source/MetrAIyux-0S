Customer workspace safety

Customer workspaces are isolated from the owner company OS.

This page explains what customers can use, what they cannot touch, and how 0meg4kAI reviews customer-side commands before any privileged action happens.

Customer can access

Their own dashboard, onboarding data, service selections, customer command queue, customer documents, customer-specific tasks, and customer-safe brain summaries.

Customer cannot access

Owner Main Automation Brain, owner social connectors, admin tutorial internals, global proof vault, all-tenant ledgers, Cloudflare secrets, admin Worker endpoints, founder/admin private materials.

0meg4kAI review

Customer commands are screened for unsafe actions, cross-tenant data access, public posting, legal/finance/HR risk, pricing changes, and owner-credential requests.

Approval flow

Risky items become approval requests. Safe items stay customer-scoped. Unsafe items are quarantined or rejected.

Customer-safe autonomy

Customers can set up a company workspace and run their own operating workflow. They do not receive control of the owner’s company, owner credentials, or production connectors.

Owner/admin layer and customer SaaS layer are intentionally separated. Live enforcement requires deployed auth, Worker gates, D1/KV bindings, and connector policies.
