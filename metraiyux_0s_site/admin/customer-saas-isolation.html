Owner/customer separation

Customer SaaS must stay separate from the owner company OS.

This page defines how the public SaaS layer, customer workspaces, and admin/orchestrator brain are separated so customers cannot damage your company systems or use your production credentials.

Separation rule

The customer SaaS layer is a product surface. The owner/admin layer is the company command system. They must not share authority, credentials, connectors, ledgers, or unrestricted brain access.

Owner/Admin Layer

Main Automation Brain, 0meg4kAI, owner social connectors, Resend owner approval email, all-company ledgers, admin tutorial, proof vault, deployment keys, governance docs, Cloudflare admin Worker.

Customer SaaS Layer

Signup, customer onboarding, company profile, service selector, customer dashboard, workspace-scoped commands, customer approval emails, customer data, customer exports, customer-specific connectors.

Shared only by policy

Public docs, generic cabinet methods, sanitized service templates, customer-safe routing summaries, non-secret knowledge chunks, and support handoff forms.

Never shared

ADMIN_TOKEN, RESEND_API_KEY, owner social OAuth, global D1 admin ledgers, root Worker routes, founder/admin private docs, production publish permissions, cross-tenant data.

Deployment pattern

Recommended: deploy the public website and customer SaaS under the public domain, deploy admin routes behind your upstream auth or Cloudflare Access, and keep admin Worker secrets in a separate Worker from customer SaaS provisioning. Customer commands call the SaaS Worker. The SaaS Worker calls 0meg4kAI policy first. Only approved/sanitized work creates admin-facing review receipts.

Owner/admin layer and customer SaaS layer are intentionally separated. Live enforcement requires deployed auth, Worker gates, D1/KV bindings, and connector policies.
