Important operating truth

This brain can route, draft, queue, record, and dispatch only when the Worker and provider connectors are actually configured. Without credentials, it produces approved drafts and receipts. Public posting, email sending, contracts, payments, hiring/firing, filings, and legal/tax actions must stay approval-gated unless you deliberately change the policy.
