Next major unlock — retained customer proof

SkyeMail, CitadelDB, SkyePay, FS27, SkyeNet, SkyeMusicNexus, RouteX, Client App Factory, Free99, SovereignDocs, Valley Verified, and MCP lanes are already counted in the engineering replacement and portfolio value. The next valuation unlock is not another internal component count; it is retained customer proof: paid checkout, onboarding, repeated workspace usage, working connector depth, and at least one full billing cycle. That is what converts the $13.5M-$39M code-backed founder range into a revenue-multiple company valuation.

Cost-to-run estimate after deployment

Lean starting stack: Cloudflare Workers Paid, Pages, D1, KV/Queues, and Resend. Cloudflare static assets and Worker routing can stay inexpensive at early traffic; Resend can start free or move to Pro when approval and lifecycle emails increase. Social, CRM, payment, SMS, and external AI providers add separate costs.

Remaining value blockers

  • No paying customers means the valuation is still asset/product value, not revenue-multiple value.
  • The reported $6K Merser access interest is pipeline validation only until payment, retention, and real client usage are proven.
  • Billing, auth, and customer provisioning must be proven in live customer use before calling it a mature SaaS.
  • Real-money payout execution requires completed contractor information, owner approval, and selected payout-provider activation; the contractor packet, inbox, notification, and approval-state lane is live.
  • Connector usage must stay approval-gated and logged.
  • Owner/admin secrets must remain outside customer scope; this is where 0meg4kAI increases value.
