Pre-audit baseline (2026-05-15)

$85,000–$220,000 was the prior estimate before the E2E audit. At that point, approval emails were silently failing (unverified domain), SkyeGate auth was returning 404 internally, and two brain routing collisions were undetected. This reflects the full website, public overview site, live Cloudflare Pages URL, full-system Worker route, executive cabinet system, 17-brain architecture, admin tutorial, customer SaaS layer, Worker kits, D1 schemas, approval email design, tenant-isolation logic, documentation, proof receipts, sitemaps, and launch materials. It is still discounted because paying customer traction, retained usage, billing, and connector depth are not yet proven.

Live deployed platform value

$275,000-$750,000 is the stronger deployed-platform band once the live public overview, full website route, D1-backed Worker layer, protected admin flow, Resend approval path, customer signup/onboarding, tenant isolation, and smoke receipts are all treated as one operating asset. The newly deployed public overview increases value because it is a sendable sales and diligence surface, not just source code in a ZIP.

Live SaaS with traction

$750,000-$3.5M+ becomes realistic only with paying customers, MRR/ARR, low churn, proof of onboarding, logs showing repeat usage, working connectors, and retained customer workspaces. At that point, valuation should shift from replacement-cost/productized-asset logic to ARR multiple logic.

Low traction

$5K-$15K MRR: early validation, likely $300K-$900K depending on retention, founder involvement, and service load.

Working micro-SaaS

$20K-$50K MRR: stronger buyer interest, likely $1M-$3M if churn is controlled and automations reduce support load.

Serious SaaS asset

$100K+ MRR: ARR multiples can become the main valuation method if revenue is recurring, margins are strong, and usage is defensible.

Remaining value blockers

  • No paying customers means the valuation is still asset/product value, not revenue-multiple value.
  • Billing, auth, and customer provisioning must be proven in live customer use before calling it a mature SaaS.
  • Connector usage must stay approval-gated and logged.
  • Owner/admin secrets must remain outside customer scope; this is where 0meg4kAI increases value.
