16th brain / customer firewall

0meg4kAI protects the owner brain and customer layer.

This is the security, QA, and tenant-isolation assistant brain. Customer activity must pass through 0meg4kAI before it can reach privileged owner automation, production social connectors, admin ledgers, or approval-sensitive workflows.

Purpose

0meg4kAI acts as the QA/security assistant for the Main Automation Brain. It screens customer-side commands, detects privilege escalation, requires approval when needed, and writes receipts.

Customer firewall

Customers can use their own SaaS workspace, onboarding, services, commands, and customer-scoped brains. They cannot command your owner orchestrator, post through your social accounts, read admin logs, or access owner credentials.

Approval decisioning

0meg4kAI decides whether a customer or internal action is safe to queue, must be approval-gated, should be quarantined, or should be rejected.

Security scans

The lightweight scan checks path scope, command language, target brain, connector intent, public-claim risk, HR/legal/finance sensitivity, and tenant-boundary violations.

0meg4kAI Review Console

Paste a customer command or automation request. This browser-local scan mirrors the Worker decision model.



Allowed vs blocked
Allowed customer-scoped
Company setup, service selection, customer onboarding, customer task routing, customer content drafts, internal customer notes, customer approval inbox, customer-specific exports.
Blocked from customers
Owner admin brain, founder/admin credentials, owner social connectors, production publishing keys, all-tenant ledgers, private admin docs, public-claim approval, pricing authority, HR/legal/finance decisions.
Owner/admin layer and customer SaaS layer are intentionally separated. Live enforcement requires deployed auth, Worker gates, D1/KV bindings, and connector policies.
