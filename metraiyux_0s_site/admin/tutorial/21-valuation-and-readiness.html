21. Valuation and Readiness

How to understand what the asset is worth now versus what it may be worth after deployment and real usage.

Lesson steps

Current value

Value now is mostly build replacement cost, IP packaging, sales utility, and demo asset value.

Deployment value

Value after deployment increases when auth, Worker, D1, Resend, connectors, and proof logs are live.

Revenue value

The biggest valuation jump happens when customers, recurring revenue, usage logs, retention, and margins exist.

Risk discount

Unverified autonomy, fictional personas, unconnected connectors, and no paying users reduce valuation.

Proof premium

Live workflows, client proof, admin receipts, social publishing, and real pipeline records increase valuation.

Operator checklist

  • Valuation page reviewed
  • Deployment checklist completed
  • Proof receipts exported
  • Revenue assumptions separated from actual revenue

Proof rule

Do not mark this lesson complete until the related page, endpoint, token, or connector has been opened and tested. Local receipts are acceptable for demo mode; live claims require Worker/D1/provider proof.
