Admin tutorial lesson 27

Customer SaaS Provisioning Walkthrough

This lesson walks the admin through the customer signup and workspace provisioning path.

Flow

  1. Visitor lands on public pricing or SaaS page.
  2. Visitor signs up through the customer signup flow.
  3. Payment/auth provider confirms account status.
  4. Provisioning Worker creates customer, workspace, subscription, service selections, and connector placeholders.
  5. 0meg4kAI creates an isolation receipt and checks for unsafe privilege scope.
  6. Customer enters their dashboard and only sees their own workspace.
  7. Owner/admin sees customer state from the admin OS, not by exposing owner controls to customer.

Deployment proof

Before selling as self-serve, prove signup, billing intent, workspace creation, tenant-scoped command routing, approval email delivery, and customer logout/access-denied behavior.

Demonstrative autonomous business OS. Live production requires deployed Workers, D1/KV/Queues, auth, secrets, provider connectors, and verified approval policy.
