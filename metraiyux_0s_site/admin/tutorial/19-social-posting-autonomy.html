19. Social Posting Autonomy

How to let the business draft, review, queue, and publish posts without fake autonomy claims.

Lesson steps

Draft automatically

The Marketing Brain drafts posts from approved service pages, blogs, proof receipts, and company doctrine.

QA review

The Quality Brain checks for fake guarantees, unsupported claims, internal notes, and compliance-sensitive language.

Approval email

The Main Automation Brain sends the draft to the admin for approval through Resend.

Connector dispatch

Once social API credentials are configured, the Worker can dispatch approved posts to the provider connector.

Receipt logging

Every draft, approval, publish event, and failure should create a D1 audit receipt.

Operator checklist

  • Social provider OAuth/API keys configured
  • Default mode set to approval-required
  • Test post uses a private/sandbox account first
  • Proof receipt generated after publish

Proof rule

Do not mark this lesson complete until the related page, endpoint, token, or connector has been opened and tested. Local receipts are acceptable for demo mode; live claims require Worker/D1/provider proof.
