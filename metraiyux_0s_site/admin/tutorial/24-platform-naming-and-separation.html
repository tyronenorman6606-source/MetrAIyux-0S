Admin tutorial lesson 24

Platform Naming and Layer Separation

The platform must have one public product identity while keeping owner/admin power separate from customer workspaces.

What to remember

The owner/admin layer is your company command layer. It contains production credentials, social connectors, approval ledgers, and the Main Automation Brain. Customers must never access this layer.

The customer SaaS layer is their workspace. It lets them sign up, onboard, configure their company, choose services, submit commands, and use customer-scoped automation.

0meg4kAI sits between these layers as security, QA, tenant-isolation reviewer, and approval-risk classifier.

Naming rule

Choose one public name, then keep the internal names stable. Recommended: CabinetOS or SovereignOffice OS for the public platform; Main Automation Brain for owner orchestration; 0meg4kAI for QA/security; Customer Cabinet Workspace for tenant accounts.

Demonstrative autonomous business OS. Live production requires deployed Workers, D1/KV/Queues, auth, secrets, provider connectors, and verified approval policy.
