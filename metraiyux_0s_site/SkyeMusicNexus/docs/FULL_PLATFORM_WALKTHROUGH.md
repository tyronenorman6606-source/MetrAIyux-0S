# SkyeMusicNexus Full Platform Walkthrough

This is the plain written packet for the guided walkthrough at `public/walkthrough.html`.

## 1. Listener First Pass

Start at `index.html`. The home surface gives the artist or listener the live catalog, current player, queue, playlists, trending songs, new drops, genre stations, artist discovery, release feed preview, and collective owner context.

Open `public/discover.html` for playlists, charts, new drops, genre lanes, and artist movement. Open `public/radio.html` when the user wants the whole catalog to play through the persistent Nexus player.

## 2. Artist Launch Pass

Open `public/index.html`. This is the Artist Workspace room map. It links to signup, create, DAW, discover, radio, streams, awards, feed, upload, player, releases, rights, exchange, drops, artist apps, store, and brain.

Open `public/signup.html` to begin artist intake. The launch record should collect or confirm artist identity, Skye ID, artist photo, contact details, paperwork status, SkyePay tracking, and owner review state. Payment and identity fields stay locked after launch setup because payout and proof records depend on stable artist references.

## 3. Creation Pass

Open `public/create.html` to choose the creation move. From there, open `public/daw.html` for the native creation room. The DAW supports transport, arrangement, tracks, mixer, pads, keys, audio import/decode/preview, mic and MIDI hooks, browser mixdown, project save, and manifest export.

Use `public/stems.html` to stage stems, references, arrangement notes, and gated project save handoff. Use `public/exports.html` to build a portable project packet for Release Forge.

## 4. Upload And Release Pass

Open `public/upload.html` when the artist has owned or licensed audio. Upload Studio handles large audio intake, protected audio vault records, generated track lines, and handoff into Release Forge and the player.

Open `public/releases.html` to create artist records, release capsules, credits, Royalty River context, and operations sequencing. Open `public/rights.html` before public promotion to record ownership, preview-use, distribution boundaries, and takedown holds.

## 5. Social And Fan Pass

Open `public/feed.html` for story-style release posts, community feed work, saves, boosts, comments, and provider-safe publishing queues.

Open `public/drops.html` to build song pages, album pages, private delivery pages, batch release packages, approval queues, and publish packaging.

Open `public/artist-apps.html` when an approved artist universe needs an installable fan app with share links, video loops, store paths, and Nexus return paths.

## 6. Business Pass

Open `public/store.html` to build digital products, merch, tickets, tips, bookings, memberships, order intents, and SkyePay checkout routes. No payout should be represented as cleared until paperwork and owner approval are complete.

Open `public/stream-analytics.html` to review source-labeled listening: human, radio, native, and local-brain movement. Open `public/achievements.html` to show artist and track milestone awards. Open `public/brain.html` for artist memory, local cycles, SkyeMeter, tools, giveaways, and activity loops.

## 7. Protected Review Pass

Open `public/admin.html` only through the shared 0S gate. Protected Review carries owner review, payout queue, analytics prism, exchange console, and capsule wall.
