Overview

Bookkeeping Catch-Up System: Turning Backlog Into a Project Plan

A milestone-based catch-up workflow that converts messy backlogs into predictable delivery phases.

🏷️

### Client Snapshot

Small business with months of unfiled transactions.

🧩

### The Challenge

Catch-up bookkeeping felt endless without structure or clear requirements.

🧠

### Core Outcome

A milestone-based catch-up workflow that converts messy backlogs into predictable delivery phases.

Execution

Solution & Build Details

A structured delivery—built for clarity, speed, and operational control.

⚙️

### What We Did
- Created catch-up intake checklist for required inputs.
- Defined milestone phases from reconcile → categorize → review → deliver.
- Added client-facing progress summaries to reduce anxiety.

🧱

### What We Built
- Catch-up checklist (accounts, statements, POS exports, payroll reports)
- Milestone plan and 'done means done' definitions
- Executive progress summary template
