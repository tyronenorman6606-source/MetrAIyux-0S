Phoenix Roofer: Storm-Season Lead Capture Without Phone Tag

Emergency intake + proof checklist to triage storm damage fast, reduce missed calls, and keep peak season profitable.

🏷️

### Client Snapshot

Roofing contractor serving multiple Phoenix-area zip codes.

🧩

### The Challenge

Peak-season calls overwhelmed the team; missed calls meant missed revenue and slow triage.

🧠

### Core Outcome

Emergency intake + proof checklist to triage storm damage fast, reduce missed calls, and keep peak season profitable.

Execution

Solution & Build Details

A structured delivery—built for clarity, speed, and operational control.

⚙️

### What We Did
- Designed a fast 'damage + eligibility' intake with service-area gating.
- Added photo-proof instructions so triage decisions happen quickly.
- Published a clear 'what happens next' workflow to set expectations.

🧱

### What We Built
- Emergency intake flow (leak, storm damage, inspection)
- Proof checklist (photos, insurance status, timeline, address)
- Expectations page that reduces repetitive calls

Implementation

Stack & Timeline

Deployment-first, Drop-ready, and structured for iteration.
