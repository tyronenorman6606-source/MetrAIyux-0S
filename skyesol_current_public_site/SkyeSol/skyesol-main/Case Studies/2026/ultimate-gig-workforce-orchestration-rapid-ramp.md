### Client Snapshot

Confidential workforce initiative requiring fast ramp-up and consistent worker readiness.

🧩

### The Challenge

Workforce programs break when intake is messy, scheduling is inconsistent, and accountability isn’t measurable.

🧠

### Core Outcome

A combined staffing pipeline + intake + reporting program that scales workforce supply without operational chaos.

Execution

Integrated Delivery

Multi-lane execution—operations, systems, trust surfaces, and automation working together as one program.

### What We Did
- Built applicant intake and readiness checklist structure.
- Defined scheduling/availability capture and escalation rules.
- Created performance visibility and feedback loop.
- Aligned messaging templates for consistency across coordinators.

🧱

### What We Built
- Worker intake + readiness checklist framework
- Scheduling workflow and exception handling (no-shows, cancellations)
- Coordinator playbook + templates
- Weekly performance reporting format
