### The Challenge

On-demand networks face volatility: incomplete records, inconsistent closeouts, and exception chaos degrade reliability fast.

### Core Outcome

Operational enablement across on-demand delivery: standardized records, exception control, and proof discipline to keep programs stable at scale.

### What We Did
- Defined standardized records and minimum-required fields for consistent dispatch and closeout.
- Built exception categories with escalation cadence and owner clarity.
- Created proof artifact requirements and closeout gates for billing readiness.
- Installed performance review rhythm to reduce repeat issues.

### What We Built
- Minimum data contract for delivery records
- Exception taxonomy + escalation ladder
- Proof artifact rules + closeout checklist
- Performance review cadence and templates
