### Client Snapshot

Confidential multi-team organization struggling with handoffs and scope drift.

🧩

### The Challenge

Most organizations lose margin and reputation in the gap between sold and delivered.

🧠

### Core Outcome

A unified program connecting sales intake, scope control, delivery workflow, and executive reporting—so deals don’t die in handoff.

Execution

Integrated Delivery

Multi-lane execution—operations, systems, trust surfaces, and automation working together as one program.

⚙️

### What We Did
- Installed proof-pack offers and requirements checklists for scope clarity.
- Built handoff packs that define minimum inputs before work starts.
- Created status lanes and executive reporting outputs.
- Defined change-control workflow for scope changes.

🧱

### What We Built
- Proof-pack offer templates + requirements checklists
- Lead handoff pack + completeness gate
- Status tracker pattern (intake → active → delivered)
- Change-control workflow and templates
