Overview

FreightQuoteNavigator: Turning Quote Requests into a Pipeline

A quote-first funnel that captures dispatch-ready shipment details and converts random inquiries into structured, workable leads.

🏷️

### Client Snapshot

Logistics broker-style operation needing consistent quote intake.

🧩

### The Challenge

Quote requests arrived via texts, calls, and random emails—killing speed, losing details, and making follow-up inconsistent.

🧠

### Core Outcome

A quote-first funnel that captures dispatch-ready shipment details and converts random inquiries into structured, workable leads.

Execution

Solution & Build Details

A structured delivery—built for clarity, speed, and operational control.

⚙️

### What We Did
- Built a quote-first funnel capturing pickup/dropoff, commodity, weight, equipment type, and timing.
- Added required-fields logic to prevent incomplete requests.
- Implemented instant confirmation and next-step messaging to accelerate response.

🧱

### What We Built
- Quote intake form engineered for dispatch realities
- Required-fields logic to prevent incomplete requests
- Instant confirmation + next-step messaging
- Lead organization structure for rapid follow-ups

Stack & Timeline
