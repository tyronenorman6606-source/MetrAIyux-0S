## The Problem: “It Worked Last Week” Is Not Reliability

Reliability means the system performs under pressure — not just on a calm day. Without access boundaries and audit trails, teams drift into invisible risk and unprovable delivery.

Phoenix customers expect fast response and clear updates. When scheduling is sloppy and records are missing, churn rises and disputes multiply.

Healthcare anchors are referenced for Phoenix relevance only. The lesson is the operational posture: prove what happened, restrict what should be restricted, and keep critical paths stable.
