## The Problem: Heat Turns “Normal Scheduling” Into Risk

Phoenix isn’t just hot — it’s operationally different. Field crews face safety constraints, hydration requirements, reduced midday productivity, and higher customer frustration when time windows slip.

Most businesses try to handle this with rules in someone’s head. That fails at scale. Heat resilience must be enforced by the system: scheduling logic, safety windows, dispatch rules, and proof records.

> Operators don’t “get motivated.” They reduce friction until output becomes inevitable. — SKAIXU.SYSTEM principle

## The System: Safety Windows + Dispatch Discipline

SkyeSol heat‑resilient deployments build scheduling around reality: earlier starts, shorter midday windows, confirmation routines, and proof capture that defends the work.
