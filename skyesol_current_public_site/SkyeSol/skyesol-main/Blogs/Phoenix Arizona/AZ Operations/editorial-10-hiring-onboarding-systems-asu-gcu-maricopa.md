## The Problem: Hiring Fails at Onboarding

Most organizations hire people and then improvise onboarding. That creates inconsistency: different training, different expectations, and unpredictable performance. In Phoenix markets, churn is expensive.

If you can’t prove what a new hire learned, and when, you can’t manage quality. Workforce ops needs the same posture as delivery ops: records, checklists, and measurable throughput.

> Operators don’t “get motivated.” They reduce friction until output becomes inevitable. — SKAIXU.SYSTEM principle

## The System: Funnel → Onboarding → Training Proof → Dashboards

SkyeSol installs workforce systems that treat onboarding like a production pipeline: each step has inputs, outputs, and proof. That allows teams to scale contractors or employees without quality collapsing.

#### Recruiting Funnel

#### Onboarding Checklist
