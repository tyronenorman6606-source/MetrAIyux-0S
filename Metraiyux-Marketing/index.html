The Product

A complete operating layer your company actually runs on.

MetrAIyux 0S is not a project management tool or a chatbot wrapper. It's a structured command routing system — built on Cloudflare's global edge — that routes work to the right function, gates risky actions behind human approval, and keeps a proof receipt of everything that happens.

🧠

16-Brain Command Routing

Every command your company runs goes through a named functional brain — CEO/Ops, Sales, Legal, Finance, HR, Technology, Government Relations, and more. Each brain routes to its domain, logs a receipt, and flags anything that needs a human sign-off before it moves.

🔒

0meg4kAI Security Gateway

A real two-layer security scanner that runs in the browser and again at the Cloudflare Worker level. It inspects commands for privilege escalation, cross-tenant boundary violations, legal risk, and financial exposure. It doesn't guess — it blocks, quarantines, or escalates with documented reasoning.

✋

Hard Approval Gates

Contracts, payments, hiring decisions, legal filings, and public claims will never move without a human approval. This is not a setting you configure — it's a hard boundary baked into every brain and every Worker endpoint. You cannot accidentally automate something that should have a person on it.

🏢

13-Cabinet Operating Model

A documented executive structure with defined authority, delegation ledgers, governance center, and resolution language — all ready to present in a contract bid, a diligence review, or a government capability statement. One company, 13 defined operating functions.

📋

Proof Receipt System

Every routed command generates a timestamped JSON receipt with routing decision, brain assignment, approval status, and proof requirements. You get a lightweight compliance audit trail without buying compliance software.

🔑

SkyeGate Auth Bridge

Token introspection against the SkyeGate identity platform. Bearer headers, role/scope claim validation, email allowlists, multi-path fallback. The admin layer is access-controlled by a real identity system, not a password box.

👥

Multi-Tenant Client Workspaces

Each customer gets an isolated workspace enforced at the Worker level. 0meg4kAI scans every customer command at runtime to verify it cannot reach owner-scoped brains, production connectors, or other tenants' data. Tenant isolation is structural, not policy-based.

⚡

Cloudflare Edge Backend

Six independent Cloudflare Workers — Admin Automation, SaaS Provisioning, Security Gateway, Site Operator, CROWN, and NEXUS — with D1 SQL for persistent storage, KV for fast lookup, and Queues for async dispatch. All wrangler.toml configured and ready to deploy.

💳

SaaS Billing & Provisioning

Stripe checkout sessions, customer signup, company profile, service selector, workspace setup, and billing intent — all wired. Wire in your Stripe keys and you have a subscription business with self-serve onboarding from day one.

Honest Technical Note

The brain routing layer uses structured keyword classification, not large language model inference. That is intentional — it makes behavior deterministic, auditable, and deployable without API costs or rate limits. The brains know their domain, route to the right function, and log receipts. They do not hallucinate. If you want an LLM layer on top, the Worker architecture is designed to accept an Ollama or OpenAI-compatible endpoint — but the platform runs and provides value without one.

How It Works

Deploy in hours. Operate for years.

The static layer deploys to Cloudflare Pages in under 30 minutes. The Workers deploy to the Cloudflare edge with wrangler. No build step, no server, no devops team required.

1

Deploy the static site to Cloudflare Pages

Drag the folder or connect your git repo. The full 522-page operating site — marketing, admin, sales enablement, client portal, blog, knowledge base — goes live immediately. No build step. Zero config.

2

Deploy the 6 Cloudflare Workers with wrangler

Each Worker has a configured wrangler.toml, D1 migration scripts, and KV namespace definitions. Add your environment secrets (SkyeGate token, Resend key, Stripe keys) and deploy. D1 databases provision automatically.

3

Protect the admin layer with SkyeGate + Cloudflare Access

Add a Cloudflare Access rule in front of /admin/* and configure your SkyeGate token for the admin brain. Now the routing and approval layer is access-controlled by a real identity system, not public-facing.

4

Run your company through the command layer

Use the admin brain chat to route work, generate approval requests, and produce receipts. Every action hits the 0meg4kAI security scanner before execution. Anything that touches contracts, payments, hiring, legal, or public publishing goes to the approval inbox first.

5

Onboard clients or resell as your own product

Add customers through the SaaS provisioning flow. Each customer gets an isolated workspace, their own brain routing context, and 0meg4kAI tenant isolation at the Worker level. Or white-label the entire platform and sell it as your own sovereign business OS.

Why Operators Trust It

It earns trust because it admits what it won't do.

The most dangerous systems are the ones that will do anything you ask. MetrAIyux 0S has explicit hard limits that cannot be configured away. Here's what makes that credible:

🚫

No autonomous contracts

No brain, Worker, or routing event will produce, sign, or send a contract without a human approval receipt. Period.

🚫

No autonomous payments

Any command touching payments, transfers, invoices, or financial commitments goes to the approval inbox. The system logs it, escalates it, and waits.

🚫

No autonomous hiring or firing

HR decisions — offers, terminations, placements — require explicit human sign-off. The brain routes them, the approval gate holds them.

🚫

No autonomous public publishing

Social posts, press releases, public claims, and external communications are gated. 0meg4kAI flags them. A human has to release them.

📋

Every decision is logged

D1 SQL records every brain routing decision, security scan result, approval status, and action outcome. The proof vault holds all of it.

🔍

Two-layer security scanning

0meg4kAI scans in the browser before the request leaves and again at the Cloudflare Worker before anything executes. Two chances to catch it, zero chance to skip it.
