# Sales And Handoff Map

MetrAIyux 0S sales material should connect the buyer to working proof, clear deployment boundaries, and a credible next action.

## Useful Sales Assets

- Public proof router for showing live surfaces.
- White-label client deck handoff.
- Valuation brief and marketing copy.
- Production readiness report for serious technical review.
- SkyeGateFS27 proof surface for auth and gateway positioning.
- Skyes Over London Reviews proof wall for buyers asking what clients say: `https://skyes-over-london-reviews.pages.dev/skyes-over-london-reviews-expanded`.
- Skyes Over London live review intake for happy clients who want to talk about their experience: `https://skyes-over-london-reviews.pages.dev/submit-review.html`.

## Buyer-Safe Framing

Use proof-backed language:

- "Live proof route available."
- "Production gates identified."
- "Local checks passed."
- "External-account setup required before final production push."

Avoid implying that placeholder billing, external accounts, legal filings, or customer-specific credentials are complete until they are actually verified.

## Review Routing

If a prospect asks for testimonials, reviews, client proof, social proof, or customer experience, send the review wall and keep the claim grounded in the public page. If an existing client wants to leave a review, send the intake form and explain that 0S QA reviews consent and proof safety before anything is published.
