## Company Identity

The archive presents Skyes Over London LC as an operator-built systems company inside the SOLEnterprises / SOLEnterprises International Nexus & Holdings ecosystem. The site uses several labels: Skyes Over London LC, SkyeSol, SOL, SOLEnterprises, Monaccor-backed operating company, and sometimes SkyeHands or SkyeHands ecosystem in valuation contexts.

Skyes Over London LC builds authority-grade web systems, AI infrastructure, operating lanes, portals, proof surfaces, content engines, and governed business workflows for operators who need control, accountability, and scale.

The archive repeatedly rejects the idea that the company is only a web agency. The strongest positioning is not "we build websites." It is "we build operating surfaces that route work, prove work, govern AI, collect signals, package offers, and turn scattered business activity into lanes."

This language matters because it is the brand doctrine. The company should not be explained publicly as a generic agency, studio, or marketing vendor. The archive wants it described as a company that builds business control systems.

## Core Company Thesis

The central thesis is that businesses do not need more isolated tools. They need connected operating lanes.

The archive defines a lane as a branded system that handles a real workflow from intake to closeout. A lane should have:

- Clear entry.
- Structured data capture.
- Routing and ownership.
- Status visibility.
- Exceptions and escalation.
- Exports and receipts.
- Access control.
- Proof or audit artifacts.
- Optional AI and governance upgrades.

This thesis is visible across CineFrame, DataPilot, AccessAtlas, CheckoutForge, SignalFlow, TrustLayer, ConnectBridge, ContentEngine, Lane Vault, SkyePack, kAIxU powered platforms, NorthStar, Valley Verified, and SkyeSuite. The buyer is not imagined as someone buying pretty pages. The buyer is imagined as someone losing money or credibility because their intake, routing, follow-up, documents, payments, AI usage, or proof chain is scattered.

## Gateway Security Doctrine

Important security doctrine from `docs/Gateway-Bind.md` and `SolenteAI/Security-Data-Handling/index.html`:

- Apps must not call provider endpoints directly from browser or app server code.
- Apps must not store provider API keys in browser storage, app config, or client env vars.
- AI calls must route through Gateway13 Netlify Functions lanes.
- Gateway auth uses bearer kAIxU keys.
- Telemetry headers identify app, build, and install/device where applicable.
- kAIxU is framed as infrastructure: UI console public, execution plane behind the gate, policy enforced centrally, usage metered, and exports available.
- The live Gate Delta contract described in the archive includes health, models, generate, and stream endpoints.
- The procurement argument is: users and apps hold kAIxU tokens, not vendor keys.

This is one of the most important company doctrines. It makes the AI platform story credible: governance is not a feature afterthought; it is the boundary.
