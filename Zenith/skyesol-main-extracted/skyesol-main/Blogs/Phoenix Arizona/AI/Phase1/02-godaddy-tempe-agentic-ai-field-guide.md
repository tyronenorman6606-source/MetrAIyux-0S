## The Shift: “AI features” are dead — long live AI that does work

The first wave of business AI was about generating words and images. The next wave is about generating actions — and that’s a bigger economic event.

GoDaddy’s Airo direction points straight at agentic AI: tools that infer goals, plan steps, and execute across products. When the platform layer goes agentic, every local business experiences it as “software got faster.”

> Agentic AI isn’t a chatbot. It’s a workflow with a steering wheel. — Phoenix AI Field Guide

## What Airo is signaling

Airo.ai is framed as a chat-driven system that completes tasks across GoDaddy’s ecosystem — from domain and site creation to policies and app-building.

The important part (for Phoenix businesses) is not the specific features — it’s the posture: one interface, many agents, real outcomes. That is exactly how enterprise AI is being sold and deployed now, just translated into SMB language.

#### Outcome-first UX

Less configuration. More “tell the system what you want” — then review and publish.

#### Suite-level agents

Multiple agents coordinate behind the scenes: site, domain, logo, compliance, and more.

#### Distribution wins

Tempe-based infrastructure puts AI into small businesses at a scale most startups can’t touch.

#### Compliance-as-default

Policies become “generated defaults,” shifting expectations for every SMB site and app.
