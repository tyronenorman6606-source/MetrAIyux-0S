## The Domain: AI where mistakes cost the most

Public safety is not a playground for automation. It is a domain of accountability: evidence, narratives, and documentation that can shape outcomes.

That’s why Axon’s AI story matters in Phoenix. It’s AI embedded inside a workflow with safeguards and review steps — because the operator must remain responsible.

## Draft One: Generative AI as a force multiplier

Axon’s Draft One positioning is explicit: it uses body-worn camera audio to produce draft report narratives quickly, with safeguards intended to preserve accountability.

#### Draft, not final

The value is time: faster first drafts. The risk is accountability — which is why review and controls matter.

#### Side-by-side verification

Review alongside original footage and transcript keeps the source visible while drafting.

#### Safeguards posture

High-stakes AI must be bounded: permissions, logging, and responsible review.

#### Ecosystem integration

AI drafts only matter if they fit into evidence retention, access control, and traceability.
