## The Market: HR is an agentic AI goldmine

Recruiting isn’t one task. It’s a chain: attract → apply → screen → schedule → interview → offer → onboard. Most of that chain is coordination.

Conversational AI works here because the workflow is legible: candidates ask questions, confirm details, select times, and move forward. The system reduces friction while preserving correctness.

> In HR tech, “AI” often means “stop wasting human time on admin.” — Phoenix AI Field Guide

## The Product: Olivia as a conversational operations layer

Paradox describes Olivia as an AI hiring assistant that answers candidate questions, schedules interviews, and streamlines workflows — especially in mobile-first recruiting.

#### Always-on candidate Q&A

24/7 responses reduce drop-off and remove scheduling bottlenecks.

#### Automation of routine steps

Screening and scheduling fit AI well because rules are definable and outcomes are verifiable.

#### Human touch preserved

The goal is not replacing humans — it’s freeing them to focus on interviews and decisions.

#### ATS integration

AI is durable when attached to the ATS and data model — not floating as a separate tool.

## Why Phoenix: labor markets at scale

The Phoenix metro is a high-growth labor market. High-volume hiring is constant — which makes HR automation economically relevant.
