## The Problem: Unstructured Intake Creates Hidden Backlogs

Most teams think they have a “customer support problem.” They actually have an intake problem . Requests arrive in DMs, texts, calls, emails — and nothing is structured. Issues get lost, follow-ups are inconsistent, and customers churn.

When the request isn’t captured as a record, the business can’t measure performance. That means you can’t improve it.

> Operators don’t “get motivated.” They reduce friction until output becomes inevitable. — SKAIXU.SYSTEM principle

## The System: Structured Intake → Routing → Proof of Resolution

SkyeSol installs support workflows that behave like an operations system: every request becomes a ticket, tickets route by category and priority, and resolution creates a proof record (notes, artifacts, timestamps).
