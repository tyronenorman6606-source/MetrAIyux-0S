## The Problem: Handoffs Create Disputes

In real estate and construction, “the work” is often not the hardest part. The hardest part is proving the work: what was inspected, what was approved, what was completed, and what changed.

Phoenix projects fail when the system can’t answer basic questions: who owned the task, when it was done, and where the proof lives. Without that, vendors argue, timelines slip, and billing becomes fragile.
