Phoenix Med Spa: Consultation Funnel That Filters Low-Intent Inquiries

### Client Snapshot

Med spa offering injectables and aesthetic treatments.

🧩

### The Challenge

High volume of vague inquiries with low booking intent.

🧠

### Core Outcome

Education + qualification + booking flow that lifts consult quality while keeping the experience premium and frictionless.

Execution

Solution & Build Details

A structured delivery—built for clarity, speed, and operational control.

### What We Did
- Built consult flow that educates and qualifies without feeling aggressive.
- Implemented clean booking policy messaging (deposit, expectations).
- Segmented intake to capture goals and timing upfront.

### What We Built
- Service education pages with expectation setting

- Deposit/booking policy section designed for trust
