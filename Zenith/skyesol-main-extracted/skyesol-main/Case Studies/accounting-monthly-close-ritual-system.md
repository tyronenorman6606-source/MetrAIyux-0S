Monthly Close Ritual: A Simple System That Prevents Year-End Panic

### Client Snapshot

Owner-managed business with inconsistent bookkeeping habits.

🧩

### The Challenge

Year-end became a fire drill because monthly close wasn’t a habit.

🧠

### Core Outcome

A repeatable monthly close checklist and owner action list that stabilizes financials and eliminates year-end fire drills.

Execution

Solution & Build Details

A structured delivery—built for clarity, speed, and operational control.

### What We Did
- Implemented a monthly close checklist with deadlines.
- Created owner action list (what to send, by when).
- Standardized executive summary output.

### What We Built
- Monthly close checklist (bank recs, AR/AP review, payroll match, category review)
- Owner action list
- Executive summary output format
