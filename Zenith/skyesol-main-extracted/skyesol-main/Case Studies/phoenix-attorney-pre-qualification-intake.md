### The Challenge

🧠

### Core Outcome

Case-type routing + fit screening to reduce staff time and prioritize qualified consults.

Execution

Solution & Build Details

A structured delivery—built for clarity, speed, and operational control.

### What We Did
- Created pre-qualification intake with case-type routing.
- Added fit-screening questions tied to jurisdiction and status.
- Published preparation checklist to improve consult efficiency.

- Case lanes (civil, family, business, etc.)
- Fit screening (timeline, jurisdiction, status)
- What-to-prepare checklist page
