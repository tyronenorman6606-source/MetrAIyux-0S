### The Challenge

Too many one-time cleans; not enough recurring revenue stability.

🧠

### Core Outcome

Recurring tier structure + add-ons that stabilizes revenue and makes scheduling consistent.

Execution

Solution & Build Details

A structured delivery—built for clarity, speed, and operational control.

### What We Did
- Designed membership-style positioning with recurring tiers.
- Built intake capturing home details and priorities.
- Created add-on menu that supports upsells cleanly.

### What We Built
- Tiered recurring plans (weekly/biweekly/monthly)
- Intake capturing home size, pets, priorities, preferred days
- Add-on layout (deep clean, fridge, windows)
