### Client Snapshot

Broker onboarding new carriers weekly.

🧩

### The Challenge

Onboarding was slow because carriers submitted incomplete packets.

🧠

### Core Outcome

A carrier packet checklist + verification steps that reduces compliance risk and accelerates first-load readiness.

Execution

Solution & Build Details

A structured delivery—built for clarity, speed, and operational control.

⚙️

### What We Did
- Created onboarding intake with carrier packet checklist.
- Added upload instructions and error-proofing language.
- Built internal verification checklist.

🧱

### What We Built
- Carrier requirements page (COI, W-9, authority, banking, safety info)
- Submit guidance that prevents missing items
- Internal verification checklist
