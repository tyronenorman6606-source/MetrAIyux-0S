Jitsu Delivery: Route Intelligence, Closeouts, and Exception Control

🏷️

### Client Snapshot

Jitsu Delivery — logistics program requiring consistent route operations and proof discipline.

🧩

### The Challenge

Route operations degrade when exceptions aren’t categorized, closeouts aren’t enforced, and record quality varies by driver or lane.

🧠

### Core Outcome

A last‑mile operations program combining route intelligence, structured exceptions, and closeout discipline to improve consistency across lanes.

Execution

Integrated Delivery

Multi-lane execution—operations, systems, trust surfaces, and automation combined into one program.

⚙️

### What We Did
- Implemented route record standard and driver readiness expectations.
- Created structured exception workflow with escalation ladder and update cadence.
- Enforced closeout discipline (proof artifacts + completeness checks).
- Installed weekly review rhythm to improve lane performance iteratively.

🧱

### What We Built
- Route record template + readiness checklist
- Exception taxonomy + escalation cadence
- Closeout checklist + proof artifact standards
- Weekly ops review and improvement loop
