### Client Snapshot

Confidential operation where incidents must be handled quickly and consistently across teams.

🧩

### The Challenge

When incidents are handled ad hoc, time is lost, narratives split, and proof disappears.

🧠

### Core Outcome

A program that turns incidents into structured events with escalation ladders and proof artifacts—reducing chaos and protecting reputation.

Execution

Integrated Delivery

Multi-lane execution—operations, systems, trust surfaces, and automation working together as one program.

### What We Did
- Defined incident categories and required data fields.
- Created escalation ladder and update cadence.
- Standardized proof artifacts and closeout notes for learning.
- Installed weekly review ritual to reduce repeat incidents.

🧱

### What We Built
- Incident categories + required fields
- Escalation ladder + communication templates
- Proof artifact rules + closeout notes template
- Weekly incident review cadence
