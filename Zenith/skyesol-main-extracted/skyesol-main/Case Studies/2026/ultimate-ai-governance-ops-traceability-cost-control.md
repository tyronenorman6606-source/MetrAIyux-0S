AI Governance + Ops: Traceability, Diagnostics, and Cost Control

A governance-first AI program: standardized request contracts, diagnostics menus, and cost attribution—built to feel enterprise.

🏷️

### Client Snapshot

Confidential organization deploying AI across multiple apps and teams.

🧩

### The Challenge

AI deployments fail when costs are untracked, errors are untraceable, and teams treat models like magic.

🧠

### Core Outcome

A governance-first AI program: standardized request contracts, diagnostics menus, and cost attribution—built to feel enterprise.

Execution

Integrated Delivery

Multi-lane execution—operations, systems, trust surfaces, and automation working together as one program.

⚙️

### What We Did
- Defined standardized request contract and error normalization rules.
- Added app/build traceability conventions and diagnostic menu pattern.
- Implemented cost attribution model per app/team/client.
- Established policy guardrails (routing rules + soft limits).

🧱

### What We Built
- Normalized request/response schema + provider adapters
- Diagnostics menu design (version, request IDs, health checks)
- Usage logging and attribution fields
- Policy gating rules for model routing
