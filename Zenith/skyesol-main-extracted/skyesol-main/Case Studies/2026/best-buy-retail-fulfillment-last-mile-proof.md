Best Buy: Retail Fulfillment Enablement With Last‑Mile Proof Discipline

Integrated last‑mile enablement: intake discipline, exception handling, proof artifacts, and performance visibility—built for high-volume retail fulfillment.

🏷️

### Client Snapshot

Best Buy — retail fulfillment program requiring reliable last‑mile execution and clean closeouts.

🧩

### The Challenge

Retail fulfillment breaks when exceptions aren’t structured, proof artifacts are inconsistent, and handoffs between systems/partners create ambiguity.

🧠

### Core Outcome

Integrated last‑mile enablement: intake discipline, exception handling, proof artifacts, and performance visibility—built for high-volume retail fulfillment.

Execution

Integrated Delivery

Multi-lane execution—operations, systems, trust surfaces, and automation combined into one program.

⚙️

### What We Did
- Standardized intake fields and handoff rules so every delivery record starts complete.
- Installed exception categories with escalation cadence and customer update rules.
- Enforced proof artifact standards and closeout checklist for billing readiness.
- Created leadership visibility rhythm for performance and issue trends.

🧱

### What We Built
- Delivery record standard (addresses, windows, constraints, contacts)
- Exception playbook (no access, reschedule, damage, late windows) + escalation ladder
- Proof artifact discipline (photo/POD equivalents) + closeout checklist
- Weekly performance review template and operating rhythm

Implementation

Stack & Timeline

Deployment-first, Drop-ready, designed to scale.

🧬

### Stack

Ops playbook + documentation standards + structured intake (Drop-ready web intake optional)

🗓️

### Timeline

2–6 weeks depending on volume and partner handoffs

### Operational Outcomes
- Cleaner closeouts and fewer missing-proof delays
- Faster resolution of last‑mile exceptions
- Improved operational consistency across lanes
