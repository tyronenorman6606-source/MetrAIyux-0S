### Client Snapshot

🧩

### The Challenge

Generic contact forms produce garbage leads. Enterprise inbound needs routing and completeness gates.

🧠

### Core Outcome

A lead capture and routing system that behaves like platform infrastructure: required fields, lane routing, and follow-up discipline.

Execution

- Built follow-up templates and internal handoff rules.
- Created reporting view and review cadence.
