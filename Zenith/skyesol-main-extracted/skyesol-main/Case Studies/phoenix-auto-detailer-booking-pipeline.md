Phoenix Auto Detailer: Turning Walk-Ins Into a Predictable Booking Pipeline

A booking-first intake system that captures vehicle details, scope, location, and timing—so the calendar fills with qualified jobs instead of DM chaos.

🏷️

### Client Snapshot

Mobile + shop-based auto detailing service in Phoenix.

🧩

### The Challenge

Leads arrived through DMs and texts with incomplete details, causing scheduling gaps and wasted time qualifying.

🧠

### Core Outcome

A booking-first intake system that captures vehicle details, scope, location, and timing—so the calendar fills with qualified jobs instead of DM chaos.

Execution

Solution & Build Details

A structured delivery—built for clarity, speed, and operational control.

⚙️

### What We Did
- Built a booking-first funnel with service packages, add-ons, and service-area rules.
- Created an intake that routes complete requests into a follow-up queue.
- Added instant confirmation messaging to reduce no-shows and back-and-forth.

🧱

### What We Built
- Service menu with package anchors (interior, exterior, full detail, ceramic prep)
- Booking request form capturing vehicle type, condition, location, preferred times
- Clear next-step confirmation and expectations messaging

Implementation

Stack & Timeline

Deployment-first, Drop-ready, and structured for iteration.

🧬

### Stack

Netlify static + Netlify Forms + structured lead fields

🗓️

### Timeline

3–7 days initial deploy; tuned weekly based on inbound patterns

Results

Operational Outcomes

📈

### Operational Outcomes
- Higher quality inbound requests
- Faster scheduling decisions
- Reduced back-and-forth messaging

🎯
