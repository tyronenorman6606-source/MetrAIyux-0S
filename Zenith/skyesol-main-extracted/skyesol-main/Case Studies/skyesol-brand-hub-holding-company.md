Overview

SkyeSol: A Brand Hub That Feels Like a Holding Company

A multi-division hub experience built to communicate ecosystem-scale credibility—not a single-service identity.

🏷️

### Client Snapshot

A multi-venture umbrella brand needing clarity and power.

🧩

### The Challenge

Holding companies fail visually when they look like a single-service business. The site had to communicate ecosystem, not one offer.

🧠

### Core Outcome

A multi-division hub experience built to communicate ecosystem-scale credibility—not a single-service identity.

Execution

Solution & Build Details

A structured delivery—built for clarity, speed, and operational control.

⚙️

### What We Did
- Designed hub-style experience with strong hero presence and structured navigation.
- Created clear venture lanes and buyer-specific CTAs.
- Maintained SVS discipline to reinforce enterprise authority.

🧱

### What We Built
- Multi-division navigation architecture
- Visual identity consistency (SVS discipline)
- What we do structured as a portfolio (not a menu)
- Strong CTAs for B2B, partners, and contractors

Implementation

Stack & Timeline

Deployment-first, Drop-ready, and structured for iteration.

🧬

### Stack

Netlify static deployment; brand-forward structure designed for expansion

🗓️

### Timeline

Deployed fast; built to evolve

Results

Operational Outcomes

Outcomes are written as operational impacts (not hype). Tune metrics as you collect real data.

📈

### Operational Outcomes
- Clearer market positioning
- Improved authority perception
- Easier to add ventures without redesign

🎯

### Next Step
