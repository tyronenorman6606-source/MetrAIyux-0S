Questions Before You Paint

Quick answers for As You Wish Pottery guests before they reserve, arrive, paint, and pick up.

Make a reservation Open arrival workspace
What does the studio fee include?

The official site says the studio fee includes paints, supplies, glazing, and firing. Pottery is selected separately.

How much does a visit cost?

The official site lists a studio fee of $10 per painter plus the pottery piece. It also notes many pieces are in the $18-35 range and kids under 2 have free studio fees. Confirm current pricing on the official site before arrival.

Can groups use this app?

Yes. The workspace lane is designed for birthday parties, field trips, classes, and team-build groups that need cleaner arrival context.

Does this replace the main site?

No. The main As You Wish site remains the source for live reservations, schedules, coupons, policies, and checkout.
