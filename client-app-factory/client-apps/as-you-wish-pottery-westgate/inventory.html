Four steps

The app explains the visit instead of dumping store categories.

1. Choose a piece

Pick from mugs, bowls, figures, keepsakes, seasonal projects, and other ceramic options.

2. Paint at the table

Use studio paints, brushes, supplies, and idea prompts while staff can assist.

3. Leave it for finishing

The studio handles glazing and kiln firing after the painter is done.

4. Pick it up

Guests return in a few days for the finished piece.
