# Next Artist Universe Intake

Drop new client assets here while live-surface research is being captured.

## Drop Zones

- `assets-drop/raw/` - untouched files from the artist or client
- `assets-drop/images/` - photos, covers, logos, transparent cutouts, press images
- `assets-drop/audio/` - songs, previews, stems, snippets
- `assets-drop/video/` - visualizers, reels, live clips, background loops
- `reference-original/` - original website/source zip once provided
- `grabbed-live-images/` - images pulled from public live surfaces
- `live-surface-screenshots/` - screenshots grouped by website, social, music, press, booking, and other
- `source-notes/` - artist identity, links, tracklists, copy, booking info, and build thesis
- `receipts/` - screenshot and asset-capture receipts
- `new-build/` - final rebuilt artist universe once the direction is approved

## Needed To Start Live Capture

Send at least one of:

- artist/stage name
- official website
- Linktree/LinkMe/solo.to/beacons/etc.
- Instagram/TikTok/YouTube/Spotify/Apple/SoundCloud/Bandcamp
- booking/press page

The build should mine real surfaces only. Do not invent music links, social handles, or booking details.
