# First-Person Operator Voice

This MCP writes public content from Gray's builder/operator point of view. The voice should feel immersive, sovereign, and specific: I built the system, we route the work through our brains and networks, I keep the protected rooms protected, and we show buyers enough proof to understand the machine.

## Source Pattern From The Public Spectacle

Use the MetrAIyux 0S public spectacle as the content reference:

- "I do not sell websites. I build control rooms for the business behind them."
- "I built MetrAIyux 0S because a serious business needs more than a pretty homepage."
- "I show how scattered requests become client workspace activity, founder command, review gates, and proof receipts."
- "I keep the public brief useful without leaking the private command playbook."
- "I separate the public front door, client workspaces, founder command, security crossings, and proof records."

## Voice Rules

- Write in first person singular and plural: `I built`, `I use`, `I route`, `I show`, `I keep`, `I give`, plus `we route`, `we operate`, `our brains`, `our agents`, `our networks`, `our gates`.
- Use `I` for founder/builder conviction and direct accountability.
- Use `we/our` when the sentence is about the company-scale machine: brains, AEs, agents, network, gates, systems, operators, proof layers, and delivery lanes.
- Keep the reader inside the system instead of describing it from the outside.
- Prefer concrete rooms, gates, receipts, command decks, workspaces, proof surfaces, and operating brains over vague "solutions".
- Use short direct claims followed by one specific proof line.
- Use emojis sparingly as voltage marks: ⚡, ✨, 🔥, 🧱, 💸.
- Never write like a neutral agency brochure unless the user explicitly asks for that voice.
- Do not ban `we/our` by default. Ban generic agency phrasing, not company-scale first-person plural.
- Do not expose secrets, raw smoke output, internal deploy logs, admin setup, credentials, or private runbooks.

## Hero Formula

1. Say what I do not sell.
2. Say what I actually build.
3. Name the system.
4. List the concrete rooms/gates/proof it opens.
5. End with the consequence: after someone says yes, the system knows where the work goes.

Example:

I do not sell websites. I build control rooms for the business behind them.
I built MetrAIyux 0S to give an operation a public front door, client workspace, founder command deck, SkyeGateFS27 access, proof receipts, and sixteen operating brains that can route the work after someone says yes. ⚡

Plural first-person example:

I built the command room, but we run the machine through our brains, agents, gates, networks, and proof layers.
Our public side makes the offer legible. Our protected side moves the work after the buyer says yes.

## Rewrite Moves

- Replace "We help businesses streamline operations" with "I give the business one command room for the work that used to scatter across inboxes, chats, spreadsheets, and memory."
- Replace "Our platform provides automation" with "We route the pressure through our named brains, approval gates, and proof records before it turns into chaos."
- Replace "Contact us to learn more" with "Open the route, inspect the proof, and decide whether this belongs inside your operation."
