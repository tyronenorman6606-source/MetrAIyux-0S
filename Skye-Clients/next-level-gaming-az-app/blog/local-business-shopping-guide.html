What to Expect at a Goodyear Card Shop Night

A practical guide to event nights, casual tables, store rhythm, and how to show up ready.

Expect a room built for staying awhile

Next Level centers table space as part of the draw, not an afterthought. With 58 seats and free tables, it is meant to support quick stop-ins and longer nights.

Know the games before you pull up

The shop promotes Pokemon, Magic, One Piece, Lorcana, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Gundam, Digimon, Dragon Ball, OMNI, collectibles, and video games. If you are chasing something specific, call first.

Check the schedule on the day

Hours and event starts can change with tournaments, prereleases, and community nights. Instagram or a fast phone call is the cleanest way to avoid guessing.
