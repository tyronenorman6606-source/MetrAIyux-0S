How to Shop Singles, Sealed, and Supplies Without Guessing

Use the Next Level app categories to move quickly between fresh releases, premium singles, daily staples, and support gear.

Start with what rotates fastest

The Featured TCG Releases and Tournaments lanes are where fresh inventory and event energy show up first. If you want the fastest read on what matters this week, start there.

Use premium and core differently

Premium Singles & Sealed is your chase lane. Daily Singles, Packs & Boxes is the staple lane. Splitting them keeps collectors and everyday players from hunting through the same pile.

Sleeves, binders, deck boxes, playmats, and trade tools are the stuff people realize they need right before round one. Good shop apps make those easy to spot.
