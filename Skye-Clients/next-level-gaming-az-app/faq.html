Quick answers before you head to Next Level Gaming.

Questions

Common shop questions

What games does Next Level Gaming carry?

The shop promotes Pokemon, Magic: The Gathering, One Piece, Lorcana, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Gundam, Digimon, Dragon Ball, OMNI, collectibles, and video games.

Do you host tournaments or events?

Yes. Next Level positions itself around tournament nights, prereleases, learn-to-play sessions, and casual community play. Check Instagram or call for this week's schedule.

Do you have table space?

Yes. The shop advertises 58 seats and free tables for casual play, so it is built for hanging out as much as quick pickup.

What are the hours?

Hours can shift with events, so the safest move is to call the shop or check Instagram before you come in.

Where is the shop located?

Next Level Gaming is at 756 N. Dysart Rd, Goodyear, AZ 85338.

Can I browse online?

Yes. The shop has a live TCGplayer storefront alongside the local shop app.

Do you buy, sell, or trade?

For current singles-buy, trade, or trade-in policies, call the shop directly. Policies can shift based on inventory, events, and demand.

Are prices and stock always current here?

No. This app is the fast overview, but sealed stock, singles, specials, and tournament capacity can move quickly. Call or stop by for the latest details.
