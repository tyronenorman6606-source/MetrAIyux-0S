What the workspace handles

The parts of production that clients usually chase manually.

Project intake

Capture campaign goals, deliverables, locations, interview needs, approvals, and due dates before the first shoot day drifts.

Cut review

Keep first-pass notes, revision requests, timing comments, and approval checkpoints in one review lane instead of scattered threads.

Delivery coordination

Track hero edit, social cutdowns, alternate exports, subtitles, and final delivery status so nobody is guessing what shipped.

Proof & accountability

Generate proof receipts showing what changed, what was approved, and what still needs a human yes before anything public moves.
